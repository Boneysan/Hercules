## The Premise

The party are new members of the **Adventurers' Guild**, sworn in at the Prontera fountain.
On paper they hunt monsters and chase coin. In truth they are walking into the slow
unraveling of the world.

The ancient gods did not *kill* the great demons of the God Demon War — they **sealed**
them (see [[Lore/Creation_Myth]], [[Lore/Timeline]]). Those seals were always meant to be
permanent. They are not. One by one they are weakening, and it is not happening by chance.

A hidden hand — the **Whisperers**, a cult web reaching from the Prontera sewers to the
Geffen academies to the Arunafeltz clergy — is deliberately loosening the seals in
sequence. Each town the party visits is suffering a *symptom* of the cascade: an awakened
horror, a corrupted institution, a leaking prison. Each arc's MVP is one of those symptoms.
The cascade builds toward the shattering of [[Characters/Demons/Satan_Morroc]]'s seal and,
ultimately, the release of [[Characters/Demons/Surt]] — the fire that ends the world. The
intelligence behind it all answers to [[Characters/Gods/Loki]], the agent of Ragnarok.

### The Central Question
[[Characters/Mortals/Thanatos]] beat a demon and *became* one. [[Characters/Mortals/Himmelmez]]
chased power and *stopped being mortal*. The campaign keeps asking the party: **you fight
monsters to grow strong — what are you becoming?**

### Player Agency in an Inevitable Cascade
The cascade is inevitable—Morroc and Surt *will* break free in Act IV. However, the party's victories in Acts I–III dictate the terms of the finale. Every local disaster they mitigate saves key NPCs who will return as a united allied front. Furthermore, dismantling cult strongholds allows the party to secure powerful relics (like fragments of [[Concepts/Ymir_Heart]]) that they will desperately need to survive the end of the world.

   

## The Meta Arc (Four Acts)

  **Act I — A World That Looks at Peace** (Arcs 1–5, levels 1–80): local horrors that seem
  unrelated. The party are nobodies becoming somebodies. Renewal early game zones — Prontera
  fields through Alberta's seas.
  **Act II — The Cracks** (Arcs 6–10, levels 68–165): the symptoms connect. Corporate rot,
  corrupted faith, a fallen kingdom. The party realize the seals are the pattern. Renewal
  mid game — Schwarzwald, Einbroch, Glast Heim, Rachel, Bio Labs.
  **Act III — The Cascade** (Arcs 11–14, levels 105–175): the great seals fail in earnest.
  Renewal high game — Abyss Lake, the New World (mid campaign reveal), Nameless Island,
  Thor's Volcano. The world tears open before the party can stop it.
  **Act IV — Ragnarok** (Arcs 15–19, levels 175–250+): the hero's revelation, a crumbling kingdom, ancient science, the witch of death, Morroc breaks, and the party stand where Thanatos once stood — with a choice he
  never got. Renewal endgame — Thanatos Tower, Prontera Prison, Varmundt Mansion, Niflheim, Morroc Ruins, Surt.

## The Arcs in Detail

### Arc 1 — Omens at the Fountain · Prontera · Lvl 1–25
The "village episode." The party take their first guild contracts in the
[[Locations/Cities/Prontera]] fields and clear the Culvert and the Cathedral basement.
Eden Group Quests give structure to the early kills; Bounty Boards near the field NPCs
teach the renewal loop of hunting and turning in. But refugees are trickling in from the
south, the ground trembles at night, and someone is painting goat headed sigils in the
sewers. The trail leads to a **Whisperer** cult cell nursing a bound imp — **Deviruchi**,
the arc's first boss. Killing it ends the symptom but not the disease; a hooded figure
escapes. *Plants the [[Characters/Demons/Baphomet]] cult and the "seals are weakening" thread.*

> **Renewal spots:** Prontera Field 07 (Rocker x170, Savage Bebe x90 — 1–35), Lasagna Field 01
> (Eggring — 1–15, fantastic spawn rate), Culvert 1–3 (12–47). Eden Group Quests Levels 1–11
> and 11–25 align with arc kills. Geffen Field 04 (Lunatic, Mandragora — 1–43) works for
> ranged classes who push ahead.

   

### Arc 2 — The Sleeping Forest · Payon · Lvl 12–55
[[Locations/Cities/Payon]]'s ancestral dead are not resting. Ancestor veneration meets
necromancy as the party descend Payon Cave. The corruptor is a flower spirit gone wrong —
**Moonlight Flower** — being *fed* by a Whisperer necromancer. *Plants the necromancy
thread that pays off with [[Characters/Mortals/Himmelmez]] in Arc 18.*

> **Renewal spots:** Payon Forest 08 (Spore x100, Boa x60 — 3–37; Strawberries sell well).
> Payon Cave 1 (Skeleton, Familiar, Poporing — 12–47). Payon Cave 4 (Sohee — 49–84) is the
> deepest floor and Moonlight Flower's lair. Eden Group Quests 11–25 and 26–40 overlap here.

   

### Arc 3 — Sand and Whispers · Morroc · Lvl 20–70
The desert of [[Locations/Cities/Morroc]]: Sphinx, Pyramids, Ant Hell. The old stories of
a "sleeping demon" beneath the city resurface as quakes worsen. The arc runs Sograt Desert
maps through mid range (Hode, Sandman, Frilldora — 48–77 on maps 16/17), Ant Hell 01 for
passive grinding (Ant Egg — 13–48, Familiars aggro), and Sphinx 2 for the dungeon proper
(Requiem, Zerom — 56–90). The party crown the arc by toppling **Amon Ra** in the Pyramid
(Phreeoni, Maya, and Orc Hero as side MVPs). They leave with a dread certainty: *something
enormous sleeps under this city.* *Heavy foreshadowing for Arc 19.*

> **Renewal spots:** Sograt Desert 01 (Peco Peco, Muka — 10–43), Ant Hell 01 (Ant Egg —
> 13–48; careful of aggro Familiars), Sograt Desert 16/17 (Hode, Sandman — 48–77). Sphinx 2
> (56–90) ties into Eden Group Quests 71–85. Items from Sograt turn in for EXP at Eden 56–70.

   

### Arc 4 — The City Above the Beast · Geffen · Lvl 30–80
The first **great seal** the party confront directly. [[Locations/Cities/Geffen]] is built
atop [[Characters/Demons/Baphomet]]'s prison, and the Whisperers have surfaced inside the
Mages' Guild itself. The arc runs Orc Village / OD1 (aggressive, AoE friendly, fire
element — 30–71), Mt. Mjolnir 06 (Poison Spore, 14–46) and Mjolnir 11 (Argiope, 60–95)
as mid range, and Geffen Field 06 (Petite Ground, 71–106) as the dungeon exterior. Climax:
**Baphomet** and Doppelganger in the lowest level. Victory reinforces the seal — and reveals
the cult's true goal: *they don't want Baphomet free, they want every seal weakened at once.*

> **Renewal spots:** Orc Village / Geffen Field 10 (Orc Warrior x120, Orc Lady x80 — 30–65;
> great AoE, fire element). OD1 (Orc Zombie, Orc Skeleton — 38–71; almost all aggro, holy
> or fire). Mt. Mjolnir 11 (Argiope — 60–95; all aggro). Geffen Field 06 (Petite Ground —
> 71–106; fire element, good EXP). Eden Group Quests 41–55 apply to Orc kills.

   

### Arc 5 — Tides and Trade · Alberta / Izlude · Lvl 33–80
A breather with teeth arc at sea. Smugglers, a sea cult, and Morroc refugees arriving by
ship through [[Locations/Cities/Alberta]]. Byalan Undersea 1–5 (non VIP accessible, wind
element recommended — 31–72), the Sunken Ship (Hydra, Pirate Skeleton — 33–68, great AoE
map), and Turtle Island, climaxing against **Tao Gunka** (Drake and Kraken as side MVPs).
*Establishes the refugee crisis and the cult's reach across trade routes.*

> **Renewal spots:** Byalan 1–5 (31–72; not memo able dungeon levels, but multi floor single target
> and AoE options). Sunken Ship 1 (Hydra x71, Pirate Skeleton x43 — 33–68; very mobby).
> Multiple Eden Group kill rewards in Byalan. Turtle Island MVP availability — verify on your
> server scripts. #needs-verification

   

### Arc 6 — The Floating Republic · Yuno · Lvl 68–115
Crossing into the [[Nations/Schwarzwald_Republic]]. [[Locations/Cities/Yuno]]'s scholars
are the first to *measure* the dimensional instability — and to suspect a fragment of
[[Concepts/Ymir_Heart]] keeps the city aloft. The arc runs Juno Field 04 (Harpy, Goat —
68–100), the surrounding Juno maps (Grand Peco, Sleeper, Goat — 71–103), and Juno Norgroad
Floor 2 (Nightmare Terror, Deleter Earth/Sky — 92–121) as the dungeon. Juperos 01 (Venatu —
98–133) is the dungeon climax: a pre industrial ruin that mirrors Schwarzwald's obsession
with machines and power. Climax: **Mistress** (+ Eddga). *Introduces the Ash Vacuum science
thread that runs to Arc 12 and the finale.*

> **Renewal spots:** Juno Field 04 / El Mes Plateau (Harpy, Goat — 68–100; also Eden EXP
> reward 71–85). Maps around Juno (Grand Peco, Sleeper — 71–103; Eden EXP reward 71–85).
> Norgroad F2 (Nightmare Terror, Deleter — 92–121; extremely mobby, great AoE; fire armor +
> Aspersio recommended). Juperos 01 (Venatu — 98–133; extremely mobby; Penomena or Ogretooth
> Card for reduction). **Gramps Low TI (70–100) opens here** — introduce the Eden Gramps NPC
> in this arc; turn in windows double as downtime between dungeon runs.

   

### Arc 7 — Iron and Ash · Einbroch · Lvl 80–120
[[Locations/Cities/Einbroch]]: labor unrest, Rekenber's supply chain, and a decommissioned
war machine that won't stay dead — **RSX 0806** — reactivated by someone who shouldn't
have access. The arc uses Einbroch Field (Metaling, Geographer — verify renewal levels) and
the Rachel adjacent plains: Rachel Field 12: The Plain of Ida (Roween — 81–115, all aggro,
earth element) and Rachel Field 01: Audhumbla Grassland (Desert Wolf — 88–123, fire armor
recommended, water element attacks). *First direct brush with [[Episodes/Arc_Rekenber]] and
the corporate antagonist that pays off in Arc 10.*

> **Renewal spots:** Rachel Field 12 (Roween — 81–115; Orc Warrior Card recommended, earth
> element). Rachel Field 01 (Desert Wolf — 88–123; fire armor + water element). Einbroch
> Mine Floor 3 (180–205) is endgame content in renewal — the mine's deep levels are
> referenced here but not entered until Arc 19. Arc 7 treats Einbroch as the *introduction*
> to Rekenber's industrial reach; the horror beneath is Arc 10's payoff.

   

### Arc 8 — The Cursed Kingdom · Glast Heim · Lvl 75–120
The iconic ruin. A fallen holy kingdom consumed by corrupted faith — a thematic rehearsal
for what's wrong with Arunafeltz in Arc 9. In renewal, Glast Heim proper sits solidly in
Act II: GH Underprison 2 (Skel Prisoner, Injustice, Rybio — 80–111, all aggro), GH
Churchyard (Dark Priest — 83–118, shadow armor recommended, holy/fire element), and Geffen
Field 06 (Petite Ground — 71–106) for the exterior approach. The party fight through
Churchyard, Chivalry, and the Abbey to the **Dark Lord** (with Amdarais beneath). Here, they find the first terrifying clue: the Whisperers' magic is not demonic in origin, but divine. Someone from the realm of the gods is teaching mortals how to unbind the seals.
*The "institutions rot from within" theme of Act II peaks here.*

> **Renewal spots:** GH Underprison 2 (80–111; mobby, good AoE; Eden EXP reward 91–99).
> GH Churchyard (Dark Priest — 83–118; Eden EXP reward 91–99). Gramps Low TI (70–100)
> overlaps with the lower end of this arc; several GH monsters are Gramps eligible.
> Holy and fire element throughout. This arc and Arc 6/7 are parallel Act II tracks —
> run them in whatever order suits the party's level and interest.

   

### Arc 9 — Frozen Faith · Rachel · Lvl 80–145
[[Nations/Arunafeltz_States]]' rot, foreshadowed in Arc 8. The church has buried the truth:
the Ice Dungeon is a site where the **Ash Vacuum bleeds through**. The arc opens on Rachel
Field 12 (Roween — 81–115) and Rachel Field 01 (Desert Wolf — 88–123), descends into Beach
Dungeon/Karu (Nereid, Medusa — 83–118, Eden EXP reward 91–99 and 100–110), and climaxes
in Rachel Sanctuary 1 (Vanberk, Isilla — 109–143). The party expose the corruption —
climaxing against **Gloom Under Night** (Ktullanux and the church's "angel weapon" Vesper
as side MVPs). *Connects faith corruption to the dimensional crisis, and drops a major breadcrumb: the cult's ultimate master is a rogue god, not a demon.*

> **Renewal spots:** Beach Dungeon/Karu (83–118; good EXP, Bounty Board Comodo). Rachel
> Sanctuary 1 (Vanberk, Isilla — 109–143; Thara Frog or Ogretooth Card essential).
> **Gramps Mid TI (101–125) opens mid arc** — Vanberk and Isilla are prime turn in candidates;
> the dungeon crawl doubles as a weekly turn in run. Cult sigil thread from Arc 1 is exposed
> within the Arunafeltz clergy here.

   

### Arc 10 — The Lab Beneath · Lighthalzen · Lvl 120–165
The dystopia peak. [[Locations/Cities/Lighthalzen]]'s slums hide the Regenschirm/Bio Labs,
where Rekenber has been *manufacturing heroes* — cloned champions and the AI horror
**Kiel D 01**. In renewal the Bio Labs are high game: Scaraba Hole (Antler/Rake Scaraba —
124–156, fire element, Bigfoot Card for reduction) bridges the gap from Arc 9, then Bio Lab
Basement F2 (Demi Human clones — 121–152) and F4 (Demon/Demi Human — 141–165, experienced
geared players only). The "false heroes" (Seyren, Eremes, et al.) are the party's dark
mirror: mass produced versions of what they're becoming. *Pays off the "what are you
becoming?" question at its bleakest.*

> **Renewal spots:** Scaraba Hole (124–156; Doha's Secret Orders quest prerequisite; Dungeon
> Teleporter direct to F1; Bounty Board El Dicastes). Bio Lab Basement F2 (121–152;
> Biolabs Entrance Quest prerequisite; Thara Frog essential; fire element). Bio Lab F4
> (141–165; Dangerous Rumors quest prerequisite; Bounty Board Lighthalzen). **Gramps High
> TI (126–150)** overlaps with Bio Lab entry — use turn in windows as downtime between Lab runs.

   

### Arc 11 — The Wrath of Heaven · Hugel → Abyss Lake · Lvl 110–155
At [[Locations/Cities/Hugel]] the secular and the divine collide. The Valkyries of Odin
Temple have turned *hostile* — proof the divine order itself is destabilizing as the seals
weaken. In renewal, Abyss Lake Dungeon 1–3 (Large Dragon monsters — 111–150) is the primary
adventuring ground; Manuk Field 03 (Tatacho, Centipede, Hillslion — 113–143, Orc Warrior
Card + fire) and Outside El Dicastes (Centipede, Dolomedes — 117–138, Bounty Board) round
out the region. Climax: **Valkyrie Randgris** (Detardeurus in Abyss Lake).
*Raises the stakes from "demons" to "the gods are failing too."*

> **Renewal spots:** Abyss Lake 1–3 (111–150; Sky Petite or Executioner Card; Dungeon
> Teleporter direct to F1; Bounty Board Hugel). Outside El Dicastes (117–138; Dolomedes
> hits hard and fast, casts Fiber Lock). Ancient Odin's Temple (the deep sanctum) is
> 187–215 in renewal — the party arrive at Hugel and push into Abyss Lake, but the temple's
> inner floors are an endgame set piece saved for Arc 19.

   

### Arc 12 — Beyond the Horizon · New World · Lvl 105–145
*In renewal the New World is mid to high game content at 105–145 — not the finale. Moving
it to Act III makes the dimensional rift a mid campaign escalation. The party return to the
New World again at higher levels during the Arc 19 finale.* #custom

The rifts have been leaking since Arc 9. Now one has been stable long enough for scouts to
cross. The party join the Rune Midgard Allied Forces Post and step through — Splendide
Field 01 (Cornus, Naga, Dark Pinguicula — 105–121; Naga uses Waterball, Cornus uses Reflect
Shield), Manuk Field 03 (Tatacho, Centipede — 113–143), and the outskirts of El Dicastes
(117–138). They meet the Sapha and Laphine and realize the New World is not a destination
but a symptom: the dimensional walls are *dissolving*, not just leaking. Climax: **Nidhoggr's
Shadow**, the entity holding the rift open from the other side.
*Sets up the Ash Vacuum thread from Arc 6 for the finale. The party now operate on an
intercontinental scale.*

> **Renewal spots:** Use Cat Hand Access Quest for rapid New World entry. Eden Group and
> Bounty Board Quests exist for Naga, Cornus, and Dark Pinguicula. Bring status resist gear.
> This arc aligns with Gramps High TI (126–150) at its upper end.

   

### Arc 13 — The Island of the Damned · Nameless Island · Lvl 120–165
The cursed Abbey on [[Locations/Other/Nameless_Island]]: the dead refuse to rest because
the boundary between worlds is failing. At its heart, **Beelzebub** — proof the demon lords
are *coordinating* now, not just leaking. The arc pairs the Nameless Abbey (Zombie Slaughter,
Ragged Zombie, Banshee, Necromancer) with Lasagna Dungeon 2: Dragon's Lair (Brute/Plant,
Earth property — 125–159; Bounty Boards Lasagna 140+) and Lasagna Dungeon 3 (141–165) as a
secondary thread — the Lasagna roots contrasting with the Abbey's death.
*The demon coalition from [[Characters/Demons/Satan_Morroc]]'s notes goes active.*

> **Renewal spots:** Lasagna Dungeon 2 and 3 accessible via Dungeon Teleporter (F1 direct).
> Bounty Boards in Lasagna for dungeon monsters (level 140+). Illusion Dungeons become
> available in this arc's level range — use them for off arc sessions.

   

### Arc 14 — The Fire That Ends the World · Veins → Thor's Volcano · Lvl 130–175
[[Locations/Cities/Veins]]' miners feel the tremors the clergy deny. Beneath Thor's Volcano
sleeps [[Characters/Demons/Surt]], the Demon God of fire. The arc opens at Vein Field 03
(Magmaring — 95–130; passive looters, AoE drop tactic), escalates into Eclage/Bifrost Tower
(Lichterns, Antique Book — 136–167; experienced geared players only, high HP monsters with
varied elemental skills), and climaxes in the volcanic depths. The party can't face Surt yet
— but they slay his **herald, Ifrit**, and learn the terrible scale of what's coming.
*The Ragnarok threat becomes literal. Act III closes with the world on fire. The "divine" orchestrator begins to reveal themselves as an entity obsessed with "cleansing" the world.*

> **Renewal spots:** Vein Field 03 (Magmaring — 95–130; stationary passive). Eclage/Bifrost
> Tower (136–167; Dungeon Teleporter direct to F1; veteran players only). Thor's Volcano mob
> levels — verify against your server scripts. #needs-verification This arc marks the
> transition from high game to endgame. After Arc 14, the party should be approaching or
> at the 3rd job range.

   

### Arc 15 — The Hero's Tomb · Aldebaran → Thanatos Tower · Lvl 175–195
*In renewal, Thanatos Tower floors 9–12 are a 180–220 dungeon — true endgame. Moving this
from classic Arc 7 to Act IV means the revelation it carries (the campaign's moral center)
lands just before the finale at maximum dramatic weight.* #custom

Staged from [[Locations/Cities/Aldebaran]]. The Nightmare Clock Tower 3F (Big Bell x109 —
150–180, Penomena Card + Pecopeco Egg Card) serves as the warm up: the tower's nightmare
version has awakened as the dimensional boundary fails. Then the party cross to Thanatos
Tower and gather the five fragments — **Cowardice, Hatred, Malice, Despair, Agony** — to
reach the **Memory of Thanatos**. The fight reveals the whole backstory: a hero who sealed
[[Characters/Demons/Satan_Morroc]] *inside himself* and was destroyed by it.
*The party now understand the Morroc threat — and the price of "winning." Clues here confirm the orchestrator has been manipulating these cycles of heroes and demons for millennia.*

> **Renewal spots:** Nightmare Clock Tower 3F (150–180; enter Clock Tower in Aldebaran, talk
> to Belljamin Button near the 2F portal; Bounty Board Aldebaran). Thanatos Tower F9–12
> (180–220; entrance at Hugel Field 01; floor warper to specific floors; exceptional EXP from
> Thanatos Daily Quests — bring 10 of each Fragment for 50% bonus EXP). Best elements by
> floor: 9 Earth, 10 Holy, 11 Earth, 12 Water. Stack and deliver all Daily Quests under
> the x3 EXP buff for multiple levels per hand in.

   

### Arc 16 — The Royal Banquet · Prontera · Lvl 180–205
As the seals weaken globally, the capital city of [[Locations/Cities/Prontera]] falls into political chaos. The Royal Families fight over the throne while the Whisperers infiltrate the nobility. The party must secure the loyalty of the Royal Guard and dive into the **Prontera Underground Prison** to root out the cult's influence.
*The goal: Securing the Rune Midgard army for the final war, saving the kingdom from collapsing from within.*

> **Renewal spots:** Prontera Underground Prison (141–165 monsters in game, but scaled up for narrative). This connects to the Episode 16.1 (Banquet for Heroes) and 16.2 (Terra Gloria) daily quests. Frame these runs as high stakes political maneuvers and rooting out assassins.

   

### Arc 17 — The Sage's Legacy · Varmundt's Mansion · Lvl 190–215
Realizing they lack the firepower to kill a rogue god or a world ender, the party seeks out a myth: the lost, floating mansion of the legendary Sage Varmundt. They must bypass its rogue AI defenses and uncover ancient research on the [[Concepts/Ymir_Heart]] and the dimensional rifts.
*The goal: Finding the blueprints or weapons needed to actually harm a god. Connects the ancient science of Arc 6 to the finale.*

> **Renewal spots:** Varmundt's Biosphere (240–250 in game, but entered earlier narratively), Tartaros Storage, and Corridor of Phantom. Connects to Episode 17.1 (Illusion) and 17.2 (Sage's Legacy). Frame the grinding here as frantic research and plundering ancient armories.

   

### Arc 18 — The Witch of Death · Niflheim · Lvl 210–235
[[Locations/Other/Niflheim]] is overflowing — every war and disaster the party failed to
prevent has flooded the realm of the dead. In renewal, Niflheim Dungeon F1 (Banquet Hall of the Dead — 200–230) and F2 (Collapsed Opera House — 240–250) sit at the extreme endgame.
Supplement with Illusion Dungeons (Illusion of Vampire, Illusion of Frozen, Illusion of
Underwater 2F — 180–250, no base level EXP modifier) and Episode 17+ daily quests for additional content to bridge the grueling endgame level gaps.
**[[Characters/Mortals/Himmelmez]]**, a mortal who became a monster, rules the chaos. She
is the party's final mirror before the end: *this is where chasing power leads.* She can be
killed — or, daringly, bargained with as the cascade worsens.

> **Renewal spots:** Talk to Bathory at Eden Group (moc_para01 174/33) for the Niflheim warp.
> Illusion Dungeons: no base level EXP modifier — consistent grinding at any endgame level.
> Use Illusion Investigation Daily Quests for EXP between Niflheim runs. This is the last
> major leveling push before the finale.

   

### Arc 19 — Nightmare of Midgard + Beyond the Veil (Finale) · Morroc Ruins → Ash Vacuum · Lvl 230–250+
The payoff of Arcs 3, 7, 15, and the whole cascade. The seal under Morroc shatters; the
city is obliterated; **[[Characters/Demons/Satan_Morroc]]** walks free (Wounded Morroc as
the field form). The party stand exactly where Thanatos stood — but as a *party*, not a
lone hero who must martyr himself.

Then the rifts tear fully open. The party have already walked the New World (Arc 12); now
they go *beyond* it — through the Dimensional Gap into the endgame set pieces: Glast Heim
Abyss (175–200, talk to Nillem at glast_01 191/273), Ancient Odin's Temple (187–215, the inner
sanctum the party only glimpsed in Arc 11) and Einbroch Mine Floor 3 (180–205, the
industrial depth Rekenber used and abandoned). The architect is exposed — [[Characters/Gods/Loki]]'s
design to trigger **Ragnarok** by waking [[Characters/Demons/Surt]]. Endgame confrontation:
Wounded Morroc, Satan Morroc unbound, and the world ending fight itself.

*The campaign ends on the choice Thanatos never had.*

> **Renewal spots:** Glast Heim Abyss (175–200). Ancient Odin's Temple (187–215; Hugel accessible).
> Einbroch Mine F3 (180–205; Einbech daily quests at einbech 143/244). 
> If your server cap is 175, compress this arc into a single dungeon run.

   

## GM Notes — Running It

### Pacing like anime arcs
  **Each arc = a "season."** Open with a town/slice of life beat, escalate through the
  dungeon, climax on the MVP, close with a *thread* (an escaped cultist, a recovered relic,
  a clue) that pulls into the next arc.
  **Track choices as flags.** Use [[Choice_Tracker]] after each session so major quest
  branches, ally changes, XP support routes, and ending gates are paid off cleanly instead
  of relying on memory across 19 arcs.
  **Recurring rival/villain:** keep one named Whisperer agent alive across Acts I–II so the
  cult has a face before the gods/demons take over as antagonists.
  **MVP fights are set pieces,** not random encounters. Telegraph them an arc in advance.

### Following the renewal leveling guide
  **Source: https://irowiki.org/wiki/Leveling_Spots** — cross check your server's mob levels
  against the table; private servers sometimes adjust spawn counts or mob stats.
  **Cap is 175/60 (3rd job) base, extendable to 250/70.** If your cap is 175, Arcs 15–18 are
  the endgame and Arc 19 collapses into a single finale session. If your cap is 250, run all
  four acts as written.
  **Bridging the Act IV EXP Gap:** The level curve from 175–250 is grueling. Do not rely solely on the main narrative to carry these levels. Structure "downtime" sessions around Illusion Dungeons, Episode 17+ daily quests (like the Royal Banquet and Terra Gloria instances), and high level bounty boards. Frame these mechanically heavy sessions as the party frantically shoring up defenses and gathering resources before the world ends.
  **Gramps tier timing:**
    Low TI (70–100) opens in **Arc 6**. Introduce the Gramps NPC in Yuno/Aldebaran.
    Mid TI (101–125) opens in **Arc 9**. Rachel Sanctuary monsters are prime turn ins.
    High TI (126–150) opens in **Arc 10**. Bio Lab entry aligns with this tier.
  **Bounty Boards** cover every region from Arcs 1–14. Each board is a ready made adventure
  hook: a local hunter hands the party a wanted poster for that arc's street level monster.
  **Eden Group Quests** run Arcs 1–5 cleanly. Present kill targets as guild contracts. For official quest scaffolding by region, use [[_iRO_Quest_Source_Map]].
  **Illusion Dungeons** (130–250, no level penalty) are available from Arc 13 onward. Use
  them for off session grinding or when the party is between major story beats.

### Major renewal structural shifts (vs. the classic build)
| Change | Classic | Renewal | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| New World | Act IV finale (Arc 17) | Act III mid campaign (Arc 12, 105–145) | Rift reveal is an escalation, not the ending |
| Thanatos Tower | Act II mid game (Arc 7, 65–99) | Act IV endgame (Arc 15, 175–220) | Moral revelation lands just before the finale |
| Glast Heim | Act I closer (Arc 6, 40–80) | Act II core (Arc 8, 75–120) | Fits renewal's 80–120 dungeon range |
| Niflheim | Act III (Arc 15, 90–99) | Act IV extreme endgame (Arc 18, 200–250) | Dark mirror lands last, before Morroc payoff |
| Ancient Odin's Temple | Act III (Arc 11, 90–99) | Arc 19 finale set piece (187–215) | Sanctum interior is properly endgame |
| Einbroch Mine F3 | Arc 9 dungeon | Arc 19 finale set piece (180–205) | Industrial depth is revealed at the end |

### Non linear options
  Arcs 6 (Yuno), 7 (Einbroch), and 8 (Glast Heim) are parallel Act II tracks — all sit in
  the 68–120 band. Run whichever the party gravitates toward first.
  Arcs 9 (Rachel) and 11 (Hugel/Abyss Lake) are parallel high game tracks — both 80–155.
  Arcs 12 (New World) and 13 (Nameless Island) can swap — both sit in the 105–165 band.
  Arcs 15 (Thanatos Tower) and 18 (Niflheim) can technically swap by level range, but the
  story logic — revelation (Thanatos) → dark mirror (Himmelmez) → release (Morroc) — is
  strongest in the listed order.

### The throughline checklist (so the cascade pays off)
1. Cult sigil in **Arc 1** → cult unmasked in **Arc 4** → cult confirmed global by **Arc 9**.
2. "Sleeping demon" rumor in **Arc 3** → backstory in **Arc 15** (Thanatos Tower) → seal breaks in **Arc 19**.
3. Dimensional instability measured in **Arc 6** → bleeding through in **Arc 9** → stable rift crossed in **Arc 12** → fully torn open in **Arc 19**.
4. "What are you becoming?" — **Arc 10** (false heroes), **Arc 15** (Thanatos), **Arc 18** (Himmelmez), answered by the party's **Arc 19** choice.
