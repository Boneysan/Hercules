# Handout: Cassell's Letter
*(Found pinned to the wall with a silver dagger after Brother Cassell escapes the Geffen Dungeon in Arc 4)*

> **DM Note:** Print this out on clean, pristine paper. Cassell's handwriting is elegant, steady, and perfectly legible.

***

My dear Adventurers,

If you are reading this, I am already gone, and the wards holding the great beast below you have begun to fray. Please do not take my departure as an insult. I simply have other appointments to keep, and I wanted to leave you with a sporting chance to survive the demon's tantrum.

I watched you fight today. You are remarkable. You fight with such passion for a world that is fundamentally broken. You look at the sky and see a roof; I look at it and see a cage. 

The gods built this world not for us, but to imprison the things they feared. Every city you love—Prontera, Geffen, Morroc—is just a padlock on a prison door. The pressure is building. The rot is setting in. If we do not shatter the glass ourselves, the explosion will leave nothing behind.

I serve the Voice that promises a clean slate. I do not hate you. In fact, I hope you survive the coming cascade. The new world will need strong foundations, and you are made of very strong stone.

Do not try to find me. The path I walk is going to get very, very cold.

With utmost respect,

*Brother Cassell*
