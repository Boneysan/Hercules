# Handout: Page from Thanatos's Diary
*(Found clutched in the hands of the Memory of Thanatos after the boss fight in Arc 15)*

> **DM Note:** This should look ancient. If you are printing it, crumple the paper, stain it with tea or coffee, and put a few red "blood" stains on the edges. The handwriting should start strong and end in erratic, desperate scribbles.

***

*(Day 412 since the sealing)*

The desert is quiet today. The people in the city above call me a savior. They build statues of me holding the great sword, striking down the Nightmare of Midgard. 

They don't know the sword broke. 
They don't know the demon didn't die.

I couldn't pierce its armor. The only way to stop it was to draw its essence into the only vessel strong enough to hold it. My own soul.

I am the lock. I am the cage. And the cage is rotting.

*(Day 890)*

It whispers to me. It doesn't use words, just feelings. Despair. Unending, suffocating agony. When I close my eyes, I see the world burning. I see the sky cracking open like a skull. 

The priests came today. They didn't bring medicine. They brought chains. They looked at me not with reverence, but with terror. They see my eyes changing color. They know I am failing.

*(Day ???)*

They are building a tower. Not a monument. A tomb. They are going to lock me at the top, away from the earth, so that when I finally break, the demon will be trapped in the sky.

To whoever finds this... if you climb my tomb... do not make my mistake. 
I thought a hero had to carry it alone. That was the demon's first lie.
The dark is too heavy for one soul. If it must be carried... do not be one. Be many.

Or it will wear your face next.
