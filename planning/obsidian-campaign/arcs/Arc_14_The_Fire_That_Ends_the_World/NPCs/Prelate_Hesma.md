# Prelate Hesma

#custom #campaign #npc

> The Arunafeltz prelate overseeing Veins — Karsh's protégé (Arc 9), sent south to keep
> the mining province faithful and quiet. Hesma watched the party expose Rachel and
> learned exactly the wrong lesson: not "stop lying," but "lie *better.*" She suppresses
> the volcano's warnings with genuine conviction that a calm flock is a saved flock,
> even as the mountain that will kill them breathes beneath her feet.

- **Role:** the arc's **human villain** — the Arc 9 cover-up pattern, *escalated* and aware;
  faith's denial pushed to the brink of the literal apocalypse.
- **Location:** the Veins temple-office; the church's sealed records of the tremors.
- **Propp function:** the villain who **blocks** (church authority bars the deep galleries,
  silences the miners) and **curses** (brands truth-tellers as faithless); fights with holy
  power and the weight of an institution.

## What she wants
To hold the province's faith together through the end — and, beneath that, to not be the
prelate on whose watch Arunafeltz admitted it was wrong. Hesma saw Rachel's faith shatter
when its lie was exposed (the party did that) and has concluded the merciful path is to keep
Veins believing right up until the fire takes them. Denial as pastoral care, with a volcano
as the deadline.

## What she knows
- The tremors are real and she has the suppressed survey data proving it — she chose the lie
  *knowingly.*
- That something enormous sleeps beneath Thor's Volcano — **Surt** — and that the demon
  coalition (Arc 13) is *aiming* at waking it (Carrion's agenda reached her).
- Karsh's network and doctrine — she's a living link in the cult-clergy hierarchy, and breaks
  toward the same "patron none of us have met" hint (Loki seed).

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Condemnation:* "You watched Rachel and chose to lie *harder.*" → "I watched you *break*
   a nation's faith and call it truth. I won't do that to mine." A genuine accusation back —
   she fights to keep the records sealed.
2. *The Arc-8 thesis:* "Aldric was right — certainty is the curse, and you're its priest." →
   pressed with Glast Heim's lesson, Hesma falters; she may *release the survey data* to save
   lives even as she damns herself, choosing her flock's survival over her institution's face.
3. *Dark:* "Keep them calm, then — we'll help you manage it." → she accepts the party as
   fellow shepherds of a comfortable lie; the evacuation never happens, miners die calm and
   faithful, and the Catechism marks the party as accomplices to a sedated slaughter.

## Theme tie-in
Hesma is **the cover-up that learned nothing** — proof that exposing one lie (Rachel) doesn't
cure the disease of institutional denial; it can just teach the next prelate to lie more
carefully. She's Act III's bitter echo of Act II's faith arcs, and a pointed challenge to a
party who thought "we exposed the truth" was a victory. The truth didn't save Rachel's faith;
will it save Veins' miners?

## GM notes / secrets
- Hesma directly *references the party's Arc 9 choices* — if they protected Rachel's faithful,
  she's harder to morally corner; if they shattered them, she throws it in their faces. Make
  the callback land.
- The *Arc-8 thesis* door is the intended pressure-release: the campaign's accumulated themes
  (Aldric's "certainty is the curse") become a *weapon* the party can wield in dialogue.
