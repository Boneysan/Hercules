# Foreman Dunmar

#custom #campaign #npc

> Veins mine-foreman, thirty years underground, with a miner's absolute trust in two
> things: the rock, and the men who read it. The rock has started lying — heat where
> there shouldn't be, a breathing in the deep galleries — and the church says the men
> who report it are drunk or faithless. Dunmar believes his miners. It may cost him
> everything, and he's made his peace with that.

- **Role:** **ally / quest-giver** — the working-class truth-teller against Arunafeltz
  denial; the party's guide into the volcanic deep.
- **Location:** the Veins mine-head; the lower galleries toward Thor's Volcano.
- **Propp function (donor):** provides the mine maps, the deep-gallery routes, evacuation
  knowledge, and heat-warding gear that survives the volcano's approach.

## What he wants
To get his people out alive and to be *believed* before the mountain proves him right the
hard way. Dunmar isn't a hero; he's a supervisor who refuses to send men into a hole he
knows is wrong. He wants the party to confirm the tremors so the evacuation he's been denied
becomes undeniable.

## What he knows
- The tremors and heat-blooms trace to something *vast* and *deliberate* below — not a normal
  vent; the rock "breathes on a schedule."
- The Arunafeltz clergy (Prelate Hesma) is **suppressing** the warnings to protect the faith's
  authority — the Arc 9 cover-up pattern, repeating (and the party have seen it before).
- The safe evacuation routes, the gallery that overlooks the magma cathedral, and how long
  the deep crews have before the next heat-bloom.

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Solidarity:* "We'll get the proof and get your people out." → full maps, warding gear,
   and the miners' trust; the evacuation succeeds and the deep crews live to thank the party.
2. *Pragmatic:* "Just show us the deep gallery." → routes only; the party reach Ifrit but the
   miners are still below when it stirs (a brutal evac scramble in the main quest).
3. *Dismissive:* "Leave the digging to us." → he keeps his maps; the party descend without
   warding *or* evacuation coordination — and miners die in the heat-bloom the party could
   have called.

## Theme tie-in
Dunmar is Greta (Arc 7) at the edge of the apocalypse: the ordinary person who simply *won't
lie about what's in front of him,* set against an institution that needs the lie. In an act
about losing, he's a small, stubborn model of integrity — and a reminder that the cascade's
victims are people with shifts and families, not set-dressing for a boss fight.

## GM notes / secrets
- His evacuation subplot is the spine of the side quest; the faction/integrity choices from
  Arcs 7 and 9 color how readily Veins' workers trust the party here.
- Keep Dunmar alive as a salt-of-the-earth Act IV voice — a miner standing at the world's end
  is a good grounding presence amid gods and demons.
