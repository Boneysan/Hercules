## Synopsis
Veins is already hot before the volcano speaks. Foreman Dunmar brings the party in
because miners are hearing breath under the rock and Prelate Hesma is ordering the
reports sealed. The first crisis is practical — evacuation ledgers, firebreaks,
ash scouting — but every practical action makes the truth harder to deny.

The demon coalition's trail points here because Thor's Volcano is not just another
seal-site. It is a furnace under the world. The rising dungeon should feel like a
countdown in heat: field fires, Bifrost ash, then magma chambers where the rock
itself seems to be waiting for permission to burn.

Ifrit is not the final enemy. That is the important thing. He is a herald, a voice
of flame announcing that Surt is no metaphor and Ragnarok is no distant myth.
Killing him gives the party a victory they need and an answer they do not: the end
is real, awake enough to answer, and close enough that Act IV must be preparation
for an unwinnable war unless the party changes the terms.

## The Beats
1. Cold open: a Veins mine-collapse; survivors who heard "something breathing" below.
2. Hook: the demon coalition (Arc 13) pointed here; the Sigil Ring scalds.
3. Rising dungeon: Vein Field → Eclage/Bifrost → volcanic depths.
4. Turn: this is not a seal weakening — this is the *fire itself,* and it's stirring on purpose.
5. MVP: Ifrit (herald, not the god).
6. Closing thread (Act III finale): the scale of Surt revealed; the party are out of time. Act IV is the reckoning.
