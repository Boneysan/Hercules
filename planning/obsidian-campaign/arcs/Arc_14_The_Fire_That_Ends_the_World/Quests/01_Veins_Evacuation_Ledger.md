# Quest — Veins Evacuation Ledger

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Dunmar keeps a ledger of every family in the mine-shaft tenements, updated by hand
> every shift change. It was meant to track who owed the company store. Now it's the
> closest thing Veins has to an evacuation plan, and Prelate Hesma's office keeps
> "misplacing" the copies that reach the temple.

- **Type:** logistics / social loop
- **Level band:** 130–160
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Foreman_Dunmar]] (a real evacuation plan) /
  [[../NPCs/Prelate_Hesma]]'s temple office (wants the ledger "reviewed" before release)
- **XP role:** the arc's field-clearing loop through Vein Field 03 (Magmaring — stationary
  passive, AoE drop tactic) between logistics runs
- **iRO source:** Veins Siblings Quest; Thor Volcano Base Quest (evacuation/logistics framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the mine-head office, where Dunmar's ledger sits open next to a temple
  seal that's supposed to authorize every evacuation order and never does.
- **Route:** the mine-shaft tenements and Vein Field 03 beyond them, confirming
  households against the ledger while clearing Magmaring drawn by the rising heat.
- **Hand-in:** either Dunmar's office or Hesma's temple desk, depending on which copy the
  party actually deliver.

## Quest object
**Charred Tally-Board** — a wooden tally-board scorched along one edge by a heat-bloom
that reached further than anyone expected. Every name on it belongs to a household Dunmar
has personally confirmed still living in the shaft tenements, tremors and all.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each pass: one field-clearing beat (honest Magmaring XP), one household beat (confirm a
  family's location and readiness to move), one ledger beat (does this update reach
  Dunmar's real plan or Hesma's stalling review).
- Ledger updates that reach Dunmar directly build a working evacuation plan that pays off
  hard in [[04_The_Deep_Shift]] and the main quest's climax — named households the party
  saved reappear at the Arc 19 allied front.
- Ledger updates routed through Hesma's office get "reviewed" indefinitely — the families
  stay on paper, unmoved, while the temple insists calm is the priority.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** evacuate families first, ledger update or no — physically move the most
   vulnerable households now, argue about the paperwork later. Slowest, riskiest for the
   party's standing with the temple, and it's the branch that saves the most lives before
   the main quest's heat-bloom.
2. **Pragmatic:** secure the ore shipments and mining equipment alongside partial
   evacuations — keeps Veins' economy from collapsing outright, splits attention between
   people and property.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** prioritize evacuating fighters and able-bodied miners who can help
   at the volcano's climax over families who can't — a colder calculus that strengthens
   the party's combat support at Ifrit's fight while leaving the tenements' weakest to
   Hesma's stalled paperwork.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** social/logistics XP; Charred Tally-Board updates; named households that
  feed the Arc 19 allied-front turnout.
- **Narrative:** the party watch a cover-up in real time — not denial by silence, but
  denial by endless, polite procedure.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** named-household flags that resurface at [[../../Act_IV_Ragnarok/Arc_19_Nightmare_of_Midgard/Quests/01_Allied_Front_Muster]]; an **Arc 18 named-dead pressure** flag the arc hub calls out directly for whichever households aren't saved.
- **Pays off:** Dunmar's founding fear that he'll be proven right by the mountain instead of believed in time.
- **What are you becoming?:** whose names the party fight to move first when the ledger can't save everyone at once.

## GM notes
- Run this as the arc's primary downtime loop, in parallel with the Bifrost Ash Scouting
  and Magmaring Firebreak quests; the three should braid into one evacuation picture by
  the time [[04_The_Deep_Shift]] forces the real crisis.
- Households left unsaved here are exactly the fuel for the Arc 18 named-dead pressure —
  don't resolve that thread quietly; let it surface again later.
