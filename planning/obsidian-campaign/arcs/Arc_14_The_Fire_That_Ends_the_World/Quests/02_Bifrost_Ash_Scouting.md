# Quest — Bifrost Ash Scouting

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The ash falling around Eclage and Bifrost Tower doesn't come from any fire the temple
> will admit to. Dunmar wants routes scouted and mapped before the miners' evacuation has
> to move through it blind. Something underneath the ash-fall runs hotter than the rest,
> in a pattern too regular to be natural.

- **Type:** high-game scouting quest
- **Level band:** 136–167
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Foreman_Dunmar]] (safe evacuation routes) / a Veins bounty
  board (standard high-value mob contract, no route-mapping required)
- **XP role:** the arc's high-game clearing loop through Eclage/Bifrost Tower (Lichterns,
  Antique Book — veteran players only, varied elemental skills)
- **iRO source:** Bifrost / Mora dailies; Thor Volcano Base Quest (scouting framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the ash-line where Vein Field's familiar heat gives way to Bifrost's
  drifting gray fall, visibility dropping with every step deeper.
- **Route:** Eclage and Bifrost Tower proper, clearing Lichterns and Antique Book nests
  while charting which corridors stay passable and which the heat has already claimed.
- **Hand-in:** Dunmar's mine-head office, where the scouted map gets pinned next to the
  evacuation ledger.

## Quest object
**Prismatic Ash Shard** — a shard of Bifrost ash fused into glass by heat no ordinary fire
produces, refracting light in colors that don't belong to any flame the party recognize.
Dunmar's readers say the fusion pattern repeats on a cycle — like something breathing, on
a schedule, deep below.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each scouting run: one high-game clearing beat (honest Lichterns/Antique Book XP), one
  mapping beat (chart a safe corridor or push into an unmapped one), one heat-signature
  beat (does this reading match the last, or is the cycle accelerating).
- Routes mapped and confirmed safe build the evacuation corridor Dunmar needs for
  [[04_The_Deep_Shift]] — a scouted path that keeps miners from moving blind through the
  ash-fall.
- Routes skipped in favor of chasing rare drops leave gaps in the map exactly where the
  evacuation will need to move fastest.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** map safe routes methodically, prioritizing the corridors closest to the mine
   tenements — slowest scouting pace, but the evacuation map is complete and trustworthy
   when it matters.
2. **Pragmatic:** chase the rare high-value mobs the bounty board wants alongside route
   scouting — balanced pace, solid gear income, a map with a few gaps.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** follow the heat signature directly toward its source rather than
   mapping safe routes at all — the fastest possible read on Surt's waking cycle, and an
   evacuation map that stays dangerously incomplete.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** high-game clearing XP; Prismatic Ash Shards; an **Ifrit telegraphy**
  modifier the arc hub calls out by name.
- **Narrative:** the party get their clearest read yet that whatever's below isn't merely
  hot — it's cycling, deliberately, like something waking on a schedule.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Ifrit telegraphy that primes [[05_The_Herald_in_the_Magma]]; the evacuation corridor [[04_The_Deep_Shift]] needs.
- **Pays off:** the mine's tremors from Quest 01, now confirmed as part of a larger, rhythmic pattern.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's scouting serves the people who'll walk this path later, or just their own curiosity about what's breathing below.

## GM notes
- This is the arc's veteran-tier leveling loop; pace it to bring the party close to the
  arc's top level band before the main quest's climax.
- The heat-signature cycle should feel ominous but not yet explicable — save the full
  reveal for Ifrit himself.
