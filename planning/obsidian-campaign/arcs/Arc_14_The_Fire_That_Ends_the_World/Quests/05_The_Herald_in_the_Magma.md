# Quest — The Herald in the Magma

#custom #campaign #quest

> Beneath Thor's Volcano sleeps the fire that ends the world. The party can't face Surt
> — not yet, not at any level a mortal reaches. But his herald has woken to announce
> him, and killing Ifrit is the only way to learn exactly how large the end is going to be.

- **Type:** main (arc & Act III climax)
- **Level band:** 150–175
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Foreman_Dunmar]] / Wynne (contract of record)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Prelate_Hesma]]
- **MVP:** **Ifrit** (herald of [[Characters/Demons/Surt]])

## Hook (Separation)
Carrion's coalition agenda (Arc 13) pointed here; Dunmar's miners feel it; the Sigil Ring
doesn't just chill now — it *scalds.* The threshold is the mine-mouth's heat-curtain, past
which the rock itself is alive. The party have spent fourteen arcs fighting symptoms of the
cascade; here they descend toward its *engine* — the literal fire of Ragnarok, stirring on
purpose.

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the deep mine-head and the first heat-curtain — the boundary past which the
  stone breathes.
- **Initiation:** Vein Field / Eclage-Bifrost approach into **Thor's Volcano** — the trial
  space *is* the apocalypse, experienced as a *place* before it's an event; a cathedral of
  magma where Surt's heat has made the rock devout.
- **Climax:** the magma cathedral where **Ifrit** heralds his master's waking.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **The herald, not the god:** Ifrit is beatable; Surt is not (and must not be — he is the
  finale's threat). The fight is about *surviving the announcement,* not defeating the fire.
- **Evacuation runs concurrent:** if the deep crews aren't out (Dunmar's *solidarity* door,
  and the side quest), the party fight Ifrit *while* heat-blooms threaten miners — forcing
  split priorities. Dunmar's coordination (or Hesma's suppression) sets how many lives ride on
  the boss fight.
- **Read the scale:** Ifrit's death-throes reveal Surt's true magnitude (a scripted vision /
  Sigrun's sword-shard from Arc 11 resonating). The party *learn* they are out of time — the
  reward is dread, not loot.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**A Cinder of the Sleeping Fire** — an ember struck from Ifrit that *will not cool.* Quenched
in water it steams and burns on; buried in snow it melts a perfect sphere and keeps glowing.
It is a sliver of the fire that ends the world, and nothing the party do extinguishes it —
the campaign's bluntest object about Act III's thesis: *some things you cannot stop, only
delay.* Carried beside the dissolving wall-shard (Arc 12) and the dimming feather (Arc 11),
the party's Act III relics are all *processes they're losing,* not prizes they've won.

## Choices & branches (a price)
- **Force the evacuation (expose Hesma / back Dunmar)** → the miners live, the church's lie is
  broken in a second province — and Arunafeltz's authority, already cracked at Rachel,
  *shatters,* loosing the chaos a faithless people bring as the end nears. Lives saved, order lost.
- **Let the church keep its calm** → Veins stays faithful and quiet, the party avoid another
  schism — and miners die sedated and believing, on the party's silence. The Catechism takes a
  heavy, quiet hit.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** Ifrit clear; Thor's Volcano / Bifrost leveling to ~175 (the 3rd-job
  threshold); the undying Cinder; transition to endgame.
- **Narrative:** **the Ragnarok threat made literal** — the party have stood at the edge of
  the fire and measured it. Act III closes with the world, functionally, *on fire,* and the
  party knowing they cannot yet put it out.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** Surt / the world-ending fire → Arc 19 (the finale's ultimate threat).
- **Pays off:** the "sleeping demon" dread (Arc 3) generalized — there's a *bigger* sleeper
  than Morroc's; the coalition agenda (Arc 13) confirmed; the Arc 9 cover-up pattern named again.
- **"What are you becoming?":** strong enough now to stand beside the end of the world — and
  that strength is itself the warning the campaign has been building.

## GM scripting notes
- **Act III → IV hinge:** after this the party should be at/near 3rd job (175+). The Cinder and
  Sigrun's shard both point toward the finale — use them to make the players *feel* the clock.
