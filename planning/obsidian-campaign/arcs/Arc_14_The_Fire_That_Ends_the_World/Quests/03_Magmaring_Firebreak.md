# Quest — Magmaring Firebreak

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Vein Field's Magmaring packs have always been a nuisance. Since the tremors started,
> they've been a warning system — clustering tighter, drifting toward the tenement wall,
> as if something below is pushing them up and out. Dunmar wants firebreaks cut before a
> cluster panics into the shaft housing.

- **Type:** repeatable field contract
- **Level band:** 95–130 (bridging into the arc's higher band)
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Foreman_Dunmar]] (protect the tenement wall) / a Veins bounty
  board (standard Magmaring contract, no containment planning)
- **XP role:** the arc's entry-level field loop through Vein Field 03 (Magmaring —
  stationary passive looters, AoE drop tactic)
- **iRO source:** Thor Volcano Base Quest; local bounty framing (firebreak/containment)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the tenement wall's outer firebreak line, cut and re-cut every season
  against ordinary field fires.
- **Route:** Vein Field 03, clearing Magmaring clusters before they drift close enough to
  the wall to matter.
- **Hand-in:** Dunmar's mine-head office, cross-referenced against the evacuation ledger.

## Quest object
**Glass-Fused Ash** — field ash fused solid by a Magmaring cluster that burned hotter than
any single monster should manage alone. Dunmar notes the fusion happened in a ring, like
the cluster was reacting to something, not just existing.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each contract: one field-clearing beat (honest Magmaring XP, AoE drop tactic), one
  containment beat (cut the firebreak wide enough or cut corners), one witness beat (miners
  and tenement families watching the party work).
- Firebreaks cut properly and consistently keep the tenement wall safe and directly
  strengthen the evacuation tally the main quest and [[04_The_Deep_Shift]] both draw on.
- Firebreaks rushed for speed clear faster but leave gaps a panicked Magmaring cluster can
  still push through — a cost the GM can cash in during the climax's evacuation scramble.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** clear firebreaks at full width, however slow, prioritizing the tenement
   wall's safety over speed — strongest containment, slowest clearing pace.
2. **Pragmatic:** save miners caught in the open when a cluster ignites, prioritizing
   rescue over containment work — reactive, effective in the moment, and it leaves the
   firebreak itself weaker for next time.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** harvest Magmaring flame for weapon-grade reagents instead of
   burning it off safely — strong personal gear gains, and a firebreak that never actually
   gets cut, just picked over for parts.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** field-clearing XP; Glass-Fused Ash; an evacuation tally modifier feeding
  the main quest's civilian-safety outcome.
- **Narrative:** the party's first physical confirmation that the mountain's unrest is
  already reaching the surface, one scorched firebreak at a time.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the evacuation tally that [[04_The_Deep_Shift]] and [[05_The_Herald_in_the_Magma]] both draw on for civilian-risk outcomes.
- **Pays off:** Dunmar's founding fear made visible in ash and ring-shaped burn patterns before anyone official will admit what's causing them.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's field-clearing routine protects a community or just clears the day's contract.

## GM notes
- Good as the arc's earliest, lowest-friction downtime loop — establish it before the
  Bifrost scouting and evacuation ledger quests raise the stakes.
- Let the ring-shaped burn pattern recur with increasing intensity across repeated runs as
  a slow-building dread cue.
