# Keeper Lysandra

#custom #campaign #npc

> The last living member of the order that sealed Thanatos's tower and spent
> generations making sure no one climbed it. Lysandra is old, exact, and tired of
> secrets — she has guarded the truth of how the hero "won" for so long that she has
> come to believe the guarding was the real crime. She has been waiting for a party
> strong enough to need the truth instead of be destroyed by it.

- **Role:** **ally / quest-giver and mentor** — the keeper of Thanatos's true story; the
  voice that turns the tower from a dungeon into a confession.
- **Location:** the Aldebaran archive; the base of Thanatos Tower.
- **Propp function (donor):** provides the tower's history, the meaning of the five
  fragments, and the rite that lets the party reach the Memory rather than merely fight it.

## What she wants
For the lesson to finally be *learned* instead of buried. Lysandra's order hid Thanatos's
fate to protect people from despair; she now thinks that secrecy is exactly how the cascade
was allowed to grow. She wants the party to understand what winning cost the hero — *before*
they're asked to pay the same price in Arc 19.

## What she knows
- The whole backstory: Thanatos didn't *slay* [[Characters/Demons/Satan_Morroc]] — he
  **sealed it inside himself,** and it consumed him from within (see [[Characters/Mortals/Thanatos]]).
- That the tower's five floors are not a dungeon but a *dissection of one broken soul* —
  Cowardice, Hatred, Malice, Despair, Agony — the pieces the demon broke him into.
- That the Morroc seal (Arc 3's shaft) is failing now, and the party are walking Thanatos's
  exact road one arc early — on purpose, so they can choose differently.

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Humility:* "Teach us everything — we'd rather know than be surprised at the end." → the
   full rite, the fragments' meaning, and her presence at the summit; the party reach the
   Memory *understanding* it, which is the only way to face it without breaking.
2. *Pragmatic:* "Just tell us how to beat what's up there." → tactics only; the party can win
   the fight but miss the revelation's weight — and arrive at Arc 19 without the moral map.
3. *Dismissive:* "We've beaten worse. Stand aside." → she lets them climb blind, grieving;
   "So did he," she says — and the tower's mirror cuts deeper for their arrogance.

## Theme tie-in
Lysandra is the campaign's final mentor before the Choice — the Wynne/Rashid/Aldric lineage
at its most consequential. She embodies the act's pivot: that the gravest danger isn't a
demon but *certainty that you can win without cost.* She hands the party Thanatos's story not
as a warning to avoid the fight, but as the one thing that might let them survive *making the
choice he made alone.*

## GM notes / secrets
- Lysandra is the in-fiction delivery of the campaign's moral center — make her the voice that
  explicitly links Thanatos's "seal it inside yourself" to the Arc 19 Choice and the Pilgrim's
  Offer (Arc 4).
- She should survive into Arc 19 if possible — her presence at the Choice, naming the price,
  is one of the finale's load-bearing beats.
