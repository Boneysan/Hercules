# Quest — Aldebaran Last Letters

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Aldebaran's post office has a locked drawer of letters addressed to people who died
> before delivery — soldiers, pilgrims, a few names Lysandra recognizes from records older
> than the current postmaster knows exist. She's been meaning to sort them for decades.
> She's out of decades.

- **Type:** social / recovery quest
- **Level band:** 176–200
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Keeper_Lysandra]] (wants Thanatos's own letters found among
  them) / the Aldebaran postmaster (wants the drawer simply emptied)
- **XP role:** downtime social/recovery loop through Aldebaran proper, bridging the Clock
  Tower daily loop and the Thanatos Tower ascent
- **iRO source:** Aldebaran quests; fragment turn-ins (social/recovery framing per the
  arc's iRO source map)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the post office's locked drawer, undisturbed long enough that the dust
  has its own history.
- **Route:** Aldebaran's streets and archives, tracing addressees who moved, died, or
  never existed under the name on the envelope.
- **Hand-in:** Lysandra's archive table, where confirmed letters get read, delivered, or
  kept.

## Quest object
**Unsent Tower Letter** — an envelope addressed in a hand Lysandra recognizes immediately:
Thanatos's own, written to someone he never named, sent from inside the tower he was
supposed to have died sealing. It was never delivered because the order that kept his
secret made sure it never left the building.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each search: one investigative beat (honest social XP tracing an addressee through
  Aldebaran's records), one delivery beat (does the letter reach someone, or does its
  truth stay locked away), one weight beat (some letters are painful — a soldier's last
  words, a confession, Thanatos's own).
- Letters delivered honestly, however painful, build **Thanatos's emotional context** —
  the arc hub's own term for what makes his backstory land as tragedy rather than lore in
  [[05_The_Five_Doors_of_a_Dead_Hero]].
- Letters kept back "for the recipient's own good" spare a few people pain in the short
  term and leave Lysandra's archive — and the party's understanding of Thanatos — a little
  thinner.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** deliver every letter to whoever's left to receive it, painful or not —
   slowest, hardest on the party's own composure, and it's the branch that makes
   Aldebaran feel like a city of people rather than a dungeon's front door.
2. **Pragmatic:** keep the genuinely painful truths back, delivering only what won't
   reopen a wound that's already closed — a mercy with a cost: some names never learn
   what really happened to their letter's sender.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** read the recovered letters aloud publicly at the tower's base,
   including Thanatos's own — a dramatic, destabilizing act that forces Aldebaran to
   confront the tower's true history all at once, whether the city is ready or not.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** social XP; Unsent Tower Letters (including Thanatos's own, if found);
  Thanatos's emotional-context modifier for the main quest.
- **Narrative:** the party learn Thanatos as a person who wrote to someone, once, instead
  of only as a cautionary legend.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** Thanatos's own letter, which sharpens the revelation in [[05_The_Five_Doors_of_a_Dead_Hero]] and colors how Lysandra delivers Thanatos's Last Word.
- **Pays off:** Lysandra's founding grief — that her order's secrecy meant even a hero's own last words never reached anyone.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party can carry someone else's unfinished grief without deciding, on their behalf, what they're strong enough to hear.

## GM notes
- Run this alongside the Clock Tower dailies as a parallel downtime loop; both should
  complete before the party climb Thanatos Tower proper in Quest 05.
- Save Thanatos's own letter as the emotional keystone — reveal it late in this quest's
  run, ideally the session before the ascent begins.
