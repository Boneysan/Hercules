# Quest — Fragment Relief Rotation

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Thanatos Tower's daily fragment turn-ins pay exceptional EXP under the x3 buff — ten of
> each Fragment for a bonus most parties grind blind. Lysandra asks the party to do
> something no one else bothers with: sort what they're carrying before they hand it over,
> and notice which fragment they're stockpiling without meaning to.

- **Type:** fragment turn-in loop
- **Level band:** 180–210
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Keeper_Lysandra]] (wants the fragments understood) / the
  Thanatos Tower Daily Quest board (standard EXP turn-in, no reflection required)
- **XP role:** the arc's core endgame turn-in loop through Thanatos Tower F9–12 (floor
  warper access; entrance at Hugel Field 01; best elements 9-Earth, 10-Holy, 11-Earth,
  12-Water)
- **iRO source:** fragment turn-ins; Thanatos Daily Quests (per the arc's iRO source map)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the floor warper platform, where the party choose which of the five
  named floors to descend into next.
- **Route:** F9 through F12, gathering Cowardice, Hatred, Malice, Despair, and Agony in
  whatever ratio the floor's monsters yield.
- **Hand-in:** the Daily Quest board, or Lysandra's archive if the party bring her a
  pouch to read before turning it in.

## Quest object
**Labeled Fragment Pouch** — a turn-in sack the party can label themselves before handing
it over. Lysandra's suggestion: write down which fragment you're carrying the most of
without trying to. Most parties, she says, already know before they check.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each rotation: one floor-clearing beat (honest F9–12 EXP under the x3 turn-in buff), one
  sorting beat (which fragment type came home heaviest this run), one confession beat
  (does the party read what the pouch is telling them, or just cash it in).
- Fragments farmed and *assigned* honestly to a party confession — acknowledging which
  flaw keeps surfacing — feed directly into the Mirror-Shard's strength for
  [[04_What_the_Tower_Remembers]] and the Arc 19 endings that key on it.
- Fragments farmed and traded purely for EXP efficiency clear fastest, but the Mirror-Shard
  stays a plain relic instead of a keyed one.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** farm fragments and read every pouch honestly before turning it in — slowest
   grinding pace, and the only branch that builds real Mirror-Shard strength.
2. **Pragmatic:** trade fragments for whichever daily reward is most useful this week,
   without sorting for meaning — standard EXP efficiency, no Mirror-Shard growth.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** assign each recovered fragment to a specific party member's
   private confession, deliberately, as a kind of ritual accounting — powerful, personal,
   and it means the party's flaws get named out loud before the summit, whether they're
   ready or not.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** turn-in EXP under the x3 buff; Labeled Fragment Pouches; a **Mirror-Shard
  strength** modifier the arc hub calls out directly.
- **Narrative:** the party start recognizing, floor by floor, which of Thanatos's five
  fractures they're closest to carrying themselves.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Mirror-Shard strength that [[04_What_the_Tower_Remembers]] pays off, and that unlocks endings in Arc 19 (see [[../../Choice_Tracker]]).
- **Pays off:** the running Catechism/Doppelganger tally from Arcs 4–14 — this is where it starts being read back to the party, gently, before the Fragments do it unforgivingly.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party can look honestly at a pouch full of their own worst tendency before the tower makes them fight it.

## GM notes
- This is the arc's primary endgame leveling loop — the x3 EXP buff makes it efficient by
  design; use the sorting/confession beat to keep it meaningful rather than pure grind.
- Cross-reference fragment types against the party's actual Arcs 1–14 choices before
  running [[04_What_the_Tower_Remembers]] and [[05_The_Five_Doors_of_a_Dead_Hero]].
