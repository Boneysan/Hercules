# Quest — What the Tower Remembers

#custom #campaign #quest

> The tower doesn't only hold Thanatos's memories. As the party climb, it starts
> showing them *their own* — replaying the choices of fourteen arcs as if the hero's
> tomb were a mirror that has been waiting their whole journey to be looked into.

- **Type:** side — the Catechism tally made visible; a reckoning vignette-chain
- **Level band:** 175–210 (interwoven with the main ascent)
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Keeper_Lysandra]] (who explains the tower's nature)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/The_Five_Fragments]]; the party themselves

## Hook (Separation)
Lysandra warns that Thanatos Tower remembers more than its builder — it is a *psychic
echo-chamber,* and a party carrying heavy choices will find the tower replaying them. The
threshold is internal: between Fragment fights, each party member is shown a scene from their
own past (Holt spared or killed, Echo saved or sold, the miners' chalked names) and asked to
sit in it.

## Spaces
- **Initiation:** the tower's "memory landings" — half-real recreations of earlier arcs that
  bleed up between floors; the party walk back through their campaign as the tower sees it.
- **Reveal space:** a mirror-gallery near the summit where every reflected choice waits at
  once — the party's whole moral record, standing.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **One vignette per Fragment, per party member:** the tower stages a personal scene keyed to
  each PC's actual choices. The "challenge" is *how the player responds to seeing it again* —
  defend it, regret it, or reckon with it.
- **Feeds the Fragments:** a party that *reckons honestly* in the vignettes weakens the
  corresponding Fragment (Cowardice, Hatred, etc.) on the main ascent; a party that *defends
  every choice* feeds them. This quest is the mechanical engine behind
  [[The_Five_Doors_of_a_Dead_Hero]].
- **No NPC judges — the player does:** the tower offers no verdict, only the replay. The
  reckoning is the party's, out loud, at the table.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**The Mirror-Shard Set** — five small mirror fragments, one earned per honest reckoning, that
together form a hand-mirror showing the party not as they are but as they've *chosen* to
become. Assembled, it's the literal object version of the campaign's question, and it can be
*carried to Arc 19* — held up at the Choice, it shows each PC the sum of their road. The
inverse of the Arc 10 Template Vial: the Vial reduced a person to a recipe; this mirror insists
a person is the sum of their *choices,* not their pattern.

## Choices & branches (a price)
- **Reckon with each memory** → the Fragments weaken, the party arrive at the Memory of
  Thanatos clear-eyed, and the Mirror-Shard Set completes — a finale asset that helps a divided
  party choose *together.* Costs them the comfort of their own self-image.
- **Defend the record / refuse the mirror** → the Fragments strengthen, the mirror stays
  broken, and the party reach the summit still certain they were always right — Thanatos's
  exact posture, the demon's first lie intact.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** weakened/strengthened Fragments (feeds the main quest); the Mirror-Shard Set
  (an Arc 19 Choice aid); reflective EXP via Lysandra's framing.
- **Narrative:** the party's fourteen-arc moral record, replayed and reckoned with, *before*
  the Memory of Thanatos asks them to understand his — and before Arc 19 asks them to choose.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Mirror-Shard Set → Arc 19 (helps a party decide the Choice together).
- **Pays off:** the entire Catechism tally (Arc 4 onward) — here it is *seen,* by name, by the players themselves.
- **"What are you becoming?":** the tower stops asking and simply *shows* — the campaign's
  question rendered as a mirror the party assemble from their own honesty.

## GM scripting notes
- This is the home of the Catechism tally's emotional payoff. Run the vignettes as short,
  sharp replays of *real* table choices — specificity is everything.
- Interleave with the main ascent rather than running it separately; each reckoning should
  immediately change the next Fragment fight, so players feel honesty *working.*
