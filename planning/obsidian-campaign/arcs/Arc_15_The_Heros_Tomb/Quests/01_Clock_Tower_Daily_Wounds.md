# Quest — Clock Tower Daily Wounds

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The Nightmare Clock Tower's third floor loses a few more minutes every day, and every
> lost minute leaves something behind — a stopped gear, a trapped spirit, a memory playing
> on a loop it can't finish. Lysandra calls it the tower rehearsing its own grief before
> the real one starts above.

- **Type:** repeatable endgame daily
- **Level band:** 150–180
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Keeper_Lysandra]] (wants the memories read, not just cleared)
  / Belljamin Button near the 2F portal (standard daily-quest framing)
- **XP role:** the arc's entry-level endgame loop through Nightmare Clock Tower 3F (Big
  Bell — Penomena Card + Pecopeco Egg Card)
- **iRO source:** Nightmare Clock Tower dailies; fragment turn-ins (per the arc's own iRO
  source map)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the 2F portal where Belljamin Button hands out the day's assignment
  without ever quite meeting your eyes.
- **Route:** the nightmare 3F itself, clearing Big Bell and its stopped-time echoes,
  finding trapped moments that repeat like a scratched record.
- **Hand-in:** Lysandra's archive table, where the day's stopped hands get sorted by what
  they remembered.

## Quest object
**Stopped Brass Hand** — a clock hand frozen at the exact moment a piece of the tower's
grief calcified into nightmare. Lysandra has a wall of them, each one a fragment of time
that never finished happening.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each daily: one clearing beat (honest Big Bell XP), one time-loop beat (a trapped
  memory playing out — sometimes wordless, sometimes recognizably Thanatos's), one record
  beat (does the party log what they saw or move past it).
- Dailies run slow enough to actually read the memories build "Memory readiness" — the
  arc hub's own term for how prepared the party are to face the Memory of Thanatos with
  understanding rather than just combat skill.
- Dailies rushed for pure XP clear just as fast, but the party arrive at the summit having
  skipped the tower's own rehearsal of what they're about to learn firsthand.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** help the trapped tower spirits finish their loop — stay long enough to let a
   stuck memory play out to its end, even when it's uncomfortable. Slowest daily, but each
   one completed weakens a small piece of the tower's grief before Quest 05.
2. **Pragmatic:** read the memories for information without stopping to release them —
   standard pace, useful context, the loops stay stuck.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** rush the dailies purely for XP, ignoring the loops entirely —
   fastest leveling, and the party climb toward Thanatos Tower having learned nothing the
   Clock Tower tried to teach them first.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** daily endgame XP; Stopped Brass Hands; a Memory-readiness modifier for
  [[05_The_Five_Doors_of_a_Dead_Hero]].
- **Narrative:** the party get their first taste of Thanatos's fracture, in miniature,
  before the real tower asks them to climb through it.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Memory-readiness modifier that shapes how the Memory of Thanatos receives the party at the summit.
- **Pays off:** Lysandra's founding thesis that secrecy, not the truth itself, was the real failure — the loops only resolve when someone finally stays to witness them.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party treat a grieving tower's daily wounds as content to clear or something to actually witness.

## GM notes
- Run this as the arc's opening leveling loop, before the party enter Thanatos Tower
  proper; three to five dailies is enough to build meaningful Memory-readiness.
- Let at least one loop directly foreshadow a specific Fragment (Cowardice, Hatred, etc.)
  so [[05_The_Five_Doors_of_a_Dead_Hero]] doesn't land cold.
