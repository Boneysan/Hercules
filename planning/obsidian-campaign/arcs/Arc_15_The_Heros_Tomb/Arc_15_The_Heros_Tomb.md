## Synopsis
Aldebaran looks orderly enough to feel insulting after Thor's Volcano. The clocks
still turn, the canals still run, and Keeper Lysandra still keeps a tower no one
wants to remember. Then the Nightmare Clock Tower starts losing time, and the city
learns that old tragedies do not stay buried just because they were scheduled out
of sight.

The party's ascent through Thanatos Tower is a pilgrimage through a broken hero's
soul. Cowardice, Hatred, Malice, Despair, and Agony are not generic fragments.
They are named doors, and each door should ask which parts of themselves the party
have fed during the campaign.

At the summit, the Memory of Thanatos gives the campaign its cruelest revelation:
Thanatos did not kill Satan Morroc. He carried the demon inside himself until the
victory hollowed him out. The party leave with Thanatos's Last Word, the Mirror-Shard,
and the knowledge that Arc 19 will ask for the same sacrifice unless they can make
a better ending than history did.

## Spaces (the symbolic geography)
- **Threshold:** the Clock Tower's nightmare 3F — time itself gone wrong as the
  boundary fails; the party climb *out of* normal time.
- **Dream-landscape:** Thanatos Tower — a vertical psyche; each floor a fragment of
  one broken hero (Cowardice → Agony). Ascending is walking *through Thanatos's soul.*
- **Climax:** the summit, the Memory of Thanatos — the hero as the thing he beat.

## The Beats
1. Cold open: Aldebaran's clockwork; the Tower's nightmare version stirs.
2. Hook: the Arc 3 shaft, the Arc 15 backstory — the sleeping demon gets a *name and a victim.*
3. Rising dungeon: Clock Tower 3F → Thanatos Tower F9–12, gathering the five fragments.
4. Turn: Thanatos didn't slay the demon. He *contained* it, and it ate him from inside.
5. MVP: Memory of Thanatos.
6. Closing thread: the party understand exactly what Arc 19 will ask — and that a lone hero's answer was death.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the *method of winning* (seal it inside a person) → the Arc 19 Choice.
- **Pays off:** the "sleeping demon" (Arc 3) — full backstory; the false-hero mirror (Arc 10) — the *real* hero, broken.
- **"What are you becoming?":** Thanatos *won* and it unmade him. The party are stronger than ever and one arc from his exact dilemma.
