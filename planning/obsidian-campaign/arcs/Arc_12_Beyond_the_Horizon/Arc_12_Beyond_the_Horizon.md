## Synopsis
The party have measured the instability in Yuno and watched it bleed through the
ice in Rachel. Now the wound holds open long enough to walk through. At the Allied
Forces Post, officers call it exploration, merchants call it opportunity, and the
people already living on the far side call it an invasion.

Envoy Aelith gives the party the arc's moral center: the New World is not an empty
quest zone. It has nations, fears, borders, and casualties. Captain Roderick Vance
pushes the opposite answer, treating the rift as a strategic corridor and the Sapha
and Laphine as obstacles to be managed.

The rising quests make the party choose what kind of first contact they are. The
climax against Nidhoggr's Shadow proves the larger truth: the dimensional wall is
not opening like a door. It is dissolving like cloth in acid. Closing one anchor
saves lives, but it does not restore the boundary. The world is now too large and
too broken for local heroics.

## Spaces (the symbolic geography)
- **Threshold:** the rift-gate at the Allied Forces Post — the most literal threshold
  in the campaign: stepping out of the world entirely.
- **Dream-landscape:** Splendide / Manuk — a *whole other world,* uncanny precisely
  because people live ordinary lives in it while reality frays at its edges.
- **Climax:** the rift-anchor where Nidhoggr's Shadow holds the wound open.

## The Beats
1. Cold open: the Allied Forces Post; the first stable rift; volunteers stepping through.
2. Hook: the bleed-site from Arc 9 connects here; Sapha/Laphine envoys.
3. Rising dungeon: Splendide → Manuk → El Dicastes outskirts.
4. Turn: the walls between worlds are *dissolving* — leaking was the optimistic version.
5. MVP: Nidhoggr's Shadow.
6. Closing thread: the party now operate intercontinentally; the Ash Vacuum sets up the finale.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Ash Vacuum / dimensional dissolution → Arc 19 (torn fully open).
- **Pays off:** instability measured (Arc 6) → bleeding (Arc 9) → **crossed** here.
- **"What are you becoming?":** beings who step outside their own world to fight — at what scale do you stop being a person?
