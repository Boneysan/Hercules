# Quest — Dicastes Border Papers

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> El Dicastes doesn't recognize the Allied Forces Post's paperwork, and the Allied
> Forces Post doesn't recognize El Dicastes' authority to say so. Somewhere between the
> two filing systems, actual people are trying to cross a border that technically
> doesn't exist yet.

- **Type:** diplomacy / turn-in
- **Level band:** 110–140
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Envoy_Aelith]] (wants a real border process) /
  [[../NPCs/Captain_Roderick_Vance]]'s command tent (wants the paperwork to say whatever
  lets the expedition through fastest)
- **XP role:** downtime diplomatic/social loop set against Outside El Dicastes (Centipede,
  Dolomedes — Bounty Board), run between clearing passes
- **iRO source:** New Surroundings; Nidhoggur's Nest access framing (border/diplomacy)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the makeshift border post outside El Dicastes, a folding table and two
  flags planted by people who don't agree on what the line between them means.
- **Route:** the outskirts themselves, clearing field threats between border-crossing
  errands while ordinary Sapha, Laphine, and human traders wait on paperwork.
- **Hand-in:** either Aelith's negotiating tent or Vance's command post, depending on whose
  writ the party actually process.

## Quest object
**Stamped Border Writ** — a travel document that means something different depending on
which side stamped it. Vance's version grants "provisional passage, pending survey."
Aelith's version, translated honestly, grants "welcome, for as long as it's kept."

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each errand: one field-clearing beat (Centipede/Dolomedes — honest XP, watch for Fiber
  Lock), one paperwork beat (whose writ gets processed, and how honestly it's explained to
  the traveler holding it), one border beat (a specific person — trader, refugee, scout —
  caught between the two systems).
- Writs processed honestly, explaining the real terms to whoever's crossing, build the
  Arc 19 ally quality the arc hub itself calls out for this quest.
- Writs pushed through fast on Vance's terms clear the border queue quickest, but plant
  the same resentment Vance's occupation posture is already building elsewhere.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** obey the paperwork as written, however slow — process every writ by its
   real terms, translate the fine print honestly to whoever's waiting. Slowest crossing
   rate, strongest long-term trust.
2. **Pragmatic:** forge access where the paperwork is clearly just bureaucratic friction —
   speeds urgent cases through without waiting on either command's approval, at the risk of
   both sides noticing the party is cutting corners on their authority.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** negotiate openly between Vance and Aelith's staff to *design* a
   border process that favors the expedition's access — efficient, diplomatically clever,
   and it makes the party co-authors of exactly the occupation Aelith fears.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** social XP; Stamped Border Writs; an **Arc 19 ally quality** modifier, the
  arc hub's own term for how the New World shows up at the finale.
- **Narrative:** the party learn that borders aren't lines — they're whoever's still
  standing at the folding table when the paperwork runs out.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Arc 19 ally quality flag directly (see [[../../Choice_Tracker]]); groundwork for [[04_First_Contact_Done_Wrong]]'s diplomacy stakes.
- **Pays off:** Vance's institutional bad faith made visible in small, bureaucratic increments rather than a single confrontation.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's diplomacy serves the people crossing the line, or whoever's flag is on the table.

## GM notes
- Run this in parallel with the Manuk Supply Line and Cornus Mercy Run; the three

  choice explicitly.
- Track the ally-quality modifier plainly — it's meant to surface, unannounced, at Arc 19's
  allied-front muster.
