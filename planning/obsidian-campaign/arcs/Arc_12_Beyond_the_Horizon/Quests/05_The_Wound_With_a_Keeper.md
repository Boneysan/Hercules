# Quest — The Wound With a Keeper

#custom #campaign #quest

> The rift held open long enough to walk through, so they did. On the other side is a
> whole world where people live ordinary lives while reality frays at the edges — and
> the thing holding the wound open is rooted in their soil, guarded by a human who wants
> it open as badly as the demon does.

- **Type:** main (arc climax)
- **Level band:** 125–145
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Envoy_Aelith]] / Allied Forces posting (Vance's command)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Captain_Roderick_Vance]]
- **MVP:** **Nidhoggr's Shadow**

## Hook (Separation)
The bleed-site the party sealed under Rachel (Arc 9) was one mouth of a wound with many; one
has now held open long enough to cross. The threshold is the most literal in the campaign —
the rift-gate at the Allied Forces Post, stepping *out of the world entirely.* Aelith warns
that what waits is not a place to conquer but a symptom to close, and that a human officer
intends to do the opposite.

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the rift-gate — reality's edge, crossed on foot.
- **Initiation:** **Splendide and Manuk** — a whole other world, uncanny precisely because
  Sapha and Laphine live *ordinary lives* in it while the horizon dissolves; the trial space
  insists the apocalypse is someone's *home.*
- **Climax:** the rift-anchor where **Nidhoggr's Shadow** holds the wound open from the New World
  side, with Vance's command entrenched around it for opposite reasons.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **Two keepers, one door:** the rift stays open because *both* Nidhoggr's Shadow (to spread the
  dissolution) and Vance (to exploit it) want it open. The party must neutralize *both* — and
  Vance can be **turned** (his door 2) to fight Nidhoggr's Shadow *beside* them, flipping the
  hardest obstacle into an ally.
- **New World support:** with Aelith's trust (*humility* door), Sapha/Laphine forces hold the
  flanks and grant the air-charm; without it, the thinning air drains the party over the fight.
- **Close, don't just kill:** slaying Nidhoggr's Shadow isn't enough — the anchor must be *closed*
  using New World craft (Aelith's knowledge). A party that only kills the boss leaves the
  wound gaping, and the dissolution *accelerates* (a worse Act IV).

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**A Shard of the Dissolving Wall** — a fragment of the boundary between worlds, which is
*not* a solid thing but a slowly un-knitting weave the party can hold as it comes apart
between their fingers. Where Arc 9's bleed was a leak the party could plug, this object
*cannot be kept whole* — it dissolves a little each session. It's the campaign's most
honest object about Act III: the thing they're trying to save is *coming apart in their
hands,* and no relic, ring, or number stops it. The dread of "we are losing" made tactile.

## Choices & branches (a price)
- **Close the rift (Aelith's way, with the New World)** → the wound is stitched, the
  dissolution slowed, the alliance sealed for the finale — but Rune-Midgard loses the
  strategic prize, and Vance (if not turned) brands the party traitors to the crown. Survival
  over advantage.
- **Hold the rift / let Vance plant his flag** → Rune-Midgard gains a foothold and the party
  gain power and standing — and the walls dissolve faster, Aelith's people turn against
  humanity, and Act IV opens with two worlds bleeding into one. The patriotic choice dooms both.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** Nidhoggr's Shadow clear; New World leveling to ~145; the Dissolving-Wall Shard;
  Cat Hand / Gramps High TI overlap; the New World alliance (if earned).
- **Narrative:** the Ash Vacuum thread (Arcs 6, 9) becomes a *place the party have stood;*
  the party now operate intercontinentally, and the dissolution is established as the
  finale's engine.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the dissolving walls and the New World alliance → Arc 19 ("beyond the veil").
- **Pays off:** instability measured (Arc 6) → bleeding (Arc 9) → **crossed and keepered** here.
- **"What are you becoming?":** conquer the apocalypse or close it — and whether the party
  proved Aelith's grim verdict on humanity right or wrong.

## GM scripting notes
- Make the *turn Vance* path a genuine, satisfying option — flipping the human obstacle into
  a frontline ally previews the Arc 19 lesson that not every enemy must be killed.
- The Dissolving-Wall Shard should visibly degrade as a prop — a countdown the party feel.
