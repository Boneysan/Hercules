# Quest — First Contact Done Wrong

#custom #campaign #quest

> A Rune-Midgard scouting party reached a Sapha border-village before the diplomats
> did, and something went badly. Now a New World town wants blood-price, the Allied
> Forces want it covered up, and the party are the only ones both sides will let speak.

- **Type:** side (a diplomatic quest with a body-count fork; sets the New World's verdict on humanity)
- **Level band:** 110–135
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Envoy_Aelith]]
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Captain_Roderick_Vance]]; a grieving Sapha elder

## Hook (Separation)
Before the party's expedition, a stray Allied Forces scout-team panicked at a Sapha
border-village — shots fired, a New World child dead, the truth already being "managed" by
Vance's command. Aelith asks the party to broker the aftermath. The threshold is
diplomatic, not martial: the party walk *unarmed* (or are asked to) into a grieving town
that has every reason to see humans as the wound Aelith fears they are.

## Spaces
- **Initiation:** the Sapha border-village and the Manuk/El Dicastes outskirts — a *living*
  New World community, not a dungeon; the party navigate grief, custom, and suspicion.
- **Reveal space:** the cover-up's paper trail at the Allied Forces forward post — Vance's
  command rewriting the incident as a "hostile native ambush."

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
This is a "make choices matter" set-piece — the party can pursue *truth, peace, or order,*
and the three pull apart:
- **Truth:** expose the scouts' panic and Vance's cover-up → the Sapha get honesty and the
  party get the New World's trust (Aelith's alliance) — at the cost of branding their own
  expedition's commander a liar before the climax.
- **Peace (blood-price):** negotiate a reparation the village will accept → the wound heals
  locally, but it may require the party to hand over the guilty scouts (human soldiers) to
  New World justice — a wrenching trade.
- **Order:** back the cover-up to keep the expedition united → Vance's goodwill and a tidy
  command — and Aelith's people *know,* and the alliance dies before the finale needs it.
- **The fork has no free door:** every path spends *someone's* justice. Act III's "we are
  losing" extends to "we cannot make this fully right."

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**The Child's Carved Bird** — a Sapha mourning-token, a small carved bird the grieving elder
presses into the party's hands: in New World custom, the bird must be carried by whoever
accepts responsibility for a death until amends are made. Whether the party can ever set it
down — and how — measures the whole quest. It deliberately rhymes with Arc 1's refugee-child
drawings: the campaign keeps making its largest themes the property of its smallest victims.

## Choices & branches (a price)
- Outcomes set the **New World's verdict on humanity,** which gates the strength of the
  finale's "beyond the veil" alliance (Arc 19). A party that chose truth/peace arrives at
  Ragnarok with the New World at their backs; a party that chose order arrives more alone.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** New World standing (alliance strength flag for Arc 19); the Carved Bird;
  Splendide/Manuk leveling.
- **Narrative:** the party's *kind* judged by an older people — and the alliance for the end
  of the world won or lost on how they handled one dead child.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the New World alliance strength → Arc 19 finale turnout.
- **Pays off:** Aelith's thesis (are humans the wound?) — tested in miniature.
- **"What are you becoming?":** asked of humanity itself, with the party answering on its behalf.

## GM scripting notes
- Run before the main quest; its outcome sets Aelith's trust and the climax's New World support.
- Keep it weapons-down where possible — a diplomatic quest among the act's monster-fights is
  a deliberate change of pace and a preview of Arc 19's "not every problem is a boss."
