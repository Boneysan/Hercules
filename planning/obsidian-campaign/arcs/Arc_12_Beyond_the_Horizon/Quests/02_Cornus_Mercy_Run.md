# Quest — Cornus Mercy Run

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Splendide Field's Cornus and Dark Pinguicula aren't monsters to the Sapha — they're
> neighbors that occasionally go feral, the way any wild thing might. Aelith wants to see
> whether the party's first instinct, faced with something hostile on a world that isn't
> theirs, is the blade or the option before it.

- **Type:** field contract with restraint
- **Level band:** 107–138
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Envoy_Aelith]] (a lesson dressed as a bounty) / the Allied
  Forces Post board (a standard kill contract, no restraint required)
- **XP role:** the arc's field-clearing loop through Splendide Field 01 (Cornus, Naga,
  Dark Pinguicula — Naga uses Waterball, Cornus uses Reflect Shield)
- **iRO source:** Onward to the New World; Ring of the Wise King (first-contact framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Splendide field-edge, where Sapha herders keep a wary distance from
  whatever the humans decide to do next.
- **Route:** the field itself, encountering feral Cornus and Dark Pinguicula that a Sapha
  herder would drive off rather than kill.
- **Hand-in:** either Aelith's post at the Allied Forces gate, or the standard bounty
  window, depending on which contract the party actually ran.

## Quest object
**White Horn Splinter** — a broken piece of Cornus horn, shed rather than harvested. A
Sapha herder gives it to the party as thanks, or doesn't, depending on how the field
encounter went. Aelith keeps a small collection of splinters from parties who earned one.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each contract: one field-clearing beat (honest Splendide XP), one restraint beat (kill,
  drive off, or negotiate with a feral creature a Sapha herder would recognize by name),
  one witness beat (a herder watching to see which the party choose).
- Contracts run with restraint — driving off rather than slaughtering, when the field
  allows it — build the alliance trust Aelith weighs directly in her own trust of the
  party.
- Contracts run as straight kill work pay the same bounty and clear the field just as
  fast, but every herder who watched adds one more data point to Aelith's fear that humans
  default to the blade.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** kill only what's actively hostile and spare what's merely startled — slower,
   requires reading each encounter, and it's the branch Aelith's trust responds to most.
2. **Pragmatic:** drive threats off the herding grounds without killing where possible —
   balanced pace, solid standing, less dramatic than sparing something mid-fight.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** clear the field completely and offer the corpses to Aelith as
   "proof of restraint" — a hollow gesture; she recognizes exactly what it is, and the
   Catechism tally notices a party performing virtue instead of practicing it.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** field XP; White Horn Splinters; alliance trust feeding
  [[04_First_Contact_Done_Wrong]].
- **Narrative:** the party learn that on someone else's world, "clearing the field" and
  "keeping the peace" are not the same contract, even when they pay the same coin.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** alliance trust that directly shapes Aelith's doors in [[04_First_Contact_Done_Wrong]] and the New World's Arc 19 turnout.
- **Pays off:** Aelith's founding fear — that every human who crosses the rift to fight confirms her worst reading of the species.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's efficiency reads, to the people watching, as mercy or as a well-practiced kill.

## GM notes
- Run this early, before Vance's occupation posture hardens in the main plot — it should
  establish the party's reputation before the diplomacy stakes get sharp.
- Let a Sapha herder recur across two or three runs so the "witness" beat has a face.
