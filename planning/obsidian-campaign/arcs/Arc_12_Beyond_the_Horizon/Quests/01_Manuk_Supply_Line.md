# Quest — Manuk Supply Line

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The Allied Forces Post runs two supply manifests: one for the expedition's own troops,
> and a thinner one for the Sapha villages whose hunting grounds the camp now sits on.
> Aelith didn't ask the party to fix that. She's watching to see if they notice it
> unasked.

- **Type:** escort / supply loop
- **Level band:** 106–135
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Envoy_Aelith]] (the Sapha's thin manifest) /
  [[../NPCs/Captain_Roderick_Vance]]'s quartermaster (the expedition's full one)
- **XP role:** the arc's escort loop through Manuk Field 03 (Tatacho, Centipede,
  Hillslion — Orc Warrior Card + fire)
- **iRO source:** New Surroundings; Cat Hand Services (supply/access framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Allied Forces Post loading yard, where two carts load side by side —
  one marked for the barracks, one for the Sapha village down the field.
- **Route:** Manuk Field 03, escorting whichever cart the party choose past Tatacho and
  Hillslion packs drawn by the noise of a camp that wasn't there a month ago.
- **Hand-in:** either the barracks quartermaster's ledger or a Sapha elder's tally stick,
  which keeps count very differently.

## Quest object
**Blue Supply Knot** — a cord-and-bead tally the Sapha use to track debts and gifts, tied
in a pattern that means something closer to "owed" than "paid." Aelith explains it once
and never again; whether the party remember the difference is the point.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each run: one escort-clearing beat (honest Manuk field XP), one manifest beat (which
  cart gets the party's protection first), one ledger beat (does the tally knot read as a
  debt repaid or a debt ignored).
- Runs that keep the Sapha manifest current build the cross-faction standing Aelith needs
  to trust the party's account of Vance's expedition — feeding [[04_First_Contact_Done_Wrong]].
- Runs that favor the barracks every time keep the expedition well-supplied and the Sapha
  quietly aware of exactly where they rank.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** supply the Sapha village first, every run, even when it slows the barracks
   — slowest for the expedition, and it's the clearest single action Aelith can point to
   when she argues for trusting the party.
2. **Pragmatic:** supply Sapha and expedition on an even rotation — steady standing with
   both, no strong signal to either side.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** split resources slowly, skimming a little from each manifest to
   build a private stockpile — efficient personal gain, and a debt knot that reads, by the
   run's end, as very much unpaid.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** escort XP; Blue Supply Knots; cross-faction standing with the Sapha and
  the expedition.
- **Narrative:** the party learn that "first contact" isn't a single conversation — it's
  who gets fed first, run after run.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the cross-faction standing that colors Aelith's trust in [[04_First_Contact_Done_Wrong]] and [[05_The_Wound_With_a_Keeper]].
- **Pays off:** Aelith's opening fear that humans confirm their own worst reading by how they treat the people already living here.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's fairness is a policy or just whoever asked last.

## GM notes
- Run this as the arc's opening downtime loop, before first contact escalates in Quest 04.
- Keep the tally-knot bookkeeping visible but light — a running count Aelith references
  once, pointedly, near the arc's climax.
