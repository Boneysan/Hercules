# Envoy Aelith

#custom #campaign #npc

> A Laphine envoy sent to meet the humans pouring through the rift — graceful, ancient,
> and quietly horrified. The Laphine and Sapha have watched the dimensional walls fray
> for a generation, and Aelith has concluded what no human wants to hear: the rift isn't
> a door humans opened. It's a *wound humans are.* She came to negotiate. She's begun to
> wonder if she came to triage.

- **Role:** **ally / quest-giver — and a mirror with no human loyalties.** She frames the
  New World not as a destination but as a symptom, and the party as possible carriers.
- **Location:** the Allied Forces Post; the Splendide and El Dicastes outskirts.
- **Propp function (donor):** provides safe passage among the Sapha/Laphine, knowledge of
  the rift-anchor, and a Laphine charm that lets humans breathe the New World's thinning air.

## What she wants
To close the wound without exterminating the cause — and she is not yet certain the cause
*isn't* humanity. Aelith wants the party to prove her pessimism wrong by *helping* rather
than conquering; every human who crosses the rift to plunder confirms her worst reading.

## What she knows
- The walls between worlds are **dissolving, not leaking** — the optimistic version (a
  patchable hole) is already obsolete (the grim escalation of Vahl's Arc 6 curve).
- **Nidhoggr's Shadow** holds the rift open *from the New World side* — the
  wound has a keeper, and it is rooted here.
- That a human in the expedition (Captain Vance) intends to *weaponize* the rift, not close it.

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Humility:* "Teach us how to help. We'll follow your people's lead." → full trust, the
   air-charm, the anchor's location; the Sapha/Laphine fight *beside* the party at the
   climax; best outcome, and Aelith becomes an Act IV ally from beyond the world.
2. *Pragmatic:* "Just point us at what's holding the rift open." → she helps warily; the
   New World peoples stay neutral, watching to see what kind of guests humanity sent.
3. *Imperial:* "This is Rune-Midgard's expedition. Stand aside." → she withdraws, grieving;
   the party are alone in a hostile world *and* prove her thesis that humans are the wound —
   which hardens the New World against Rune-Midgard for the finale.

## Theme tie-in
Aelith scales "what are you becoming?" up to a *civilizational* register: not "what are you,
adventurer," but "what is your *kind,*" asked by someone old enough to judge. She's the
campaign's argument that the party's choices are a verdict on humanity itself — and the
finale's stakes are partly *whether the other world decides humans are worth saving.*

## GM notes / secrets
- Her air-charm is the clean mechanical gate for New World content; reward the *humility* door.
- Aelith is the bridge to the Arc 19 "beyond the veil" set-pieces — a kept ally here means the
  New World peoples answer the call at Ragnarok. A burned one means the party face the end
  more alone.
