# Captain Roderick Vance

#custom #campaign #npc

> The decorated officer commanding the Rune-Midgard Allied Forces expedition through the
> rift — a patriot's patriot who has looked at a dissolving universe and concluded that
> the only sane response is to make sure *his* nation holds the high ground when it ends.
> He doesn't want to close the wound. He wants to plant a flag in it.

- **Role:** the arc's **human villain** — the cascade answered with *conquest;* the
  militarized cousin of Krenn's profiteering (Arc 6).
- **Location:** the Allied Forces forward command in Splendide; the rift-anchor approach.
- **Propp function:** the villain who **steals** (claims the rift and New World resources for
  the crown) and **blocks** (military authority walls the party off from the anchor, brands
  diplomacy as treason); fights with disciplined Allied Forces troops.

## What he wants
Strategic advantage in the apocalypse. Vance sincerely believes that if the world is ending,
the patriotic duty is to ensure Rune-Midgard inherits whatever's left — even if seizing the
rift accelerates the dissolution. He is not evil to himself; he's *responsible,* in the
Karsh mold (Arc 9), with an army.

## What he knows
- The rift can be *held* and exploited rather than closed — and that exploiting it is exactly
  what keeps **Nidhoggr's Shadow** anchored (Vance and the demon want the same door open, for
  opposite reasons).
- That the Whisperers have a hand in his expedition's funding (Krenn's account again) — and he
  doesn't care, because their goals briefly align with his.
- The New World's strategic geography — which he reads as a map of conquest, not a home.

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Condemnation:* "Holding the rift open is killing two worlds." → "One of them isn't mine."
   He commits the expedition against the party; a fight that pits them against *human
   soldiers,* not monsters — deliberately uncomfortable.
2. *Pragmatic:* "Close it and you're still a hero — the one who sealed the breach." → a real
   off-ramp: appeal to his patriotism *correctly* and Vance can be turned to *closing* the
   rift for the glory of having done so. Vanity redirected toward survival.
3. *Dark:* "What's Rune-Midgard's cut?" → he reads the party as fellow patriots and folds
   them into the occupation — power and authority for becoming agents of the conquest Aelith
   feared. A heavy Catechism mark, and it sours the New World alliance for the finale.

## Theme tie-in
Vance is **"what are you becoming?" at the scale of nations** — the impulse to *win* the
apocalypse rather than prevent it, dressed in a flag instead of a profit margin or a robe.
He's the Act III proof that even love of one's own people becomes a horror when it decides
some worlds are expendable. The party, who keep "winning" encounters, are asked what winning
is *for.*

## GM notes / secrets
- The *turn-him* door (door 2) is the elegant solution — let appealing to his better
  patriotism close the rift *with* the army instead of through it. It models the Arc 19 idea
  that the finale's villains can sometimes be *redirected* rather than killed.
- If folded in (door 3), Vance's occupation is a live problem the New World allies hold
  against the party at Ragnarok.
