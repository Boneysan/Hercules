# The Familiar Dead

#custom #campaign #npc

> Niflheim is overflowing, and somewhere in the flood of the unrested is a face the
> party loved. An ally who died on the road. A mentor lost in Act II. A soul the party
> failed to lay to rest. They are here now, in the witch's country, and they have not
> forgotten the party — which in Niflheim is a kind of curse.

- **Role:** **ally / guide — and the party's grief, given a voice.** The one who can lead
  them through Niflheim *and* the one whose presence here is an accusation.
- **Location:** Niflheim's outer wards; the Banquet Hall of the Dead.
- **Propp function (donor / helper):** provides passage through the land of the dead, the
  truth of how Himmelmez rules, and a reckoning the party have been deferring since Act II.
- **Who they are (GM's choice — tailor to the table):**
  - **[[../../Campaign/Recurring_Cast#Quartermaster Wynne — Adventurers' Guild handler (recurring ally, Acts I–II)]]** or **Captain Mara** — if lost in Act II to the cult.
  - **Father Quill** (Arc 13) — if his vigil ended in collapse.
  - **The soul from [[../../Act_III_The_Cascade/Arc_13_Island_of_the_Damned/Quests/04_A_Name_You_Knew]]** — the dead the party already half-reckoned with.
  - **Echo** ([[../../Act_II_The_Cracks/Arc_10_The_Lab_Beneath/Quests/04_The_One_Who_Said_No]]) — if returned for the bounty; the cruelest version.

## What they want
Depends on how they died and how the party treated them — peace, or answers, or to know it
*meant* something. Universally: they want the party to see what Niflheim has become, and to
understand that everyone the party failed to save ended up *here,* in the witch's hall.

## What they know
- That **[[Characters/Mortals/Himmelmez]]** rules because she *organized* the overflow — she
  gave the drowning dead a queen, and they were grateful.
- That Himmelmez was, once, exactly what the party are: an adventurer who kept getting
  stronger and never stopped. The dead remember her *climb.*
- The way to Himmelmez's throne — and the one thing that might reach her: she still keeps,
  somewhere, the person she was before.

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Grief faced:* "I'm sorry. I should have saved you." → the Familiar Dead forgive, or
   accept the apology, and guide the party freely; the reckoning the party deferred is paid,
   and they walk into Himmelmez's hall *whole.*
2. *Pragmatic:* "Just help us reach her." → they help, but the unspoken grief sits between
   the party and the climax — Himmelmez will *use* it.
3. *Denial:* "You're not really them. Don't slow us down." → the cruelest refusal; they fade,
   and the party face the witch having denied their own dead one last time — exactly the
   armor Himmelmez recruits.

## Theme tie-in
The Familiar Dead are the campaign's accumulated cost, made personal one final time before
the end. They are proof that "what are you becoming?" has always also been "what have you
left behind?" — and they hand the party the choice of whether to enter the final mirror
carrying their grief honestly or denying it to the last.

## GM notes / secrets
- **Choose the dead for maximum table-specific weight** — ideally a lost ally the party
  genuinely mourn. This NPC is the emotional bridge between Arc 13's reckoning and Arc 18's
  mirror.
- Whether the party faced their grief here directly affects the Himmelmez confrontation
  (below) — a party at peace with their dead can *reach* her; a party in denial only mirrors her.
