# Himmelmez — The Witch of Death (campaign treatment)

#custom #campaign #npc

> See canon: [[Characters/Mortals/Himmelmez]]. This file is her role as the campaign's
> final mirror and Arc 18 antagonist.

> She was an adventurer once. She fought monsters to grow strong, exactly like the party,
> and she *never stopped* — until the growing was all that was left of her and the line
> between hunter and horror had been crossed so gradually she never felt it. Now she
> rules the overflowing land of the dead, not by conquest but by *welcome:* she gave the
> drowning dead a queen, and they love her for it. She is not the party's enemy. She is
> their reflection, twenty years down the road they're already on.

- **Role:** the arc's **antagonist and the campaign's last mirror** — the living (or
  un-living) answer to "what are you becoming?"
- **Location:** Niflheim — the Banquet Hall of the Dead, the Collapsed Opera House throne.
- **Propp function:** the villain who **curses** (commands the unrested dead), **steals**
  (souls, including ones the party knew), and uniquely **recruits** — she offers, sincerely,
  to make the party what she is.

## What she wants
For the party to *understand* — and, if they're worthy, to *join* her. Himmelmez genuinely
believes she solved the problem the whole campaign struggles with: she stopped fearing what
she was becoming and *became it,* and found power, purpose, and a kingdom of the grateful
dead on the far side. As the cascade worsens, she offers the party the same crossing — and,
daringly, an *alliance:* a witch who rules death is a powerful friend against Surt.

## What she knows
- The cascade's near-end state and the demon coalition's plan (she's adjacent to it, not of it).
- That the party's road is *her* road — she can name their compromises (the Catechism tally)
  because she made every one of them first.
- A way to survive the Arc 19 Choice that Thanatos didn't have — *her* way: don't seal the
  demon inside a single martyr, **rule** it. Whether that's salvation or damnation is the
  finale's question, and she truly doesn't think it's the latter.

## Dialogue tree — the three roads (this is the arc's design)
Per the campaign's "make choices matter" rule, the party may **kill, refuse, or bargain** —
and *why* changes everything:

1. **Kill her** — destroy the mirror. A clean, righteous end to a monster — *if* the party
   can still call her one. The dead she ruled scatter back into chaos (a worse Niflheim, and
   Arc 18's overflow). Choosing this *because she's evil* differs from choosing it *because
   the party are afraid she's right* — the GM should make the party say which.
2. **Refuse her** — reject the offer, leave her throne standing. The hardest road: the party
   look at their own reflection, acknowledge the resemblance, and *walk away from it anyway* —
   the campaign's thesis (you don't have to become this) enacted against its most seductive case.
3. **Bargain with her** — make the witch of death an ally against the end. A real option:
   Himmelmez's power could be decisive in Arc 19 — but the price is letting the road she
   represents stay *open,* and carrying her into the finale's Choice as a live temptation. The
   most dangerous and most interesting path.

## Theme tie-in
Himmelmez is **the whole campaign asked in a single person.** Thanatos shows what winning
costs; Echo shows that a made-thing can refuse the road; Himmelmez shows what *embracing* the
road builds. She is the dark twin of Echo and the dark future of the party — and she is
*happy,* which is the horror. She makes "what are you becoming?" not a warning but an *offer.*

## GM notes / secrets
- **Make all three roads genuinely viable and genuinely costly.** This is the second-to-last
  Choice and the rehearsal for the last; never signpost a "correct" answer.
- If the party **returned Echo for bounty** (Arc 10), Himmelmez *has Echo's pattern* and can
  throw it in their faces — the cruelest payoff. If they **saved Echo,** Echo's refusal of the
  road is the party's best counter-argument to the witch.
- Whether the party killed/refused/bargained here **gates the Arc 19 endings** — carry the
  decision forward explicitly.
