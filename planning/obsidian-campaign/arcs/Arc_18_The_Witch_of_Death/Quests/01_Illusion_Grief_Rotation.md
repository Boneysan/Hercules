# Quest — Illusion Grief Rotation

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The Illusion Dungeons don't just replay old fights — near Niflheim's border, they start
> replaying the party's own. A vampire's parlor becomes a compromise the party made in
> Act II; a frozen hall becomes a face they didn't save. The Familiar Dead call it bleed —
> Niflheim's overflow finding the easiest doors first.

- **Type:** off-arc daily bridge
- **Level band:** 190–250 (no base level EXP penalty)
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/The_Familiar_Dead]] (confront what surfaces honestly) /
  the Illusion Investigation daily board (standard grinding framing, no reflection
  required)
- **XP role:** the arc's no-penalty endgame leveling loop through Illusion of Vampire,
  Illusion of Frozen, and Illusion of Underwater 2F
- **iRO source:** Illusion dungeons; Illusion Investigation Daily Quests (per the arc's
  iRO source map)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Illusion Dungeon warp point, which near Niflheim reads less like a
  door and more like a held breath.
- **Route:** whichever Illusion is running that day, its usual monsters interspersed with
  memory-bleed encounters keyed to the party's own record.
- **Hand-in:** the Familiar Dead's waiting place at Niflheim's border, where the day's
  Frosted Memory Shards get looked at, or not.

## Quest object
**Frosted Memory Shard** — a fragment of illusion-ice that holds a moment, perfectly
preserved, the way grief preserves the worst version of a memory. Some shards show
nothing personal at all. Some show exactly the face the party were hoping not to see
today.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each run: one no-penalty clearing beat (honest endgame XP, any level), one bleed beat
  (does today's Illusion surface a personal memory or stay generic), one response beat
  (confront what surfaces, push past it, or use it).
- Runs where memories are confronted honestly build the **Arc 19 mental-resilience flag**
  the template itself names — the party arrive at Himmelmez's throne having already
  practiced looking at their own reflection.
- Runs where memories are farmed past without engagement clear just as efficiently, but
  the party reach [[04_The_Dead_You_Failed]] having never rehearsed the thing that quest
  demands of them cold.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** farm illusions but stop, every time a personal memory surfaces, to actually
   sit with it — slowest grinding pace, and the clearest builder of the mental-resilience
   flag.
2. **Pragmatic:** confront memories when they're unavoidable but otherwise keep the
   grinding pace efficient — balanced XP and resilience-building.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** trade recognition of a memory for a power spike — let a surfaced
   grief sharpen the party's aggression instead of processing it — fast, strong runs, and
   a resilience flag that reads, underneath, as armor instead of acceptance.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** no-penalty endgame XP; Frosted Memory Shards; the Arc 19
  mental-resilience flag.
- **Narrative:** the party get a private, low-stakes rehearsal for the confrontation
  Niflheim is building toward.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the mental-resilience flag that eases [[04_The_Dead_You_Failed]] and colors how the party face Himmelmez in [[05_The_Queen_the_Drowned_Chose]].
- **Pays off:** whatever grief the party have been carrying since Acts I–III, given a controlled space to surface before Niflheim forces it.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party use a private memory as a chance to grieve, or as fuel.

## GM notes
- This is the arc's primary no-penalty leveling loop; use the bleed-memories sparingly but
  precisely — one true, specific callback per session beats several vague ones.
- If Wynne, Mara, or another Act II ally died, let their memory surface here first, softer
  than it will in the Banquet Hall.
