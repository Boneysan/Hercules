# Quest — The Queen the Drowned Chose

#custom #campaign #quest

> Niflheim is overflowing — every war and disaster the party failed to prevent has
> flooded the land of the dead, and the drowning souls did what drowning people do:
> they followed the one person who offered them a hand. Her name is Himmelmez. She was
> an adventurer once. She is what you become if you never stop.

- **Type:** main (arc climax; the campaign's final mirror)
- **Level band:** 200–250
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/The_Familiar_Dead]] / Bathory at Eden (the Niflheim warp)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Himmelmez_Witch_of_Death]]; [[Characters/Mortals/Himmelmez]]
- **MVP / decision:** **Himmelmez** (kill · refuse · bargain)

## Hook (Separation)
The dead the party failed — at Payon, in the desert, on the deep shift, at the Nameless
Abbey — all flowed *here,* and the party's grief (the Familiar Dead) waits at the gate. The
threshold is the warp into Niflheim itself: the *living* entering the dead's own country,
crossing the last border before the end. This is the road's destination — the place the
campaign has been quietly asking the party to look at since Arc 1.

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** Bathory's warp into Niflheim — the living stepping into death's nation.
- **Initiation:** the **Banquet Hall of the Dead** (F1) and the **Collapsed Opera House**
  (F2) — death rendered as decadent spectacle; the trial space is Himmelmez's *aesthetic,*
  power as a grand performance for an audience of the grateful dead. Supplement with Illusion
  Dungeons.
- **Climax:** Himmelmez's throne, amid the overflow of everyone the party couldn't save.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **Reach her through your own dead:** the path to the throne runs through souls the party
  failed (Arcs 2, 3, 5, 13, 14). A party that *reckoned* with their dead (the Familiar Dead's
  grief-faced door; Arc 13's *A Name You Knew*) passes with the dead's blessing; a party in
  denial fights them — the campaign's unpaid grief, collected at the door.
- **The confrontation is a conversation first:** Himmelmez offers understanding, then
  alliance, before she offers battle. The party choose **kill, refuse, or bargain** (see her
  [[../NPCs/Himmelmez_Witch_of_Death|dialogue tree]]) — and the GM makes them name *why.*
- **The mirror scales to the tally:** if it comes to a fight, Himmelmez wields the party's own
  accumulated compromises (Catechism tally) — like the false heroes (Arc 10) and the Fragments
  (Arc 15), but as a *person who chose them on purpose and thrived.*

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**The Adventurer's Pin** — Himmelmez's own first guild-pin, from when she was new at a
fountain like the party once were, which she still keeps on her throne. She offers it to the
party: *"I was you. Here's the proof."* It is the exact twin of **Wynne's first ledger-page**
(Arc 1) — the object that began the party's road, held by the person at its end. Whether the
party take it, refuse it, or close her hand around it for the last time *is* the arc. The
campaign's opening image returns as its final temptation.

## Choices & branches (the decision IS the quest)
- **Kill her** → the mirror shattered; Niflheim's dead scatter into chaos (harder Arc 19
  overflow); the party must decide if they struck down a monster or a warning.
- **Refuse her** → the road acknowledged and walked away from; the campaign's thesis enacted;
  Himmelmez, strangely, *respects* it — and may aid the finale unasked.
- **Bargain with her** → a witch-queen ally for Ragnarok, at the price of keeping her road
  open and carrying the temptation into the Choice.
- All three **gate the Arc 19 endings.** Record the decision and the stated reason.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** Niflheim F1–F2 leveling to ~250 (the last major push); the Adventurer's Pin;
  Illusion Investigation dailies; Himmelmez resolved per the chosen road.
- **Narrative:** the campaign's central question answered *by someone who chose yes* — and the
  party's own answer rehearsed one final time before the finale demands it.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the kill/refuse/bargain decision → Arc 19's available endings; Himmelmez as a
  possible finale ally.
- **Pays off:** the necromancy thread (Arcs 2, 13); the false-hero/Echo mirror (Arc 10); every
  dead the party failed; the whole "what are you becoming?" arc — *here it becomes an offer.*
- **"What are you becoming?":** Himmelmez *is* the answer, and she's recruiting.
