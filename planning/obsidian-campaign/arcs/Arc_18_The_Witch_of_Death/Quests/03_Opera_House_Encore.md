# Quest — Opera House Encore

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The Collapsed Opera House has been performing the same show since before it fell, and
> the dead in the seats never stop applauding. Himmelmez wrote it herself, the Familiar
> Dead say — a performance about a hunter who kept growing stronger, act after act, until
> the audience realized they were watching a coronation, not a tragedy.

- **Type:** high-end dungeon loop
- **Level band:** 220–250
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/The_Familiar_Dead]] (understand the performance before
  facing its author) / the opera's spectral stage manager (wants the show completed,
  audience or no)
- **XP role:** the arc's top-tier endgame clearing loop through Niflheim F2 (Collapsed
  Opera House)
- **iRO source:** Piano Keys; Illusion dungeons (endgame performance/encore framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the collapsed proscenium arch, where the first act is already running
  whether an audience arrives or not.
- **Route:** backstage, the orchestra pit, and the ruined seats, clearing hostile stagehands
  and phantom performers act by act.
- **Hand-in:** the Familiar Dead's box seat, where the night's Cracked Opera Mask gets set
  down or worn.

## Quest object
**Cracked Opera Mask** — a performer's mask split down the middle, one side serene, one
side screaming. Himmelmez, the Familiar Dead say, wore something like it for years before
she stopped needing the mask at all.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each performance: one endgame-clearing beat (honest top-tier XP through the Opera
  House's phantom cast), one performance beat (does the party interrupt the show, let it
  finish, or take a role in it), one recognition beat (the performance's final act
  increasingly resembles the party's own campaign).
- Performances silenced early spare the party the show's full weight but leave Himmelmez's
  own story untold in the party's hearing, weakening the leverage they'll have when they
  finally speak with her.
- Performances allowed to finish, or joined, build direct **bargain leverage** for
  [[05_The_Queen_the_Drowned_Chose]] — the party arrive already understanding her better
  than any other mortal she's faced this age.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** silence the performance once its cruelty becomes clear, refusing to let
   Niflheim's dead keep applauding a coronation dressed as art — a merciful interruption,
   and it costs the party the deepest understanding of Himmelmez's own journey.
2. **Pragmatic:** let the performance complete without interference, watching and
   listening for what it reveals — standard clearing pace, solid insight into Himmelmez's
   history.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** step into a role and let Himmelmez hear the party sing her own
   story back to her — a bold, intimate gambit that builds the strongest possible bargain
   leverage, and one Himmelmez will absolutely remember when the party finally stand
   before her throne.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** endgame XP; Cracked Opera Masks; a bargain-leverage modifier the
  template names directly for [[05_The_Queen_the_Drowned_Chose]].
- **Narrative:** the party watch Himmelmez's whole climb performed as opera — ambition,
  triumph, and the exact moment growing strong stopped being a means and became the only
  thing left.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the bargain-leverage modifier that shapes all three roads (kill/refuse/bargain) in [[05_The_Queen_the_Drowned_Chose]].
- **Pays off:** the "what are you becoming?" question, staged as literal theater before the party face its living answer.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party can watch their own possible future performed and still choose differently.

## GM notes
- Run this as the arc's final endgame leveling loop, immediately before
  [[04_The_Dead_You_Failed]] and the main quest, so its leverage is fresh at the throne.
- The "sing back" branch is the design centerpiece — reward it with specific, personal
  dialogue options when the party finally meet Himmelmez, not just a flat bonus.
