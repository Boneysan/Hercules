# Quest — The Dead You Failed

#custom #campaign #quest

> Before the throne, the party must cross the banquet hall — and every guest at the
> table is someone they didn't save. The witch sets a place for the party at the head
> of it. The only way through is to walk the length of the table and meet each face.

- **Type:** side — the campaign's reckoning, by name; the gate to Himmelmez's throne
- **Level band:** 200–240 (immediately before the climax)
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/The_Familiar_Dead]]
- **Key NPCs:** the named dead of Acts I–III; [[../NPCs/Himmelmez_Witch_of_Death]] (watching)

## Hook (Separation)
Himmelmez doesn't bar the party from her throne — she *invites* them, through the Banquet
Hall, where she has seated every soul the party failed. The threshold is a long table the
party must walk: a deliberate, unhurried procession past their own record of loss. There is
no door to force, only faces to meet.

## Spaces
- **Initiation:** the **Banquet Hall of the Dead** — Himmelmez's masterpiece, a feast where
  the party's failures sit as honored guests; the trial space is *memory as hospitality,* the
  witch's cruelest kindness.
- **Reveal space:** the head of the table, where a chair waits for the party — Himmelmez's
  offer, staged as a seat at the feast of the dead.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **Meet each guest:** the Payon dead the party robbed or honored; the desert souls; the
  refugees lost at sea; Bjorn's flock; the chalked names of the deep shift; lost allies. Each
  rises and *speaks* — and the party answer with honesty, defense, or denial.
- **No combat by default:** like the Fragments and Himmelmez herself, this is reckoning, not
  battle. A party that *owns* its failures earns each guest's release (they rest, leaving the
  hall); a party that denies them leaves a hall full of the unrested to fight *for* Himmelmez
  at the climax.
- **The empty chair:** the head seat is the offer. To sit is to begin accepting Himmelmez's
  road (a heavy flag toward the *bargain*/join endings); to remain standing is to keep the
  party's feet on their own path.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**The Place-Card** — at the head of the table, a name-card in Himmelmez's hand bearing *the
party's own name(s),* as honored guests. It is the final, most intimate version of Wynne's
ledger (Arc 1) and the deep-shift Tally-Board (Arc 14): the book of names, narrowed to *the
party,* placed among the dead they failed. Whether they tear it up, pocket it, or set it back
on the table is a wordless answer to the whole campaign. The dead have been keeping a seat for
them since the fountain.

## Choices & branches (a price)
- **Walk the table and release the dead** → the hall empties, the party arrive at Himmelmez's
  throne *unburdened and clear,* able to refuse her from strength rather than fear; the
  Familiar Dead find peace. Costs the party every comfortable story they told about their failures.
- **Refuse the procession / cut through** → fast, and the party stay armored — but the hall's
  dead fight beside Himmelmez, and the party face the witch still carrying everyone they denied.
  The deferral the whole campaign warned against, called in at the threshold of the end.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** the dead released (eased climax) or roused (harder climax); the Place-Card;
  Niflheim leveling; Illusion dailies.
- **Narrative:** the party's entire road of loss, faced in one room, *before* its architect
  offers to make them her heir — the emotional clearing-of-the-ground for the campaign's last
  two Choices.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the cleared (or carried) grief → how the party meet Himmelmez and, beyond her, the Arc 19 Choice.
- **Pays off:** every named dead since Arc 1; Arc 13's *A Name You Knew;* the Tally-Board (Arc 14); Wynne's ledger (Arc 1).
- **"What are you becoming?":** the question asked by a banquet of the people the party's becoming-strong couldn't save.

## GM scripting notes
- This is the campaign's grief made into a room. Run it slowly; let each guest be specific to
  the table's real history. Resist combat — the power is in the walking and the answering.
- The empty chair is the single most important prop of the arc — make sitting or standing a
  deliberate, witnessed choice that the GM carries into Arc 19.
