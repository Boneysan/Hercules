# Quest — Banquet Hall Procession

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The Banquet Hall of the Dead seats its guests by a logic only Himmelmez understands —
> the recently drowned, the anciently forgotten, and sometimes someone the party swore
> they buried properly. The Familiar Dead walk the procession route with the party,
> because someone should be there who still remembers names instead of place settings.

- **Type:** repeatable undead route
- **Level band:** 200–230
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/The_Familiar_Dead]] (walk the procession honestly) /
  Niflheim's dead-court herald (wants the procession kept moving, no delays)
- **XP role:** the arc's core dungeon-clearing loop through Niflheim F1 (Banquet Hall of
  the Dead)
- **iRO source:** Niflheim Message Delivery; Sign Quest (procession/undead-court framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Hall's outer doors, where the seating logic first becomes visible —
  guests arranged by grief, not rank.
- **Route:** table by table through the Banquet Hall's endless procession, clearing
  hostile seatings while reading who's been placed where.
- **Hand-in:** the Familiar Dead's own place at the table, where the day's Black Table
  Ribbon gets tied off or left loose.

## Quest object
**Black Table Ribbon** — a place-setting marker, one per seat, that names whoever's
meant to sit there. Most are strangers. Occasionally the party recognize a name from
their own record — an ally lost in Act II, a bystander from a mercy run gone wrong.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each procession: one hall-clearing beat (honest undead XP against hostile seatings),
  one recognition beat (does a ribbon name someone the party knew), one response beat
  (seat them properly, banish the seating entirely, or bargain for information).
- Processions run with the dead seated and honored properly build the **grief-clearing
  credit** the template names directly — reducing the emotional weight Himmelmez can
  wield against the party in [[05_The_Queen_the_Drowned_Chose]].
- Processions run as pure banishment clear the Hall fast, but leave every grief unresolved
  for Himmelmez to point to later.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** seat the dead properly, honoring each ribbon's name with the small rite the
   Familiar Dead can teach — slowest procession, and the branch that most reduces
   Himmelmez's later leverage.
2. **Pragmatic:** banish hostile seatings efficiently without dwelling on names — standard
   clearing pace, no additional grief-credit, no additional cost either.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** bargain with recognizable names for information about Himmelmez's
   court before moving them on — useful intelligence, and a use of the party's own dead
   that the Familiar Dead visibly dislikes.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** undead-clearing XP; Black Table Ribbons; the grief-clearing credit that
  feeds [[04_The_Dead_You_Failed]] and [[05_The_Queen_the_Drowned_Chose]].
- **Narrative:** the party walk, table by table, through exactly how much the cascade has
  cost — named, seated, and waiting.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the grief-clearing credit that softens or sharpens Himmelmez's leverage in [[05_The_Queen_the_Drowned_Chose]]; direct groundwork for [[04_The_Dead_You_Failed]].
- **Pays off:** every named loss from Arcs 1–17 that the GM chose to seat here — the campaign's accumulated cost made literal.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party honor their dead's names or process them like any other clearing objective.

## GM notes
- This is the arc's primary emotional-stakes loop; use it to seat specific, table-tracked
  losses from the party's actual campaign history, not generic NPCs.
- Run this before [[04_The_Dead_You_Failed]] so its grief-clearing credit is banked before
  that quest forces the reckoning directly.
