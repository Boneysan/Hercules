## Synopsis
Niflheim is not simply full. It is over capacity, and the overflow has names the
party know. Every war they failed to prevent, every evacuation they botched, every
body turned into a statistic can press against this arc if the GM wants the endgame
to remember the ledger.

The Familiar Dead offers the party a way through the Banquet Hall and Collapsed
Opera House, but never lets the trip become sightseeing. This is a social dungeon
as much as a combat one: dead allies, failed enemies, and innocent bystanders all
ask what the party's victories cost them.

Himmelmez is the final mirror before the finale. She was mortal, ambitious, strong,
and unwilling to stop. She can be fought as the Witch of Death, but the arc is
stronger if she can also be negotiated with. A bargain with her gives Arc 19 one of
its most dangerous non-martyr endings. Killing her denies Niflheim's claim, but may
leave the party with fewer ways to bind the fire.

## Spaces (the symbolic geography)
- **Threshold:** the warp into Niflheim (via Bathory at Eden) — the living entering the dead's own country.
- **Dream-landscape:** the Banquet Hall / Collapsed Opera House — death as decadent
  spectacle; the trial space is Himmelmez's *aesthetic,* power as performance.
- **Climax:** Himmelmez's throne amid the overflow of everyone the party couldn't save.

## The Beats
1. Cold open: the dead arriving faster than Niflheim can hold — the party recognize names.
2. Hook: Voss's "cold north, at scale" (Arc 2) and Beelzebub's coalition (Arc 13) converge on her.
3. Rising dungeon: Niflheim F1 → F2 + Illusion Dungeons.
4. Turn: Himmelmez was *exactly* the party — an adventurer who kept getting stronger and never stopped.
5. MVP: Himmelmez (killable *or* negotiable).
6. Closing thread: Morroc's seal is breaking *now* — the finale begins (→ Arc 19).

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the *bargain* option → widens the Arc 19 ending space.
- **Pays off:** necromancy/soul-harvest (Arc 2, 13); the "what are you becoming?" question — **she is the answer, and she's recruiting.**
- **"What are you becoming?":** the most direct ask in the campaign — she offers the party her own road, and means it.
