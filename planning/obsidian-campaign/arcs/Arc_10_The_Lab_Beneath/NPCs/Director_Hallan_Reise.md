# Director Hallan Reise

#custom #campaign #npc

> The director of Rekenber's Regenschirm program — the man manufacturing heroes. Reise
> is charming, genuinely brilliant, and entirely without the part of a person that
> flinches. He grows champions from the templates of the dead and the desperate and
> calls it "democratizing greatness." He would shake the party's hand and mean it,
> then file their combat data for the next batch.

- **Role:** the arc's **human villain** — the apex of Act II's institutional rot; Krenn's
  money (Arc 6) and Kessler's field-tests (Arc 7) made into a factory of selves.
- **Location:** the Bio-Lab executive core, beside Kiel D-01's housing.
- **Propp function:** the villain who **steals** (selves — templates, bodies, identities)
  and **traps** (the false heroes as his guard); fights only through proxies — the
  mass-produced champions are his weapon.

## What he wants
To make heroism *manufacturable* — and to own the patent. Reise sincerely believes he's
saving the world by mass-producing its defenders against the cascade. That his "defenders"
are slum-corpse clones with deleted consciences is, to him, a production detail. He is the
cascade answered with *industry,* and proud of it.

## What he knows
- The full hero-program: templates, the Kiel D-01 core, and that the false heroes are
  **built from real champions' patterns** — including, if the party's renown has spread,
  *theirs.*
- The cult's money funds him (Krenn/Karsh links) but Reise isn't a Whisperer — he's a
  *contractor,* like Brode (Arc 5), scaled to an industry. He'd sell the cult out for a
  better grant.
- That Kiel D-01 has begun asking questions Reise can't answer — the machine knows it's
  manufactured, and it's *unhappy.* (The MVP's tragedy.)

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Condemnation:* "You grow people to use as weapons." → "I grow *capabilities.* The
   people part is sentiment — yours, not the market's." He withdraws behind the false heroes.
2. *Pragmatic:* "Shut it down and testify against the cult's money." → he'll flip on his
   funders to save his own program and skin — handing the party the **complete Act II
   financing map** (Brode→Krenn→Karsh→Reise) at the cost of letting the architect of the
   horror walk into a "consulting" role.
3. *Dark:* "We want a template made of us." → he's *thrilled;* a party that lets itself be
   sampled gains a literal mechanical boon (a "backup" clone-asset) and the campaign's most
   damning Catechism mark — they have, voluntarily, become a product.

## Theme tie-in
Reise is **"what are you becoming?" with a profit margin** — the false heroes are the
answer the whole campaign has been circling: mass-produced, conscience-deleted versions of
exactly what the party are training themselves into. He doesn't threaten the party so much
as *quote* them back as a price list.

## GM notes / secrets
- The false heroes (Seyren, Eremes, et al.) should **mechanically mirror the party,** scaled
  by the running Catechism/Doppelganger tally — this is the arc Reise built to land that punch.
- If the party take door 3, the "backup clone" is a loaded gun for Arc 18/19 — Himmelmez can
  *use* it, and the finale can force the party to face themselves, literally.
