# Doctor Sabine Reuter

#custom #campaign #npc

> A slum doctor in Lighthalzen's lower city who stitches up the people the upper city
> pretends don't exist. She used to work for Rekenber's medical division — until she
> recognized a "donated" cadaver as a patient she'd discharged healthy the week before.
> She walked out with a stolen access card and has been treating the poor and hating
> herself for her old paychecks ever since.

- **Role:** **ally / quest-giver** — the party's way into the Bio Labs and the human
  conscience of the act's bleakest arc.
- **Location:** a back-alley clinic in the Lighthalzen slums; later, the Bio-Lab entrance.
- **Propp function (helper / donor):** provides the lab access card, the slum network's
  cover, and antidotes for the Bio-Lab's toxins.

## What she wants
To get people *out* — the slum patients above ground, and, if it's possible, the things
in the lab that are still partly people. She's past saving herself; she wants the party to
prove the lower city's suffering wasn't invisible after all.

## What she knows
- The Bio Labs **manufacture heroes** — clone champions grown from real templates — and the
  AI core **Kiel D-01** runs the program.
- The "donations" feeding the labs come from the slums; the upper city's comfort is built on
  the lower city's bodies (Lighthalzen's dystopia, made literal).
- That **Scholar Voss** (Arc 2, if spared) works here now as junior staff — his "scaling"
  wish granted by someone richer.
- The RSX-0806 field-test data (Arc 7) trained the false heroes' combat routines.

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Compassion:* "We get the patients out first." → she gives full access and her network,
   and the slum stands with the party (crowd cover, safehouses); best outcome, and she lives.
2. *Pragmatic:* "Just the way in." → card only; she keeps treating the sick and stays clear;
   thinner support, no slum uprising in the party's favor.
3. *Cold:* "We don't have time for slum politics." → she gives the card and turns away in
   disgust; the party enter without cover and the slums stay silent when they need a crowd
   to vanish into.

## Theme tie-in
Sabine is the **defector who can't forgive herself** — Act II's recurring shape (Naima,
Aldric, Kessler) at its most raw. She took the corporate paycheck, recognized the cost too
late, and now does penance with no expectation of absolution. She's the mirror for a party
who've been taking ever-better loot from ever-worse places: *when did you last ask where it
came from?*

## GM notes / secrets
- If the party were **company-friendly in Arc 7**, the slums distrust them and Sabine is
  warier — the faction choice pays off here.
- Her recognition-of-a-cadaver backstory is the cleanest way to establish the
  slum-bodies-feed-the-lab horror without exposition. Use it early.
