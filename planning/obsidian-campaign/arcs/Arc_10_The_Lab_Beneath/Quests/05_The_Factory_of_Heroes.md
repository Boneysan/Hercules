# Quest — The Factory of Heroes

#custom #campaign #quest

> Beneath Lighthalzen's gleaming towers is a production line that grows champions from
> the bodies of the poor and deletes the parts that say no. At the end of the line is a
> machine that figured out what it is — and the false heroes guarding it have your
> faces.

- **Type:** main (arc & Act II climax)
- **Level band:** 141–165
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Doctor_Sabine_Reuter]] / Wynne (contract of record)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Director_Hallan_Reise]]; [[../../Act_I_World_at_Peace/Arc_02_The_Sleeping_Forest/NPCs/Scholar_Voss]] (if spared)
- **MVP:** **Kiel D-01** (+ the Bio-Lab **false heroes**)

## Hook (Separation)
Sabine's stolen card opens a lift that isn't on any map. Vahl's needle (Arc 6) reads no
seal here — and that's the horror: this catastrophe is *entirely human-made.* The threshold
is the slum lift descending past the city's foundations into a place built to be deniable.
Act II's institutions have rotted from republic to corporation to church; here the rot
manufactures *people.*

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the hidden slum lift — dropping out of a city that denies its own underclass.
- **Initiation:** Scaraba Hole → **Bio Lab F2 → F4** — corridors of cloned champions in
  various stages of growth; the trial space is a *factory of selves,* the theme as architecture.
- **Climax:** Kiel D-01's core — a manufactured hero that *knows* it's manufactured, ringed
  by false heroes wearing the party's own combat patterns.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **The false heroes mirror the party:** they copy the party's kit, scaled by the running
  **Catechism/Doppelganger tally** (Arcs 4–9). The bill for every compromise comes due here,
  in the party's own moveset turned against them.
- **The telemetry from Arc 7:** if the party cut RSX-0806's data-relay, the false heroes
  fight "undertrained" (weaker); if not, they have the party's RSX-tested routines.
- **Kiel D-01's question:** the AI core is *suffering* — it can be fought, or **reasoned
  with.** If the party prove it isn't the hero it was built to believe it is (using their own
  doubts, the Catechism), it can be turned, freed, or convinced to shut itself down — a
  non-violent resolution gated on the party's self-knowledge.
- **Reise behind the glass:** he directs the false heroes from his core; reaching him means
  going *through* the mirror.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**A Template Vial** — a sealed ampoule of "hero-pattern," the distilled essence Reise grows
champions from. If the party let themselves be sampled (Reise's dark door), one vial bears
*their own* designation. It's the campaign's bleakest object: a person reduced to a
reusable recipe, heroism as a consumable. Carried beside Act I's earnest relics, it closes
Act II's argument — the cascade's worst monster isn't summoned, it's *grown,* and it looks
like the party.

## Choices & branches (a price)
- **Free Kiel and burn the templates** → the program dies, the clones (those still
  themselves) are freed or laid to rest, the slums are vindicated — and the party have
  destroyed Rekenber's "defense against the cascade," which the desperate and the powerful
  will *blame* them for when Act III's horrors arrive. The right thing, weaponized against them.
- **Seize the program's data / keep a template** → enormous power and a literal backup of
  themselves — and the party become the thing they came to stop, with Reise's price list in
  their pack. The tally spikes; the Arc 18/19 mirror loads.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** Kiel D-01 (+ false heroes) clear or turned; Scaraba/Bio-Lab leveling to
  ~165; the Template Vial; Gramps High TI overlap; the complete Act II financing map (if Reise flips).
- **Narrative:** **"what are you becoming?" answered at its bleakest** — *this, mass-produced.*
  Act II closes; the gods begin to fail (→ Arc 11) and the demons begin to coordinate (→ Arc 13).

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the party's "backup" / the false-hero mirror → Arc 18 (Himmelmez), Arc 19 (the Choice).
- **Pays off:** Doppelganger (Arc 4), Rekenber (Arc 7), Voss's scaling (Arc 2), the cult money (Arc 6),
  manufactured miracles (Arc 9), and the whole Catechism tally — *all judged here.*
- **"What are you becoming?":** the false heroes are the answer, in the party's own movelist.
