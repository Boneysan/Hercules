# Quest — Scaraba Chitin Order

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The Bio Labs run on chitin plating harvested from Scaraba Hole — cheap, heat-resistant,
> and mined by whichever junior adventurers are desperate enough to work the tunnel
> Rekenber won't staff with its own people. It's the last honest grind before the lab
> stops pretending to be honest at all.

- **Type:** repeatable bridge grind
- **Level band:** 124–156
- **Quest-giver:** local guild board (Rekenber-funded, but processed through Doha's Secret
  Orders framing) / [[../NPCs/Doctor_Sabine_Reuter]] (watches who else works the tunnel)
- **XP role:** the arc's bridge-leveling loop through Scaraba Hole (Antler/Rake Scaraba;
  fire element, Bigfoot Card for reduction) between the slum approach and the lab proper
- **iRO source:** Doha's Secret Orders (prerequisite quest); Biolabs Entrance Quest chain;
  Dungeon Teleporter direct to F1

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Scaraba Hole entrance, where a Rekenber quartermaster weighs every
  load without ever looking up from the ledger.
- **Route:** tunnel by tunnel through Antler and Rake Scaraba nests, sharing the dark with
  other crews working the same contract for the same reasons.
- **Hand-in:** the quartermaster's scale, or Sabine's clinic if the party divert a load her
  way instead.

## Quest object
**Chitin Heat Plate** — a fire-resistant plate cut from a harvested Scaraba, standard
Bio-Lab material. Sabine notes the newest requisition specs call for plates cut to a size
that doesn't match any known lab equipment — sized, instead, for something built to wear
armor. The false heroes' first uniforms, months before the party ever see one walk.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each haul: one tunnel-clearing beat (Antler/Rake Scaraba — honest fire-element XP), one
  crew beat (other desperate adventurers working the same tunnel, sometimes hurt, sometimes
  cutting corners), one requisition beat (does this load's spec match anything Sabine can
  explain).
- Farming efficiently and sharing safe routes with other crews builds a small reputation
  among Lighthalzen's junior adventurers — useful goodwill, no direct evidence.
- Diverting or slow-walking loads toward Sabine's clinic instead of the lab quietly denies
  Rekenber material and builds the requisition-mismatch evidence toward the false-heroes
  reveal in [[05_The_Factory_of_Heroes]].

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** farm efficiently and split every haul evenly with whichever crew is short —
   slower personal gain, but Scaraba Hole stops being a place where the desperate compete
   each other into the ground.
2. **Pragmatic:** protect junior adventurers working the tunnel from Scaraba packs that
   outmatch them, without slowing your own pace — standard reward, solid goodwill, no
   evidence gathered.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** overharvest the tunnel and sell the surplus straight to the
   quartermaster at a markup — fast gear money, and it accelerates whatever the
   oddly-sized plates are actually for.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** bridge-leveling XP; Chitin Heat Plates; "Bio Lab readiness" (gear/prep
  modifier for the F2/F4 push).
- **Narrative:** the party notice the lab's supply chain is provisioning for something
  bigger than itself before they ever see what it's building.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the oddly-sized plate spec that resolves as false-hero armor in [[05_The_Factory_of_Heroes]].
- **Pays off:** nothing yet directly — this is the arc's quiet first tell that Rekenber is manufacturing people, not just weapons.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's grind route helps a community of the equally desperate, or just clears the next rung faster than everyone else.

## GM notes
- Treat this as the natural leveling bridge from the slum approach into Bio Lab F2 — three
  to five hauls is a full loop.
- The oddly-sized plate detail should land as a throwaway line here and pay off hard when
  the false heroes' armor matches it exactly.
