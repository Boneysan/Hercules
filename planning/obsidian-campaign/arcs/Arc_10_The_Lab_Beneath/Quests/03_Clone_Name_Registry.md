# Quest — Clone Name Registry

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Bio Lab F2's demi-human clones are logged by serial, not name — S-7, S-8, S-9 — right up
> until the moment one of them stops moving and Rekenber needs a family to *not* ask
> questions. Someone has been quietly writing the real names back in. Sabine wants to know
> if it's helping or just making the grief worse.

- **Type:** recovery / moral turn-in
- **Level band:** 128–158
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Doctor_Sabine_Reuter]] (wants names restored) /
  [[../../Recurring_Cast#Echo — The One Who Said No (recurring ally / mirror, Acts II-IV)]]
  (has strong feelings about being numbered)
- **XP role:** the arc's dungeon-clearing loop through Bio Lab Basement F2 (Demi Human
  clones — Thara Frog essential, fire element)
- **iRO source:** Biolabs Entrance Quest; Biolab Gear Exchange (record/turn-in framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** F2's intake ward, rows of housing cells stenciled with serial numbers
  over scratched-out first attempts at names.
- **Route:** ward to ward, clearing hostile clone-stock while reading what's left of each
  cell's occupant — a serial, sometimes a name, sometimes both crossed out.
- **Hand-in:** Sabine's clinic, where the recovered names get read aloud once, which is
  more ceremony than the lab ever gave them.

## Quest object
**Blank Name Tag** — an empty registry tag meant to be filled in and never was. Sabine
keeps a growing stack of tags the party fill in themselves, one per recovered identity,
because Rekenber's own paperwork never got that far.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each sweep: one clone-ward clearing beat (honest fire-element XP), one identity beat
  (recover a name, a number, or nothing at all), one Echo beat (Echo, if present, reacts
  differently to each choice — this is the quest that shapes how Echo sees the party
  before [[04_The_One_Who_Said_No]]).
- Names recovered and given back build the emotional case Echo needs to trust the party as
  more than one more crew passing through the lab — directly strengthening the Echo Trusts
  the Party flag.
- Numbers logged efficiently clear the ward faster but leave the registry as cold as
  Rekenber left it — a colder Echo, a thinner case for Arc 19's Refusal ending.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** name the clones — track down every scrap of identity, however slow, and
   write it on a Blank Name Tag. Slowest clearing, and the one branch Echo visibly responds
   to.
2. **Pragmatic:** number them properly instead — a clean, accurate registry by serial, no
   names recovered. Fast, orderly, and it treats the dead exactly as the lab already did.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** erase the records entirely, denying Rekenber any registry at all —
   no names restored, no serials logged, just Rekenber's own paperwork trail wiped. Useful
   as sabotage, useless as memory.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** dungeon-clearing XP; Blank Name Tags (filled or not); Echo's trust
  modifier — the arc hub's own note that this directly strengthens the **Echo Trusts the
  Party** flag from [[../../Choice_Tracker]].
- **Narrative:** the party decide whether the lab's dead get remembered, filed, or erased.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the trust groundwork [[04_The_One_Who_Said_No]] needs to land; a direct contribution to **Echo Trusts the Party** (see [[../../Choice_Tracker]]).
- **Pays off:** Sabine's guilt over the "donated" cadaver — the registry is her attempt to give the lab's discarded people back their names.
- **What are you becoming?:** the party decide, cell by cell, whether a manufactured life gets a name or a number.

## GM notes
- Run this immediately before Quest 04 so Echo's introduction lands on however the party
  handled the registry — a party that named the dead meets a warier, more hopeful Echo.
- This is the arc's clearest lever on the **Echo Trusts the Party** flag; track it visibly
  on the Choice Tracker, not just in dialogue.
