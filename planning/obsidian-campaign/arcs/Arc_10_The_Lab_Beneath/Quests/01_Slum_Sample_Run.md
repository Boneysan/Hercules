# Quest — Slum Sample Run

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Rekenber's "donation drives" pay slum families more than a month's honest work for a
> vial of blood and a signature nobody reads closely. Sabine wants the party running her
> *real* clinic rounds alongside the collectors — so at least some of what gets drawn
> down here actually heals someone first.

- **Type:** social / medical route
- **Level band:** 122–150
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Doctor_Sabine_Reuter]] (the clinic's honest rounds) / a
  Rekenber donation-drive contractor (the paid, official rounds)
- **XP role:** the arc's approach-loop through the slum tenements bordering Scaraba Hole,
  bridging street-level threats into the dungeon proper
- **iRO source:** Biolabs Entrance Quest (prerequisite chain); Dangerous Rumors (medical/
  informant framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the slum-wall checkpoint, where "donation" collectors and Sabine's clinic
  runners cross paths on the same stairs.
- **Route:** tenement to tenement, treating the ordinary sick while noting which addresses
  the official collectors visit twice.
- **Hand-in:** Sabine's back-alley clinic, where the night's cards get sorted — treated,
  flagged, or quietly disappeared.

## Quest object
**Unlabeled Blood Card** — a donation record with a name blacked out and a serial number
inked in its place. Sabine has a drawer full of them. Every one belonged to a patient she
treated and later couldn't find.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each round: one triage beat (honest medical XP treating the ordinary sick), one
  collection beat (a donation drive working the same block, sometimes crossing paths),
  one card beat (a blood card recovered, matched to a name, or lost to the record).
- Cards recovered and matched to living patients build Sabine's case that the labs are
  drawing from people who never agreed to anything — evidence that strengthens the party's
  standing when they finally breach the labs in [[05_The_Factory_of_Heroes]].
- Cards sold or ignored keep the clinic running on Rekenber coin a little longer, but the
  drawer of blacked-out names keeps growing.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** protect patients from the collectors outright — turn drives away, treat for
   free, absorb the lost income. Slowest, costs the clinic real money, and it's the only
   branch where fewer people go missing this week.
2. **Pragmatic:** sell surplus samples to the collectors to fund the clinic's actual
   medicine — a grim trade that keeps Sabine's real work funded at the cost of feeding the
   thing she's trying to starve.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** swap sample cards to shield a specific patient from being flagged
   — effective, personal, and it means someone else's card goes through in their place; the
   party choose who the lab notices.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** social/medical XP; Unlabeled Blood Cards (evidence); Sabine's trust.
- **Narrative:** the party learn exactly how the slums feed the labs — not by force, but by
  a paycheck nobody can afford to refuse.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the blood-card evidence trail that corroborates the lab's sourcing in [[05_The_Factory_of_Heroes]]; Sabine's trust baseline, which the arc hub notes is already colored by the party's Arc 7 faction choice.
- **Pays off:** Sabine's founding wound — recognizing a "donated" cadaver as a patient she'd discharged healthy.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's own leveling work draws from the same slum that's already being drawn from.

## GM notes
- If the party were company-friendly in Arc 7, play the slum's wariness here — fewer
  volunteered addresses, slower trust.
- Run three or four rounds before the main quest; let one recurring patient's card go
  missing partway through to make the stakes personal before the lab breach.
