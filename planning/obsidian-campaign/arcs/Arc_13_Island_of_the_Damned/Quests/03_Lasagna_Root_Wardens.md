# Quest — Lasagna Root Wardens

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Lasagna Dungeon's living roots are the one thing on this island that isn't dead and
> isn't pretending. Quill calls them the boundary's last honest anchor — wherever the
> roots still grow, the wall between worlds is thin but not yet gone. He wants them
> mapped before someone decides they're more useful cut than growing.

- **Type:** off-arc grind bridge
- **Level band:** 128–162
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Father_Quill_the_Doorkeeper]] (protect the boundary anchors) /
  a Lasagna bounty board (standard harvest contract, no boundary concern attached)
- **XP role:** the arc's bridge-leveling loop through Lasagna Dungeon 2 — Dragon's Lair
  (Brute/Plant, Earth property — Bounty Boards Lasagna 140+) into Dungeon 3
- **iRO source:** Lasagna / Illusion routes; Guild Relay Quest (resource/boundary framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the tunnel mouth where Abbey stonework gives way to living root-wall,
  the one boundary on the island still holding by itself.
- **Route:** Lasagna Dungeon 2's Brute and Plant nests, deeper into Dungeon 3 as the
  party's level allows.
- **Hand-in:** Quill's chapel, or the bounty board's coin window, depending on the roots'
  fate.

## Quest object
**Living Root Seal** — a knot of root tissue that pulses faintly, warm in a place that's
otherwise cold straight through. Quill says it's the closest thing on the island to a
seal that's *holding* rather than failing — proof the boundary can still be strengthened,
not just watched as it breaks.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each run: one dungeon-clearing beat (honest Brute/Plant XP, earth property), one root
  beat (protect, harvest, or graft what the party find), one boundary beat (does this
  section of tunnel feel more stable or less, compared to last visit).
- Roots protected and left growing build a small but real boundary-stability credit that
  the main quest can draw on when the Cursed Abbey's own boundary starts to fail at the
  climax.
- Roots harvested for their reagent value pay well immediately, and thin the one anchor
  Quill was counting on.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** protect the roots and leave them growing — no harvest income, slowest
   session, and it's the only branch that visibly strengthens the island's boundary
   instead of just surviving it.
2. **Pragmatic:** harvest a portion for the bounty board while leaving the healthiest
   growth untouched — balanced income and boundary credit, a compromise Quill accepts
   without enthusiasm.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** graft harvested roots directly into the Abbey's failing wards,
   redirecting the boundary's one strong point to shore up a different, weaker one —
   effective triage, and it strips Lasagna's own stability to patch somewhere else.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** bridge-leveling XP; Living Root Seals; a boundary-stability credit the
  arc hub names directly, relevant to how hard the climax's boundary failure hits.
- **Narrative:** the party find the one piece of the island that isn't losing, and decide
  what to do with something that's actually working.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the boundary-stability credit that softens or sharpens the boundary-failure beat in [[05_The_Council_of_the_Drowned]].
- **Pays off:** nothing yet directly — this is the arc's rare moment of something holding, a counterweight to the Abbey's despair.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's leveling grind can also mean leaving one good thing better than they found it.

## GM notes
- Good as an off-arc grinding loop; this is also the arc hub's named gateway into Illusion
  Dungeon access opening from this level range onward.
- Keep the boundary-stability credit's mechanical effect small but real — a difference in
  how many reinforcements arrive at the Abbey's climax, not a stat block change.
