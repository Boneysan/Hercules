# Quest — Drowned Coin Tithe

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The island's dead pay a ferryman's toll that was old before Quill was born — a coin
> pressed into whatever's left of a hand, drowned in the tide pools around the Abbey.
> The Broker Carrion has started buying the coins back. Quill wants to know why a demon
> coalition's concierge cares about small change.

- **Type:** recovery / social turn-in
- **Level band:** 125–158
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Father_Quill_the_Doorkeeper]] (recover the coins honestly) /
  an intermediary working for [[../NPCs/The_Broker_Carrion]] (buying them at a markup)
- **XP role:** the arc's recovery loop through the Abbey's tide-flooded lower crypts and
  approach to Lasagna Dungeon 2
- **iRO source:** Guild Relay Quest; Party Relay Quest (recovery/currency framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the tide pools ringing the Abbey's lower wall, drowned twice daily by
  water that shouldn't reach this high.
- **Route:** pool to pool, recovering coins from hands that don't let go easily.
- **Hand-in:** Quill's chapel table, or Carrion's intermediary at the ferry dock,
  depending on where the coins end up.

## Quest object
**Drowned Coin** — a ferryman's toll, greened with tide-rot, stamped with a face no
current mint uses. Carrion's buyback rate rises every time the party sell him a batch —
he wants them badly enough to overpay, which is itself the tell that they matter.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each recovery run: one tide-pool clearing beat (honest recovery XP against whatever
  guards the pools), one identification beat (does the coin's drowned owner have a name
  Quill recognizes), one sale-or-keep beat (Carrion's agent is always nearby, always
  buying).
- Coins returned to the dead — paid properly, per Quill's old rite — quiet a portion of
  the Abbey's unrest and deny Carrion whatever he's collecting them for.
- Coins sold to Carrion's intermediary fund the party well, and feed directly into
  whatever the coalition's broker is building toward — a detail that pays off, ugly, in
  [[05_The_Council_of_the_Drowned]].

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** pay the dead properly, returning each recovered coin to its drowned owner
   per Quill's rite — slowest, no coin income, and it's the branch that visibly weakens
   the Abbey's restlessness.
2. **Pragmatic:** pay the ferrymen — sell recovered coins to the legitimate ferry
   operators who need the old currency to keep running crossings — steady income, neutral
   effect on the Abbey.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** keep the coins for leverage against Carrion, refusing his buyback
   and stockpiling them instead — denies him his stated goal, at the cost of the party
   carrying drowned currency that reacts, faintly, to the demon coalition's presence the
   closer they get to the council.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** recovery XP; Drowned Coins (paid, sold, or hoarded); a Himmelmez
  foreshadowing flag the arc hub calls out for this quest.
- **Narrative:** the party learn Carrion's coalition is currency-hungry for reasons that
  won't be clear until Niflheim.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Himmelmez foreshadowing flag (→ [[../../Act_IV_Ragnarok/Arc_18_The_Witch_of_Death/Arc_18_The_Witch_of_Death]]); Carrion's buyback pattern, which pays off as part of the coalition's agenda in [[05_The_Council_of_the_Drowned]].
- **Pays off:** Quill's old rite of paying the dead, shown as mechanically real rather than just a line of flavor text.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party treat the dead's toll as sacred, tradable, or a weapon to hold back.
