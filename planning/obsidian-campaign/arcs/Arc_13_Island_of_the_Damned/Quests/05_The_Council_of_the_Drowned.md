# Quest — The Council of the Drowned

#custom #campaign #quest

> The dead on the Nameless Island won't rest because the wall between worlds is failing
> here — and someone has turned that failure into a venue. Beelzebub isn't haunting the
> Abbey. He's chairing it. The demon lords have stopped leaking one at a time. They're
> in the same room now.

- **Type:** main (arc climax)
- **Level band:** 145–165
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Father_Quill_the_Doorkeeper]] / Wynne (contract of record)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/The_Broker_Carrion]]
- **MVP:** **Beelzebub**

## Hook (Separation)
The dissolution the party touched in the New World (Arc 12) has surfaced in the realm of the
dead: the Nameless Abbey's souls won't stay down, and Quill's nineteen-year vigil is finally
losing. Carrion's coalition has booked the failing boundary as a meeting-hall. The threshold
is the ferry to an island no map names — leaving even the *named* world behind for a place
that exists only because reality is forgetting to.

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the ferry across to the Nameless Island; the ferryman who won't wait.
- **Initiation:** the **Cursed Abbey** (+ Lasagna Dungeon 2–3) — the Arc 2 necromancy thread
  grown to a whole island; the trial space is *every soul the party ever failed to lay to
  rest,* pooled in one place. The dead the party "saved" in Act I are here, past saving.
- **Climax:** Beelzebub's council-sanctum, where the demon lords convene and the coalition's
  agenda sits on the table.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **Break the venue, not just the host:** Beelzebub draws power from the *gathered* coalition
  and the failing boundary. **Disrupt Carrion's bindings** (freeing the enslaved risen) and
  **Quill's rite re-stitches the local boundary** — do both and Beelzebub fights cut off from
  his council; skip them and he fights with the strength of every demon lord in the room.
- **Quill at the door:** if the party earned his *reverence* door, he holds the chapel as a
  safe-fallback and lays risen to rest mid-fight (thinning adds); without him, the dead are
  endless.
- **Seize the minutes:** Carrion's coalition agenda can be taken in the chaos — the literal
  roadmap of Arcs 14–17, including the first hard pointer at the Morroc seal and Surt.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**The Coalition's Seating Plan** — Carrion's meticulous chart of which demon lord sits where,
annotated with their aims: Beelzebub, the absent **Satan Morroc** (a chair left *open,*
waiting), and a head-of-table seat marked only with a symbol the party last saw on the Arc 1
Sigil Ring and the Arc 3 capstone. It's the campaign's most chilling object because it's so
*mundane* — the end of the world has a guest list, and there's a place set for something
none of the demons have met. The Loki throne, drawn by a human hand that doesn't know whose
chair it is.

## Choices & branches (a price)
- **Lay the island's dead to rest (Quill's way)** → the Abbey is freed, Quill's vigil ends in
  victory, the coalition loses its venue — but it requires *staying* to perform rites over
  thousands, time the accelerating cascade is charging interest on (the GM advances Vahl's
  doomsday clock). Mercy, paid in time the party may not have.
- **Burn the Abbey and chase the agenda** → fast, the minutes seized, the coalition scattered —
  but the dead are *destroyed,* not freed (an atrocity by omission, the Payon sin at island
  scale), and Quill, unrelieved, simply stops. The efficient choice spends a saint and a
  multitude.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** Beelzebub clear; Cursed Abbey / Lasagna leveling to ~165; the Seating Plan;
  Illusion Dungeons open for off-arc grinding; Lasagna bounty boards.
- **Narrative:** the **demon coalition confirmed active and coordinated** — the cascade now
  has a committee, an agenda, and an empty chair for Morroc. Act III's penultimate gut-drop.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the coalition agenda and the empty Morroc chair → Arc 15 (Thanatos backstory),
  Arc 19 (the seal breaks); the head-of-table symbol → Loki.
- **Pays off:** the necromancy/soul-harvest thread (Arc 2) at full apocalyptic scale; every
  unrested dead the party left behind, gathered to be reckoned with.
- **"What are you becoming?":** the island is a ledger of the party's failures to lay things to
  rest — and they decide, again, whether mercy is worth the time it costs.

## GM scripting notes
- The empty-chair / head-of-table reveal is a major Loki telegraph — stage it so the players
  *notice* the unfilled seats and the familiar symbol.
