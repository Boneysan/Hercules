# Quest — Abbey Bell Rotation

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> The Abbey's bell hasn't rung true in nineteen years — Father Quill keeps it silent on
> purpose, because a true ring calls every restless soul on the island toward the sound.
> He's asking the party to ring it anyway, on a rotation, because a controlled gathering
> beats an uncontrolled one.

- **Type:** repeatable undead patrol
- **Level band:** 122–155
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Father_Quill_the_Doorkeeper]] (a controlled vigil) /
  [[../NPCs/The_Broker_Carrion]]'s agents (want the bell mapped for their own purposes)
- **XP role:** the arc's field-clearing loop through the lower Cursed Abbey (Zombie
  Slaughter, Ragged Zombie, Banshee — drawn to the bell's toll)
- **iRO source:** Nameless Island Entrance Quest; undead hunting (bell/patrol framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the bell tower stair, which Quill hasn't climbed in years for reasons he
  doesn't explain until asked directly.
- **Route:** the lower Abbey grounds, where the bell's toll draws risen dead the party
  clear before they reach the chapel door.
- **Hand-in:** the chapel steps, where Quill counts how many answered the bell and how
  many the party sent back down.

## Quest object
**Cracked Abbey Bell** — a hairline fracture running through the bell's bronze, widening
by a fraction every time it rings. Quill knows exactly how many more true tolls the bell
has left in it and has never told anyone the number.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each rotation: one undead-clearing beat (honest Abbey XP against whatever the bell
  draws), one toll beat (ring it fully, dampen it, or strike it in a specific pattern),
  one listening beat (something in the gathered dead's response that sounds almost
  organized).
- Rotations run as controlled vigils — ringing on schedule, clearing thoroughly — reduce
  the Abbey's ambient unrest and build clues toward the coordinated nature of what's
  gathering below, feeding directly into [[05_The_Council_of_the_Drowned]].
- Rotations rushed or skipped let unrest build faster than the party can clear it, and the
  bell's remaining true tolls run out sooner than Quill would like.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** ring the bell fully to gather and face the dead head-on, giving Quill's
   nineteen-year vigil a controlled release instead of a slow leak — costs the bell one of
   its few remaining true tolls, but clears the largest single mass of unrest at once.
2. **Pragmatic:** silence the bell and clear the grounds by patrol instead — slower,
   steadier, preserves the bell's remaining tolls for when Quill truly needs them.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** tune the bell to a specific pitch that makes one particular dead
   name answer, testing whether the "organized" gathering responds to something more
   precise than noise — effective intelligence, and a use of the bell Quill visibly
   dislikes.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** Abbey-clearing XP; Cracked Abbey Bell fragments; "dead-council" clues
  toward Beelzebub's coordination.
- **Narrative:** the party start to recognize the difference between an ordinary haunting
  and something with an agenda.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the dead-council clues that resolve in [[05_The_Council_of_the_Drowned]]; the bell's dwindling tolls as a ticking resource Quill may need at the climax.
- **Pays off:** Quill's nineteen-year vigil made mechanically real — every ring costs him something he doesn't replace.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party treat an old man's carefully rationed sacrifice as a resource to spend or a burden to share.

## GM notes
- Track the bell's remaining tolls loosely across the arc; make the last one matter at the
  climax if the party have been using it freely.
- Good recurring loop through the lower Abbey before the deeper crypt work in Quest 04.
