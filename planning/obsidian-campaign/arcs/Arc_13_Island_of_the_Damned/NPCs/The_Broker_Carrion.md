# The Broker — "Carrion"

#custom #campaign #npc

> The necromancer-lieutenant who arranges the demon lords' coalition: not a demon, but
> a human go-between who books the Nameless Abbey for their councils the way a
> chamberlain books a hall. Carrion is precise, unbothered, and proud of the work —
> the cult's logistics genius (Brode's spiritual heir) operating one tier from Hell.
> If [[../../Act_I_World_at_Peace/Arc_02_The_Sleeping_Forest/NPCs/Scholar_Voss]] was
> spared, **Carrion *is* Voss** — his scaling ambition arrived at its endpoint.

- **Role:** the arc's **human villain** — the broker who makes the demon coalition *function;*
  the necromancy thread's apex operator.
- **Location:** the Cursed Abbey's antechambers; Beelzebub's council approach.
- **Propp function:** the villain who **traps** (binds the risen as the council's servants and
  guards) and **steals** (souls as currency for demonic deals); fights with the dead and with
  contracts the party can't see.

## What he wants
To be **indispensable** — the one human the demon lords cannot do without. Carrion treats the
end of the world as the ultimate seller's market and himself as its essential middleman. He
has no faith and no fear; he has *clients,* and the clients are demon gods. (As Voss: this is
exactly the "data, not people" logic of Arc 2, finally given infinite scale.)

## What he knows
- The **coalition's agenda** — what the demon lords are coordinating *toward:* the Morroc seal
  (Arc 15/17) and the waking of Surt (Arc 14/17). He holds the meeting minutes of the apocalypse.
- That the boundary-failure here is *deliberate,* arranged to give the dead — and the demons —
  a venue.
- The cult's true hierarchy reaching up past Cassell and Karsh toward "a patron none of them
  have met" — the closest a human villain comes to naming [[Characters/Gods/Loki]].

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Condemnation:* "You broker for things that want everyone dead." → "Everyone *will* be
   dead. I'm arranging the seating." He sets the risen on the party and slips toward the council.
2. *Pragmatic:* "Sell us the minutes — what are they planning?" → he'll trade the coalition's
   agenda (the finale's roadmap) for safe passage off the island; the cleanest intel in Act
   III, at the price of letting the apocalypse's concierge walk.
3. *Dark (if Voss):* "You've come a long way from Payon, Scholar." → a personal reckoning;
   Voss/Carrion is *delighted* to be remembered, and a party that engages his vanity learns
   everything — and confronts how far a man they once spared has climbed on the bodies they
   let him keep.

## Theme tie-in
Carrion is the campaign's coldest thesis made flesh: **the apocalypse runs on human
collaborators, and the most dangerous aren't believers but *facilitators.*** If he's Voss, he
is also the party's own mercy come back monstrous — proof that "we let him go" has a price
measured in worlds. The ultimate "what are you becoming?" asked of a choice the party made
twelve arcs ago.

## GM notes / secrets
- **Strongly consider making Carrion the spared Voss** — it's the campaign's best long-fuse
  payoff and makes the Arc 2 mercy fork *matter* at apocalyptic scale.
- His "patron none of them have met" line is a key Loki seed for the finale; plant it even if
  the party kill him.
- His coalition minutes are the arc's intel object — the literal agenda of Arcs 14–17.
