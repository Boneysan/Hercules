## Synopsis
Nameless Island is the campaign's death-thread grown past containment. What began
as restless ancestors in Payon and ugly necromancy in the margins has become a
whole island where the dead organize themselves around a hunger with a name.
Father Quill asks the party to cross because he has been keeping a door shut for
nineteen years, and tonight the door is speaking back.

The Cursed Abbey gives the party a deathly mirror of their own work. Every rescue
that failed, every body left behind, every name reduced to a quest objective can
return here if the GM wants the bill to come due. The Broker Carrion turns that
pain into commerce, moving relics, names, and souls between interested powers.

Beelzebub's council changes the campaign's strategic picture. The demon lords are
not merely waking in sequence. They are coordinating around the failing boundaries,
with Satan Morroc's prison and Surt's fire as the endgame. Defeating Beelzebub
breaks the council's local host, not the coalition itself.

6. Closing thread: the coalition's next move points at Thor's Volcano and Surt (→ Arc 14).
