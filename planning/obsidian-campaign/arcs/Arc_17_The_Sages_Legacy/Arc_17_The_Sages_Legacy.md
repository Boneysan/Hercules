## Synopsis
Arc 16 secures political force. Arc 17 asks whether force is enough. The royal
archives point the party toward Varmundt's Mansion, a place half legend and half
weapons platform, where the Sage studied [[Concepts/Ymir_Heart]], dimensional seams,
and the problem of fighting things that do not die cleanly.

The mansion is not a library with traps. It is an argument built out of artificial
worlds. Biospheres mimic ecosystems, Tartaros stores failed containment tools, and
the Corridor of Phantom turns memory into hostile terrain. Mansion AI Alpha can
help, but only in the way a damaged system helps: literally, selectively, and with
priorities that may not include the party's survival.

The Whisperers and mercenary scavengers arrive for the same reason the party does.
In the core, Beta or the Awakening Silva Papilia forces the party to win the right
to use Varmundt's most dangerous answer: stabilizers, weapon blueprints, and a
ritual framework that can re-forge a seal without repeating Thanatos's martyrdom.
The price is that the tools need power death can understand, pointing directly to
Himmelmez.

## Spaces (the symbolic geography)
- **Threshold / Separation:** finding the entrance to the hidden, floating mansion — stepping into a machine that has outlived its maker.
- **Dream-landscape / Initiation:** the artificial biospheres — fake worlds mimicking reality, proof that protection can become control.
- **Climax space:** the core research laboratory, where Varmundt's answer waits behind defenses that no longer know friend from intruder.

## The Beats
1. Cold open: a royal archive lead from Arc 16 names Varmundt as the last person who planned for Ragnarok.
2. Hook: breaching the mansion's automated defenses before the Whisperers do.
3. Rising dungeon: Biosphere → Corridor of Phantom → Tartaros Storage.
4. Turn: Varmundt foresaw this crisis, but his solution is dangerous enough to look like hubris.
5. MVP set-piece: Beta / Awakening Silva Papilia in the core lab.
6. Closing thread: the party emerge with tools to fight the end, but need a death-aspected power source (→ Arc 18).

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the mechanical means to re-forge, stabilize, or refuse the Thanatos road in Arc 19.
- **Pays off:** Arc 6's ancient science and Ymir-Heart thread; Arc 12's dimensional knowledge.
- **"What are you becoming?":** Varmundt tried to save the world by reducing it to systems. The party must use his work without becoming his method.
