# Quest — Corridor of Phantom Map

#custom #campaign #quest

> The Corridor of Phantom doesn't just hide Tartaros Storage — it turns memory into
> terrain, replaying the mansion's worst incidents as hostile ground. Mansion AI Alpha
> won't map it for anyone; that would mean admitting some of those memories are its own.
> Captain Vale's teams are mapping it anyway, one triggered trap at a time.

- **Type:** scouting / map quest
- **Level band:** 192–214
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Mansion_AI_Alpha]] (a map that respects the Corridor's
  defenses) / [[../NPCs/Captain_Rusk_Vale]]'s salvage team (wants routes bought outright)
- **XP role:** the arc's approach-scouting loop through the Corridor of Phantom, directly
  gating safer access to Tartaros Storage and [[05_Tartaros_Dive]]
- **iRO source:** Episode 17.1/17.2 questlines; Illusion Investigation daily framing per
  the arc's iRO source map

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Corridor's entrance, where the mansion's ordinary architecture gives
  way to hallways that remember things that never quite finished happening.
- **Route:** phase by phase through the phantom-memory terrain, some replaying Varmundt's
  own failed experiments, some replaying incidents the party recognize from earlier arcs.
- **Hand-in:** either Alpha's control node or Vale's checkpoint, depending on who receives
  the finished map.

## Quest object
**Phantom-Phase Map** — a chart that only holds its shape while the Corridor's memory-loops
stay stable. Alpha warns that a map drawn from baited defenses will read as safe long after
it stops being true; a map drawn from patient observation holds.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each scouting pass: one navigation beat (honest Corridor-phase XP against replaying
  hostile memories), one mapping beat (chart a route by observation or by triggering
  defenses to see what fires), one Vale beat (does his team's parallel salvage run cross
  paths with the party's, and how).
- Routes mapped by careful observation hold up and grant genuinely **safer Tartaros
  access** — the reward the template itself names — directly benefiting
  [[05_Tartaros_Dive]]'s approach.
- Routes mapped by baiting defenses are faster to chart but leave triggered traps reset
  and waiting, a debt the main quest's approach will pay.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** map safe routes through patient, careful observation, leaving the Corridor's
   defenses untriggered — slowest scouting, and the only branch that produces a map still
   accurate by the time the main quest needs it.
2. **Pragmatic:** bait a portion of the Corridor's defenses deliberately to learn their
   patterns faster — quicker mapping, and a Corridor that's marginally more alert by the
   time the party return.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** sell the route data to Vale in exchange for salvage rights or
   intel on his operation — fast, valuable trade, and a mansion's memory-map now in the
   hands of a man who profits from exactly the kind of shortcut Alpha exists to prevent.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** scouting XP; Phantom-Phase Maps; safer Tartaros access (a concrete
  approach-difficulty modifier for [[05_Tartaros_Dive]]).
- **Narrative:** the party learn to read a mansion that remembers its own failures as
  vividly as it remembers its defenses.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the safer-access modifier for [[05_Tartaros_Dive]]; a data point in whether Alpha or Vale ends up trusting the party more by the main quest's climax.
- **Pays off:** Alpha's founding caution — that knowledge here is a tool, a prize, or a thing that must sometimes stay locked, tested directly by how the party choose to map it.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party earn safe passage through patience, or buy it by feeding a scavenger's operation.

## GM notes
- Run this in parallel with the Biosphere Sample Rotation; both quests' outcomes should
  combine to set the approach conditions for [[05_Tartaros_Dive]].
- If the party sell to Vale, let it recolor his later dialogue in the main quest — he
  should reference the map specifically, not just generically "help" from the party.
