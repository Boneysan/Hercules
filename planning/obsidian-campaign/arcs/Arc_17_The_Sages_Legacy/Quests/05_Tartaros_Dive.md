# Quest — Tartaros Dive

#custom #campaign #quest

> The party descend into storage hell to recover the component everyone wants and no one should use unexamined.

- **Type:** hook / bounty route (dungeon dive and arc climax setup)
- **Level band:** 190-215
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Mansion_AI_Alpha]] / [[../NPCs/Schwarzwald_Scientist]]
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Mercenary_Captain]], Beta / Awakening Silva Papilia
- **Frames:** Tartaros Storage and Corridor of Phantom as the practical high-risk route to Arc 19 tools.

## Spaces
- **Threshold / Separation:** the freight lift dropping below the mansion's safe permissions.
- **Dream-landscape / Initiation:** Tartaros Storage, where sealed inventions are stacked like sins in crates.
- **Climax space:** the containment aisle where Beta awakens around the stolen or recovered Core.

## Quest object
**The Stolen Core** — a god-wounding component removed from its stabilizer. It is powerful because it is incomplete.

## Challenge (condition -> consequence)
- If the party recover the Core from Vale without killing his crew, Ilse can rebuild its stabilizer.
- If they kill through the salvage team, the Core is intact but the mansion marks them as another hostile extraction force.
- If they destroy the Core, Arc 19 loses a weapon option but gains a safer seal-repair route.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** rescue trapped salvage workers first -> Vale may escape, but Arc 19 ally turnout improves.
2. **Pragmatic:** seize the Core first -> best weapon readiness, higher defense escalation.
3. **Dark:** bargain with Vale for forbidden inventory -> extra tools, fewer people willing to stand near the party later.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** Stolen Core or Stabilized Core, Beta set-piece resolved, Arc 19 weapon flag.
- **Narrative:** Varmundt's legacy becomes a question, not a prize.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the final means to wound Loki's certainty and survive Surt's threshold.
- **Pays off:** Arc 16's lesson that command without restraint becomes another crisis.
- **"What are you becoming?":** people who can walk out of an armory without becoming its next disaster.
