# Quest — Biosphere Sample Rotation

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Varmundt's Biosphere runs artificial ecosystems that don't know their maker is dead —
> they keep cycling, keep producing, keep testing whatever wanders in. Ilse Rechter needs
> live samples to calibrate the mansion's most dangerous tools without guessing, and
> guessing, this close to the end, isn't a mistake anyone survives twice.

- **Type:** repeatable research loop
- **Level band:** 190–212
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Schwarzwald_Scientist]] (Doctor Ilse Rechter — wants
  calibration data) / [[../NPCs/Mansion_AI_Alpha]] (wants proof the samples serve
  protection, not extraction)
- **XP role:** the arc's core research-clearing loop through Varmundt's Biosphere,
  feeding the Safe Calibration tally toward the Re-forged Seal ending
- **iRO source:** Episode 17.1 (Illusion) daily framing; Varmundt-style dailies per the
  arc's iRO source map

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Biosphere's outer containment ring, where the ecosystem's artificial
  weather still cycles on a schedule its maker set decades ago.
- **Route:** biome to biome, collecting live samples from whatever the mansion's systems
  are currently testing — some hostile, some simply strange.
- **Hand-in:** Ilse's temporary lab camp, where the Living Sample Case gets read against
  the stabilizer equations she's trying to solve.

## Quest object
**Living Sample Case** — a containment box that keeps its contents alive, barely, long
enough to reach Ilse's instruments. What's inside changes every rotation: a stabilized
spore, a fragment of something that shouldn't still be growing, once, unmistakably, a
piece of dimensional tissue that reads on her instruments exactly like the Arc 6 Yuno
data.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each rotation: one biome-clearing beat (honest research XP against the Biosphere's
  automated defenses), one collection beat (preserve a sample intact or harvest it fast
  and rough), one readings beat (report the calibration data honestly or shade it toward
  what Ilse wants to hear).
- Samples preserved and readings reported honestly build the **Safe Calibration tally**
  the quest hub calls out directly — the mechanical foundation of the Re-forged Seal
  ending in Arc 19.
- Samples harvested for rare components sell well and speed up Ilse's timeline, but push
  her calibration toward power over precision.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** preserve every sample intact, reporting calibration data exactly as
   measured even when it's discouraging — slowest research pace, and the branch that
   builds the Safe Calibration tally honestly.
2. **Pragmatic:** harvest rare components alongside sample collection to fund the
   expedition's other needs — balanced pace, partial calibration accuracy.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** falsify safe readings to speed the mansion's weapon research past
   Alpha's caution — fast progress toward a usable tool, and a Re-forged Seal that will
   work with a hidden, unaccounted cost the GM can cash in during Arc 19.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** research XP; Living Sample Cases; the Safe Calibration tally that keys
  directly into the [[../../Choice_Tracker]] flag "Varmundt Tools Stabilized."
- **Narrative:** the party learn Varmundt's mansion is still doing exactly what he built
  it to do — testing whether the people who arrive can be trusted with what it protects.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Safe Calibration tally that determines the **Varmundt Tools Stabilized** flag (see [[../../Choice_Tracker]]) and shapes the Re-forged Seal ending.
- **Pays off:** the Arc 6 Yuno dimensional-measurement thread, now yielding usable data instead of just dread.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's research serves precision and safety, or just gets to "done" fastest.
