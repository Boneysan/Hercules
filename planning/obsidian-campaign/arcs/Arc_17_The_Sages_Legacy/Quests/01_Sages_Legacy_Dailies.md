# Quest — Sage's Legacy Dailies

#custom #campaign #quest

> Repeated endgame tasks become frantic research shifts: stabilize, retrieve, decide what must not be used.

- **Type:** side (repeatable preparation loop with a moral counter)
- **Level band:** 190-215
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Schwarzwald_Scientist]]
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Mansion_AI_Alpha]], salvage crews, injured researchers
- **Frames:** Episode 17+ daily quests as research operations toward the finale.

## Spaces
- **Threshold / Separation:** Ilse's field lab board, where each daily is written as a hypothesis.
- **Dream-landscape / Initiation:** rotating mansion sectors: Biosphere samples, storage hazards, corridor phantoms.
- **Climax space:** the calibration table where every recovered item is judged as weapon, shield, or forbidden thing.

## Quest object
**The Rift Lens** — a calibrated instrument showing whether a recovered component stabilizes a seal, pierces one, or does both.

## Challenge (condition -> consequence)
- Completing dailies as written builds the Safe Calibration tally.
- Taking shortcuts through overloaded sectors builds the Raw Power tally.
- If Raw Power exceeds Safe Calibration, Arc 19 weapon use becomes stronger but more dangerous.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** prioritize rescue and stabilization tasks -> more allies and safer endings.
2. **Pragmatic:** prioritize components -> faster Arc 19 weapon readiness.
3. **Dark:** recover forbidden notes -> unlocks a severe anti-god option with Catechism consequences.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** repeatable EXP framing, Safe Calibration / Raw Power tally, rift-stabilizer components.
- **Narrative:** preparation becomes visible work rather than abstract downtime.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** Arc 19 ending modifiers tied to how the weapon was built.
- **Pays off:** the campaign's repeated question of whether method matters when stakes are catastrophic.
- **"What are you becoming?":** researchers, scavengers, or people building a better apocalypse.
