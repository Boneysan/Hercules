# Quest — Illusion Investigation

#custom #campaign #quest

> The party enter a fake world built to test whether they can be trusted with tools real enough to wound a god.

- **Type:** main (threshold, Biosphere investigation, Beta telegraph)
- **Level band:** 190-215
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Mansion_AI_Alpha]]
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Schwarzwald_Scientist]], [[../NPCs/Mercenary_Captain]]
- **Frames:** Varmundt's Biosphere and illusion systems as meaningful dungeon space, not just endgame grind.

## Spaces
- **Threshold / Separation:** the mansion airlock, where Alpha strips the party's assumptions before opening the first simulated ecosystem.
- **Dream-landscape / Initiation:** artificial biospheres that imitate Midgard badly: forests with no decay, oceans with no tides, cities with no citizens.
- **Climax space:** the observation core where Beta's purge routines first speak through the lab.

## Quest object
**The Access Lattice** — a permission key that opens deeper doors only after the party logs what they preserved, not just what they killed.

## Challenge (condition -> consequence)
- Cataloging living systems before clearing monsters unlocks stabilizer data.
- Speed-clearing the sectors unlocks weapon components but damages the mansion's trust score.
- If Vale steals a Stolen Core during the run, Beta wakes early in [[Tartaros_Dive]].

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** preserve the test ecosystems -> stronger Arc 19 stabilizer option, slower component gain.
2. **Pragmatic:** harvest what is needed -> weapon path advances, Alpha restricts later permissions.
3. **Dark:** weaponize the simulation failures -> highest damage data, but the Rift Lens records the party as a risk.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** Access Lattice, first rift-stabilizer data, unlocked Tartaros route.
- **Narrative:** the party learn that Varmundt's tests judge restraint as much as victory.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Arc 19 re-forging tools.
- **Pays off:** Arc 6's Yuno measurements and Arc 12's crossed rift.
- **"What are you becoming?":** people who can use power without stripping the world for parts.
