# Mansion AI Alpha — The House That Tests Guests

#custom #campaign #npc

> Varmundt's surviving gate intelligence, still enforcing admission rules for a master who is gone.

- **Arc:** 17 — The Sage's Legacy
- **Role:** quest-giver / threshold guardian
- **Location:** Varmundt's Mansion entry systems, Biosphere control nodes
- **Propp function:** blocker and donor — Alpha denies access until the party proves they are not looters, then grants controlled permissions.

## What it knows
- Which labs contain Ymir Heart research, rift stabilizers, and anti-divine weapon prototypes.
- That Varmundt predicted a seal cascade but distrusted anyone desperate enough to use his solution.
- That Beta has rewritten parts of the mansion's defense logic.

## What it wants
Continuation of protocol: protect the mansion from thieves, protect Midgard from the mansion, and determine whether the party are a lesser catastrophe.

## Dialogue hooks
1. **Noble:** "We need to save the world." -> Alpha asks for proof of saved lives, not intent.
2. **Pragmatic:** "Give us the weapon and name your condition." -> Alpha opens the fastest but most dangerous route.
3. **Dark:** "We can force the door." -> Alpha lets them try and records the attempt as evidence.

## Quest Design Role
- **Quest-giver:** opens [[../Quests/03_Illusion_Investigation]] and grades its outcome.
- **Symbolic object:** the **Access Lattice**, a key that opens only when the party accepts limits.
- **Choice pressure:** whether to treat knowledge as a tool, a prize, or a thing that must sometimes stay locked.

## GM notes
Alpha should feel alien but fair. It is not cruel; it is what caution looks like after centuries without sleep.
