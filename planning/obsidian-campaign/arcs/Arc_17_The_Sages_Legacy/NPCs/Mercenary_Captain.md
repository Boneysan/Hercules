# Captain Rusk Vale — Mansion Salvage Commander

#custom #campaign #npc

> A professional scavenger who has decided the end of the world is a procurement opportunity.

- **Arc:** 17 — The Sage's Legacy
- **Role:** human villain / rival expedition leader
- **Location:** Tartaros Storage, Corridor of Phantom, stolen lab checkpoints
- **Propp function:** thief and trap-setter — he steals components, reroutes defenses, and leaves other people to trigger the mansion's punishments.

## What he knows
- Which artifacts buyers want and which ones are too dangerous to sell honestly.
- How to bait Beta into purging a room after his team has already escaped.
- That the party need components he can hold hostage.

## What he wants
To leave the mansion rich, alive, and able to claim he merely did what everyone else was too sentimental to do.

## Dialogue hooks
1. **Noble:** "People will die if you strip those systems." -> "People are dying already. I'm just not dying poor."
2. **Pragmatic:** "Sell us the parts." -> he names a price: abandon a trapped rival team or trade a future favor.
3. **Dark:** "Work for us." -> he will, if the party proves they are colder than he is.

## Quest Design Role
- **Human villain:** makes the arc's moral pressure mortal instead of purely mechanical.
- **Symbolic object:** the **Stolen Core**, a weapon component removed from its stabilizer housing.
- **Branch price:** recovering the Core intact may mean sparing Vale; destroying his operation may cost the weapon's cleanest configuration.

## GM notes
Vale should not be grand. He is the everyday face of apocalypse capitalism: practical, funny, and awful.
