# Doctor Ilse Rechter — Schwarzwald Field Scientist

#custom #campaign #npc

> A Republic researcher who came to loot Varmundt's Mansion and stayed because she understood what the data meant.

- **Arc:** 17 — The Sage's Legacy
- **Role:** ally / mentor
- **Location:** temporary lab camp near the Biosphere entrance
- **Propp function:** helper and interpreter — she turns impossible machinery into usable objectives and warns what each shortcut costs.

## What she knows
- How Arc 6's Yuno readings match Varmundt's old rift equations.
- Which daily operations can be reframed as scavenging power cells, stabilizer lenses, and safe routes.
- That the mansion's weapon can harm a divine architect but might widen the same cracks it targets.

## What she wants
To prove Schwarzwald science can repair what Schwarzwald ambition helped break.

## Dialogue hooks
1. **Noble:** "No weapon that kills the world to save it." -> she prioritizes stabilizers over raw damage.
2. **Pragmatic:** "What works fastest?" -> she marks the Tartaros route and warns it will wake Beta early.
3. **Dark:** "What did Varmundt hide?" -> she shows the sealed notes and asks whether the party really wants a god's wound in mortal hands.

## Quest Design Role
- **Ally / mentor:** grounds the arc in understandable stakes.
- **Symbolic object:** the **Rift Lens**, a focusing tool that reveals whether power stabilizes or tears.
- **Throughline:** pays off Yuno's measurements and turns them into Arc 19 survival mechanics.

## GM notes
Ilse is a scientist with a conscience, not a pure exposition device. Give her firm opinions and let the party challenge them.
