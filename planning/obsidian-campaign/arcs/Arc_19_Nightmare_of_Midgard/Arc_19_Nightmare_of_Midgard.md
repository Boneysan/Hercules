## Synopsis
Morroc breaks in the open. The campaign's oldest dread stops being foreshadowing
and becomes geography: a crater, a ruined house-key, refugees with nowhere left to
return, and Wounded Morroc walking where the city used to be. The party stand where
Thanatos stood, but not as one doomed hero. They arrive with allies, tools,
compromises, debts, and choices.

The first movement is the Morroc disaster itself: ally turnout, refugee survival,
Satan Morroc's release, and the terrible recognition that killing the field form
will not be enough. The second movement goes beyond the veil through every locked
endgame depth the campaign has promised: Glast Heim Abyss, Ancient Odin's Temple,
Einbroch Mine F3, and the Ash Vacuum torn fully open.

There Loki finally steps on-page. The Voice was not madness, not a demon, and not a
metaphor. It was a god authoring a script in which every hero becomes a tool and
every seal becomes kindling. The final fight against Satan Morroc and Surt is
therefore not only about power. It is about authorship: whether the party repeats
Thanatos's road, improves it, bargains around it, or refuses the story entirely.

## The Central Choice
Frame the finale around the **Thanatos dilemma, with options he never had.** The
available endings are gated by the campaign's running tallies:
- **The Catechism tally** ([[../../Act_I_World_at_Peace/Arc_04_City_Above_the_Beast/Quests/05_The_Pilgrims_Offer]]) — did the party take the cult's seal-knowledge? It may be the only thing that lets them *re-forge* a seal instead of dying inside one.
- **Thanatos's Last Word / Mirror-Shard** (Arc 15) — did they understand the old road well enough to avoid simply repeating it?
- **Royal legitimacy and stabilizer integrity** (Arcs 16–17) — did they secure an army and a working technical answer?
- **The Himmelmez bargain** (Arc 18) — did they make an ally of death?
- **Allies kept or lost** (Wynne, Mara, Elsbeth, Sun-Hwa, Holt, Mira, Echo...) — who stands with them at the end.

### Ending Gates
1. **The Shared Seal** — unlocked by Thanatos's Last Word, high ally turnout, and Echo or another living proof of refusal. No single martyr carries the seal; the party and allies distribute the cost.
2. **The Re-forged Seal** — unlocked by the Catechism tally, the Mirror-Shard, and Arc 17 stabilizer work. No one dies, but the cure carries cult-derived knowledge the party must own publicly.
3. **The Queen's Bargain** — unlocked by bargaining with Himmelmez in Arc 18 and facing the dead honestly. Death itself helps bind the world-ending fire, but Niflheim gains a negotiated claim afterward.
4. **Thanatos's Road** — available if the party lack trust, tools, or allies. Someone can still become the seal alone. It works, and that is the tragedy.
5. **The Refusal** — unlocked by living proof the road has an off-ramp (Echo saved in Arc 10, the Fragments owned in Arc 15). The party decline to end the story the way it was written — no seal, no martyr, no bargain. What survives is uncertain, but it is *theirs*, and Loki stops laughing.
6. **Ragnarok Unbound** — failure state if the party arrive with power but no restraint. Surt wakes fully; the campaign ends by deciding what, if anything, survives the fire.

## Throughline (everything closes)
- **Pays off:** the sleeping demon (Arc 3) breaks; the shaft opens; Loki is revealed (Voice, since Arc 1); Thanatos's method (Arc 15) becomes the party's option; "what are you becoming?" (Arcs 10/15/18) **answered by the choice.**
- **Plants nothing new:** any sequel seed should come from the ending the players choose, not from a new villain reveal.
