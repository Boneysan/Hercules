# Satan Morroc — Unbound Field Form

#custom #campaign #npc #boss

> The ancient terror beneath Morroc, finally released into the world the party tried to prepare.

- **Arc:** 19 — Nightmare of Midgard + Beyond the Veil
- **Role:** demon boss / living payoff of the oldest campaign dread
- **Location:** Morroc Ruins, Arc 3 shaft, Dimensional Gap threshold
- **Propp function:** attacker and threshold guardian — his escape destroys the old map and opens the road beyond it.

## What he represents
Satan Morroc is not the architect. He is the catastrophe Loki arranged everyone around. Fighting him is not the campaign's answer; it is the door to the answer.

## What he knows
- The shape of Thanatos's old seal from the inside.
- The taste of every weakened seal feeding the cascade.
- That Loki's plan needs the party exhausted, powerful, and standing in exactly the wrong place.

## Quest Design Role
- **Boss face:** anchors [[../Quests/04_Where_Thanatos_Stood]].
- **Symbolic object:** the **Black Seal Shard**, a broken piece of the prison beneath Morroc.
- **Choice pressure:** defeat is possible; containment is the question.

## GM notes
Do not let him explain the plot. His presence should make Arc 3's rumor physically true: the thing beneath the city was always real, and now it is walking.
