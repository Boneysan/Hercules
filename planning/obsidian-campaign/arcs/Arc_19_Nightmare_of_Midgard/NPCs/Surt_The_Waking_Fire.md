# Surt — The Waking Fire

#custom #campaign #npc #boss

> The world-ending fire at the far edge of Loki's design.

- **Arc:** 19 — Nightmare of Midgard + Beyond the Veil
- **Role:** final threat / Ragnarok pressure
- **Location:** Ash Vacuum, beyond the torn dimensional walls, mirrored by Thor's Volcano
- **Propp function:** looming attacker — not subtle, not social, not corruptible. Surt is what happens if every delay fails.

## What he represents
Surt is the literal answer to Act III's fire: the end state of the cascade if no one changes the story. He is less a schemer than a horizon.

## What he knows
Almost nothing useful to mortals. He knows burning, waking, and the pull of a world whose seals have become kindling.

## Quest Design Role
- **Final pressure:** gives [[../Quests/05_The_Choice_He_Never_Had]] urgency.
- **Symbolic object:** the **Undying Cinder** from Arc 14, now flaring in response.
- **Choice pressure:** the party are not choosing in a quiet room; they are choosing while the world catches.

## GM notes
Use Surt sparingly. Loki is the argument; Satan Morroc is the released disaster; Surt is the clock striking midnight.
