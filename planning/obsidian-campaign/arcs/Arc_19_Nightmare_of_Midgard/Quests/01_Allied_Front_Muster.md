# Quest — Allied Front Muster

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Every faction the party has touched since the Prontera fountain gets one message,
> carried by riders, airships, and whatever's left of the Sapha border network: the
> shaft is opening. Who answers isn't decided today. It was decided across eighteen
> arcs, and today is just when the answer arrives — or doesn't.

- **Type:** finale ally-turnout quest
- **Level band:** 220–250+
- **Quest-giver:** the war effort itself — coordinated through whichever allies the
  party kept: [[../../Arc_16_The_Royal_Banquet/NPCs/Royal_Guard_Commander]],
  [[../../Arc_16_The_Royal_Banquet/NPCs/Kronecker_G_Heine]], and any surviving faces from
  Arcs 1–18
- **XP role:** the arc's pre-climax logistics loop, converting every campaign-long
  flag on [[../../Choice_Tracker]] into physical bodies on the field before
  [[04_Where_Thanatos_Stood]]
- **iRO source:** Continental Guard Quest (per the arc's iRO source map); finale
  mobilization framing

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the war-room map table, where pins for Hugel, Prontera, the New World,
  and every surviving ally get placed — or stay in the box.
- **Route:** the muster routes themselves, escorting messengers and matching promised
  support against what actually arrives, town by town.
- **Hand-in:** the staging ground outside the Arc 3 shaft, where the final headcount gets
  read aloud before the seal breaks.

## Quest object
**War-Council Thread** — a length of cord the war-room table uses to physically connect
pins: Hugel to Prontera, Prontera to the New World post, each thread representing a
promise kept or broken across the whole campaign. By the muster's end, the table looks
like a web or like wreckage, depending on how the party spent eighteen arcs.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each muster run: one logistics beat (honest finale-tier XP escorting messengers and
  supply through contested ground), one accounting beat (does a promised faction's
  support actually arrive, and in what state), one deployment beat (where arriving allies
  get positioned before the seal breaks).
- Allies deployed safely and given clear roles survive to matter at
  [[04_Where_Thanatos_Stood]] and beyond — directly building toward the **Shared Seal**
  ending gate's high-ally-turnout requirement (see [[../../Choice_Tracker]]).
- Allies deployed aggressively for maximum immediate impact hit harder up front but take
  losses that thin the turnout available for the Choice itself.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** deploy every arriving ally safely, prioritizing their survival over
   immediate battlefield impact — slowest to maximize force now, and the branch that
   preserves the largest living turnout for the Choice.
2. **Pragmatic:** deploy allies aggressively where their skills matter most, accepting
   calculated losses for a stronger initial front against Wounded Morroc — a harder,
   faster fight, with a leaner turnout afterward.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** hold the strongest allies back entirely for evacuation duty,
   treating the muster as triage rather than a battle line — the safest option for named
   allies, and it means the party face Wounded Morroc with less immediate support than
   they mustered.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** finale-tier XP; War-Council Threads; direct **ending-gate modifiers**
  feeding the Shared Seal, Re-forged Seal, and Refusal thresholds on
  [[../../Choice_Tracker]].
- **Narrative:** the party watch the entire campaign's relationships convert, all at once,
  into who's actually standing beside them when the ground opens.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the concrete ally-turnout number that [[04_Where_Thanatos_Stood]] reads aloud and [[05_The_Choice_He_Never_Had]] gates its endings on.
- **Pays off:** every ally-trust flag built since Arc 1 — Wynne's contracts, Aelith's trust, Kronecker's army, Eadric's faith, all of it, tallied at once.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's eighteen arcs of choices built a web that holds, or a scatter of broken threads.

## GM notes
- This is the mechanical payoff quest for the entire campaign's Choice Tracker — read the
  full flag table before running it and stage the muster's turnout accordingly.
- Don't resolve the Choice here; this quest only tallies who arrives. The decision itself
  belongs to [[05_The_Choice_He_Never_Had]].
