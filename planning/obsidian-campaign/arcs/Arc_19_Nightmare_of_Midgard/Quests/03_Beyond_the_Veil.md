# Quest — Beyond the Veil

#custom #campaign #quest

> The rifts tear fully open. The party have already walked the New World — now they go
> *beyond* it, through the Dimensional Gap into every locked depth the campaign only let
> them glimpse. Each was a door shut in their faces once. They open every one, and at
> the end of them all, the Voice is finally waiting to be seen.

- **Type:** main (the finale's second movement — the endgame set-pieces & the Loki reveal)
- **Level band:** 200–250+
- **Quest-giver:** the open rift / [[../NPCs/Loki_The_Voice]] (drawing them in)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Loki_The_Voice]]; returning allies and foils
- **Set-piece grounds:** Glast Heim Abyss · Prontera Underground Prison · Ancient Odin's
  Temple (inner sanctum) · Einbroch Mine F3 · Varmundt's Biosphere

## Hook (Separation)
With Satan Morroc contained but the seal undecided, the dimensional walls — dissolving since
Arc 9, crossed in Arc 12 — tear fully open. The threshold is the Dimensional Gap itself: the
party step *beyond the world* to confront the architect. Every depth they were turned away
from across the campaign is now a stage on the road to the Voice.

## Spaces (each a payoff of a glimpsed depth)
- **Glast Heim Abyss** — beneath the cursed kingdom (Arc 8); the rot's true bottom.
- **Prontera Underground Prison** — under the first city (Arc 1); the cascade's beginning,
  reached from its end.
- **Ancient Odin's Temple (inner sanctum)** — the divine deep glimpsed in Arc 11; heaven's failing heart.
- **Einbroch Mine F3** — the industrial depth Rekenber used and abandoned (Arc 7); the
  factory's grave.
- **Varmundt's Biosphere** — the narrative *final room;* monsters take only 10% damage, so treat
  it as the threshold of the Choice regardless of mechanical outcome.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **A campaign in five rooms:** each set-piece re-tests a theme — GH Abyss (institutional rot),
  the Prison (where it began), Odin's Temple (failing gods), Mine F3 (manufactured violence),
  the Biosphere (the unwinnable room). The party move through their *own* journey, inverted, to
  reach Loki.
- **The Voice resolves into a person:** every seed (Arcs 1, 3, 11, 13) is collected and Loki is
  *seen.* The confrontation is not a damage race — see his
  [[../NPCs/Loki_The_Voice|dialogue]]. He is unmade only by a story that refuses to end the way
  he wrote it: Thanatos's lesson, Echo's refusal, the allies the party kept.
- **The accumulated campaign is the weapon:** the Mirror-Shard (Arc 15), the New World alliance
  (Arc 12), [[../../../Recurring_Cast#Echo — The One Who Said No (recurring ally / mirror, Acts II-IV)|Echo]] saved (Arc 10), a faced grief (Arc 18), Thanatos's Last Word (Arc 15) — each
  is a mechanical and narrative counter to Loki's certainty.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**The Whole Reliquary** — here the party's entire collection of campaign objects *resonates as
one:* the Sigil Ring (Arc 1), the Keystone Shard (Arc 4), Vahl's Readout (Arc 6), the Falling
Feather (Arc 11), the undying Cinder (Arc 14), Thanatos's Last Word (Arc 15), the Adventurer's
Pin (Arc 16). Separately they were clues; together, held up before Loki, they are the *proof of
a journey he authored but could not control.* The campaign's every object becomes one key — and
the lock is the architect's belief that the party were only ever his instrument.

## Choices & branches
- **Confront Loki with the party's earned story** → his certainty cracks; the finale's true
  Choice (next quest) opens with its full range, including the endings Thanatos never had.
- **Confront him with only power** → he is delighted; the party can wound the world's ending but
  not its *author,* and the Choice narrows toward sacrifice. Loki wins by being agreed with.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** the endgame depths cleared; the Whole Reliquary resonance; Loki revealed and
  destabilized; the Choice unlocked at full breadth.
- **Narrative:** the architect unmasked — **the Voice was Loki** — and the cascade reframed one
  final time: it was never fate, never the cult, never the demons. It was a story, and the party
  are about to decide its last line.

## Throughline
- **Pays off:** every Voice seed; every glimpsed-but-locked depth; every campaign object; the
  whole cascade's authorship.
- **Plants:** the Choice (next quest) at full range.
- **"What are you becoming?":** revealed as Loki's question all along — and answered by refusing his.

## GM scripting notes
- If the server cap is 175, **compress to a single depth + the Biosphere** and go straight to
  the Choice (per the arc hub).
- The Biosphere's 10%-damage rule is a *narrative* device — the party cannot "win" it by force,
  which is the point; the Choice, not the DPS, ends the campaign.
- Let the players assemble the Loki reveal from their own collected seeds wherever possible.
