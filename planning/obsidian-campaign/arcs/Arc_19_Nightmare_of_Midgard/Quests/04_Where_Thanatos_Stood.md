# Quest — Where Thanatos Stood

#custom #campaign #quest

> The seal under Morroc shatters. The city the party first met as refugees in Arc 1 is
> gone in an instant, and the thing that has slept beneath it since the God-Demon War
> walks free. The party stand on the exact spot where a hero once made an impossible
> choice alone. They are not alone. That is the only thing that has changed — and it
> may be enough.

- **Type:** main (the finale's first movement — the seal breaks)
- **Level band:** 200–250+
- **Quest-giver:** the cascade itself (no NPC; Morroc breaks and the party are *there*)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Satan_Morroc_Unbound]]; [[../NPCs/Loki_The_Voice]] (unseen, felt);
  [[../../Arc_15_The_Heros_Tomb/NPCs/Keeper_Lysandra]] (if she lives);
  [[../../../Recurring_Cast#Mira Ashkey — Morroc refugee / keeper of the house-key (recurring witness, Acts I-IV)|Mira Ashkey]] and [[../../../Recurring_Cast#Echo — The One Who Said No (recurring ally / mirror, Acts II-IV)|Echo]] if earned
- **MVP:** **Wounded Morroc → [[Characters/Demons/Satan_Morroc]]**

## Hook (Separation)
The Arc 3 shaft — the door the party stared at for fourteen arcs — finally opens, because the
seal above it fails. Morroc is obliterated; Wounded Morroc rises on the ruin-field. The
threshold is the most earned in the campaign: the party walk into the crater of the city
whose refugees they've followed since the fountain, onto the ground where Thanatos stood, to
do what he did — or to do it *differently.*

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the shattered seal / the open Arc 3 shaft — the campaign's oldest locked door, breached.
- **Initiation:** the Morroc Ruins field — Wounded Morroc's domain, the city as a wound.
- **Climax:** the seal-heart where Satan Morroc fully manifests, the spot of Thanatos's sacrifice.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **Wounded Morroc first, then the truth:** the field form (Wounded Morroc) is the gate; past
  it, Satan Morroc manifests *and* the question of *how to seal it again* arrives — the
  campaign's accumulated answers all become mechanics here:
  - **Thanatos's Last Word** (Arc 15): the party *can* seal it inside a person — but they know
    now they don't have to do it *alone,* which is the difference between his doom and a chance.
  - **The Pilgrim's Catechism** (Arc 4): if the party took the cult's seal-craft, they can
    *re-forge* the seal instead of sealing it in flesh — no martyr, but the cure bears the
    cult's fingerprints.
  - **Allies present** (Lysandra, Eadric, Aelith's New World, [[../../../Recurring_Cast#Echo — The One Who Said No (recurring ally / mirror, Acts II-IV)|Echo]] saved, even a bargained
    Himmelmez): each kept relationship is a hand on the seal — literally distributing the weight
    Thanatos carried alone.
- This quest *sets up* the Choice (next quest); it does not resolve it. The party must survive
  Satan Morroc's manifestation to *reach* the decision.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**The Refugees' Key** — among Morroc's ruins, the party find a house-key dropped in the first
Arc-1 exodus, carried all this way by someone who hoped to go home. The home is gone; the key
remains. It is the campaign's opening (refugees fleeing a trembling city) closing its loop in
the party's palm — proof of exactly what is at stake, and who has always paid for it. Everything
the party have fought has been, in the end, about whether anyone gets to go home.

## Choices & branches (a price — the full Choice is next quest)
- This movement's fork is *who stands with the party* when Satan Morroc rises — every ally kept
  or lost across 16 arcs reports here (or doesn't). The turnout is the mechanical and emotional
  measure of the whole campaign, and it determines which endings the next quest can offer.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** Wounded Morroc / Satan Morroc engaged; the Refugees' Key; the finale's ally
  turnout tallied; access to the Choice.
- **Narrative:** the campaign's oldest dread realized — and the revelation, mid-fight, that the
  party stand where Thanatos stood with the one thing he never had: company.

## Throughline
- **Pays off:** the sleeping demon (Arc 3); the shaft; Thanatos's lesson (Arc 15); every ally
  thread; the refugees (Arc 1).
- **Plants:** the Choice (next quest); the felt presence of the Voice (→ Loki reveal).
- **"What are you becoming?":** the party reach the exact moment that broke Thanatos — and find
  out what they've become *together.*

## GM scripting notes
- Stage the ally turnout explicitly — name who shows up because the party earned them. This is
  the campaign's accounting, made visible.
- Do **not** resolve the seal here; this fight ends with Satan Morroc *contained but not
  decided* — the party have bought the moment to choose, which is the next quest.
