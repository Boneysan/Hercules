# Quest — Ragnarok Aftermath Seeds

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Whatever ending the party chose at the Choice, there is a morning after it. Ash
> settles. Records burn or survive. People who lived through the God-Demon War's
> second coming need to know what to plant, literally and otherwise, in ground that
> just finished being a battlefield.

- **Type:** epilogue / survival loop
- **Level band:** 230–250+
- **Quest-giver:** whichever allies and institutions survived the Choice in
  [[05_The_Choice_He_Never_Had]] — Kronecker's war council, Lysandra's archive,
  Aelith's New World envoys, or their successors if they didn't
- **XP role:** the campaign's final downtime loop, run after the finale to establish
  what the world looks like once the story the party told is the one that stuck
- **iRO source:** Crow of Destiny; Ash Vacuum / New World follow-ups (per the arc's iRO
  source map, epilogue framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the edge of whatever's left — Morroc's crater, the Ash Vacuum's
  settling edge, or the ground the party's chosen ending actually leaves behind.
- **Route:** the aftermath itself, salvaging records, rescuing survivors, and
  recovering equipment from a world figuring out what "after" means.
- **Hand-in:** wherever the survivors are regrouping — Prontera, Hugel, or a new
  settlement, depending on the ending chosen.

## Quest object
**Green Shoot in Ash** — a single plant pushing up through scorched ground, found by
the party or planted deliberately, depending on the branch. Whether it means anything
is exactly the question the epilogue is built to ask.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each pass: one recovery beat (honest post-finale XP salvaging the aftermath), one
  priority beat (records, people, or weapons — what gets saved first), one legacy beat
  (does this action read as building toward a future or just cataloguing an ending).
- Passes that prioritize saving people and records build the **post-campaign hooks** the
  template names directly — concrete threads a GM can spin into a sequel or epilogue
  campaign.
- Passes that prioritize saving weapons "for what comes after" leave the world better
  armed and its story less resolved — an ending that reads as a pause, not a close.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** save records — recover the campaign's own history: Lysandra's archive,
   Quill's names, Ilse's stabilizer notes, whatever survived — slowest, and it's the
   branch that ensures the next generation actually knows what happened and why.
2. **Pragmatic:** save people — prioritize survivors over artifacts, rebuilding
   communities before institutions — a warmer, more grounded aftermath with less
   preserved knowledge.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** save weapons and dangerous tools "for what comes after" — a
   colder legacy, useful if the GM intends a sequel built on unfinished threats, and a
   pointed final answer to "what are you becoming?" for a party still arming for the
   next fight.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** epilogue XP; Green Shoots in Ash; explicit **post-campaign hooks** for
  whatever the table wants to do next.
- **Narrative:** the party get to decide, literally with their own hands, what kind of
  world their choice actually built.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** whatever sequel or epilogue material the table wants — this is the one quest in the campaign explicitly designed to open new threads rather than close them.
- **Pays off:** the entire campaign's throughline checklist (see [[../../Tabletop_Campaign_Overview]]) — this is where every earlier plant either bears fruit or doesn't.
- **What are you becoming?:** the campaign's last answer — not what the party fought, but what they chose to leave standing.

## GM notes
- Run this only after [[05_The_Choice_He_Never_Had]] resolves; its content should be
  built entirely around whichever ending gate the table landed on.
- This is a good place to let each player narrate one thing their character does now
  that the fighting (mostly) stops — treat it as a genuine epilogue scene, not just a
  mechanical wrap-up.
