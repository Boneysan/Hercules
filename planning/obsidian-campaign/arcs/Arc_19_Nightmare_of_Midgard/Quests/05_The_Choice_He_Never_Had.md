# Quest — The Choice He Never Had

#custom #campaign #quest

> Everything the campaign asked, every choice tallied, every ally kept and dead failed,
> comes down to one room and one decision. Thanatos stood here and chose alone, and it
> destroyed him. The party stand here together, and they choose with sixteen arcs of
> who-they've-become in their hands. This is the last line of the story. Loki wrote
> every line before it. He does not get to write this one.

- **Type:** main — **the campaign's finale.** Pure decision; the fights are behind them.
- **Level band:** 200–250+ (the culmination; no grinding — this is roleplay)
- **Present:** [[../NPCs/Loki_The_Voice]]; [[Characters/Demons/Satan_Morroc]] (contained);
  [[Characters/Demons/Surt]] (stirring); every surviving ally and foil
- **Stakes:** the seal, the world, and what the party have become

## Hook (Separation)
Satan Morroc is contained but not resolved; Surt stirs beneath; Loki waits, delighted, to see
how his instrument ends his story. The threshold is internal and final — there is nothing left
to fight, only to *decide.* The party stand on Thanatos's spot with his warning in hand:
*"I thought a hero had to carry it alone. That was the demon's first lie."*

## The Choice (gated by the whole campaign)
The available endings are **set by the party's accumulated record** — this is where every tally
pays out. The GM offers the endings the party have *earned,* not a free menu:

- **Martyrdom (Thanatos's road).** A party member seals the demon inside themselves. It works.
  It costs that person everything — but, *unlike* Thanatos, they do it *chosen and witnessed,*
  not alone in despair. Always available; the campaign never forecloses sacrifice. The
  difference from his doom is the company.
- **The Re-Forging (the Catechism road).** If the party took the cult's seal-craft (Arc 4) and
  faced what it made of them (Arc 15), they can *re-forge* the seal — no martyr. The price: the
  cure bears the cult's fingerprints, and the party must live knowing they ended the cascade
  with the cascade's own tools. Loki finds this *very* funny, which is how the party know it costs.
- **The Distributed Seal (the allies road).** If the party kept their relationships — Lysandra,
  Eadric, Aelith's New World, a saved Echo, the survivors of every arc — the weight Thanatos
  bore alone is *shared* across many hands. No one is destroyed because no one carries it all.
  The literal mechanical embodiment of his lesson; available only to a party that didn't burn
  its bridges.
- **The Bargain (the Himmelmez road).** If the party allied with the witch (Arc 18), her power
  over death can *hold* what cannot be sealed — ruling the demon rather than imprisoning it. A
  survival with a shadow on it: the road Himmelmez walks stays open, and the party have used it.
- **The Refusal (the Loki road).** The party decline to end the story the way it was written —
  refusing both sacrifice and the demon's terms. The hardest, and only possible if the party
  *proved the road has an off-ramp* (Echo saved, Arc 10; the Fragments owned, Arc 15). Loki
  stops laughing. What survives is uncertain — but it is *theirs.*

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**The Mirror-Shard, Held Up** (Arc 15) — at the decision, each party member looks into the
assembled mirror and sees the sum of their road. It shows not what they *are* but what they
*chose,* and it is the last thing they consult before they answer. The campaign's question —
*what are you becoming?* — is finally answered not by the GM, not by Loki, but by each player,
looking into the object they built from their own honesty, and deciding the last line out loud.

> **The failure state — Ragnarok Unbound.** If the party arrive with power but no restraint —
> allies lost, dark shortcuts compounded, no proof of refusal — none of the roads above are
> *offered.* Surt wakes fully, and the last session decides what, if anything, survives the
> fire. This is not an ending the GM presents; it is what happens when nothing else was earned.

## How it resolves
- **There is no single correct ending.** Each road is a valid, earned answer to the campaign's
  question — sacrifice, ingenuity, fellowship, dangerous alliance, or defiant refusal. The GM's
  job is to make sure the *price* of whichever road is honored, and that **Loki's certainty —
  that the party were only ever his instrument — is what truly loses,** whichever ending they choose.
- **Cassell's last line.** Whatever the party chose at the Pilgrim's Offer sixteen arcs ago,
  give Cassell (or his memory) one final word here — the human face of the cascade, closing its
  eyes. He is sincere to the end.

## Throughline (everything closes)
- **Pays off:** Thanatos (Arc 15); the Catechism (Arc 4); every ally and the New World alliance
  (Arcs 12, 17); Echo (Arc 10); Himmelmez (Arc 18); the Voice → Loki; the refugees and Wynne's
  ledger (Arc 1); the whole sixteen-arc tally of who the party became.
- **"What are you becoming?":** answered — by the party, out loud, in the last line of the story
  Loki couldn't finish.

## GM scripting notes
- **Run this as pure roleplay.** No dice decide the ending; the campaign already rolled them,
  arc by arc. The GM's only mechanic is honoring the price.
- Offer only the endings the party *earned.* A party that burned every bridge may have only
  Martyrdom and the Bargain; a party that walked clean and kept faith has the full range. That
  constraint *is* the campaign's verdict, and it should feel like one.
- End on the chosen road's image, then on Cassell's last line, then on the Mirror — each player
  saying who they became. Then close the book.
