# Bijou — The Cult Infiltrator

#custom #campaign #npc #boss

> The assassin in court dress: a Whisperer-made weapon wearing the shape of royal necessity.

- **Arc:** 16 — The Royal Banquet
- **Role:** human villain / arc MVP face
- **Location:** Royal Banquet floor, Underground Prison ritual chamber, inner castle vault
- **Propp function:** attacker, thief, and blocker — she kills claimants, steals command seals, and blocks the army from mobilizing.

## What she knows
- Which nobles can be baited into betraying each other.
- How to open the prison cells beneath the castle as a ritual circuit.
- That Prontera's army is not just a military force; it is one of the last collective powers Loki must prevent from acting together.

## What she wants
To make the kingdom choose legitimacy over survival, then destroy both.

## Dialogue hooks
1. **Noble:** "You murdered guests under truce." -> "Truce is only a word powerful people use between betrayals."
2. **Pragmatic:** "Who hired you?" -> "Everyone did, in pieces. Fear is a generous patron."
3. **Dark:** "Teach us how to make courts obey." -> she offers a method: remove the people who hesitate.

## Quest Design Role
- **Human villain:** gives the MVP fight a face and motive.
- **Symbolic object:** the **Bloodied Seating Card**, showing each assassination as a place setting.
- **Branch price:** killing her quickly preserves command but buries evidence; taking her alive exposes the court rot but costs lives during the delay.

## GM notes
Bijou should feel like the court's own logic made flesh. She is not an outside monster invading politics; she is politics with the mask removed.
