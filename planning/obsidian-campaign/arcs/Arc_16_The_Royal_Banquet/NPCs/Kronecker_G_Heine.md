# Kronecker G. Heine — The Spare Prince

#custom #campaign #npc

> A royal heir with enough claim to matter and enough conscience to be dangerous.

- **Arc:** 16 — The Royal Banquet
- **Role:** ally / political mentor
- **Location:** Royal Banquet, guest apartments, war council chamber
- **Propp function:** helper who can be lost — he translates court politics into actionable stakes and can secure the army if the party keeps him alive and honest.

## What he knows
- Which royal families are using the crisis for advantage.
- The old castle routes into the Underground Prison.
- The location of the royal war seals needed to mobilize Prontera's forces.

## What he wants
To prevent the throne from becoming a prize people murder for while the world ends outside the windows.

## Dialogue hooks
1. **Noble:** "The crown exists to protect people." -> he risks his claim to back evacuation and open mobilization.
2. **Pragmatic:** "Pick the faction that can win." -> he offers a fast military route, but weaker houses will remember being abandoned.
3. **Dark:** "Give us blackmail." -> he supplies it; the party gain leverage and lose his trust.

## Quest Design Role
- **Ally / mentor:** anchors the banquet investigation in human stakes.
- **Symbolic object:** the **Unworn Crown Seal**, proof that authority can be refused instead of seized.
- **Throughline:** previews the Arc 19 answer to Thanatos: power does not have to be carried alone or possessed forever.

## GM notes
Kronecker is not naive. He understands compromise; what he fears is becoming the kind of ruler who only understands compromise.
