# Commander Elric Valdemar — Royal Guard Commander

#custom #campaign #npc

> The officer holding Prontera's chain of command together while the Seven Families turn the palace into a battlefield.

- **Arc:** 16 — The Royal Banquet
- **Role:** quest-giver / military ally
- **Location:** Prontera Castle guard wing, later the Underground Prison checkpoint
- **Propp function:** donor and dispatcher — he grants access, troops, and legal cover, but only if the party proves they are not another faction's knife.

## What he knows
- Which royal houses can actually muster soldiers before Ragnarok reaches the capital.
- That the prison doors below the castle have been opened from the inside.
- That Bijou's faction is not merely political; someone is using court etiquette as an assassination map.

## What he wants
A kingdom that can still give orders tomorrow. Elric does not care who wins the banquet arguments if the army fractures before Morroc breaks.

## Dialogue hooks
1. **Noble:** "Put us where people need saving." -> Elric assigns civilian protection and opens the cleaner alliance route.
2. **Pragmatic:** "Who controls the troops?" -> he shares guard rosters and expects the party to preserve command assets.
3. **Suspicious:** "Which noble is lying?" -> he gives access to sealed arrest records, but the party owe him a public favor later.

## Quest Design Role
- **Quest-giver:** starts [[../Quests/05_The_Royal_Banquet]] and authorizes [[../Quests/03_Underground_Prison_Cleanup]].
- **Symbolic object:** the **Red Command Pin**, a small authority token that later resonates in Arc 19's Whole Reliquary.
- **Choice pressure:** protecting the monarchy's legitimacy can conflict with exposing its corruption.

## GM notes
Keep him competent. The pressure of Arc 16 should come from impossible tradeoffs, not from the guard being foolish.
