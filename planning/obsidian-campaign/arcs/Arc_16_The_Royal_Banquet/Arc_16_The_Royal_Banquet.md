## Synopsis
The party return from Thanatos Tower with the knowledge of what the finale may cost,
only to find Prontera tearing itself apart. The Seven Royal Families are arguing
succession while the city needs a war cabinet. Refugees crowd the gates, old allies
ask for impossible priorities, and the Royal Guard no longer knows which orders are
real.

The Royal Banquet turns courtly procedure into a battlefield. Audience rounds,
relief rotations, and prison cleanups are not side errands; they are how the party
builds or loses legitimacy. Kronecker G. Heine can become the bridge between the
party and the crown, but only if they prove they are not another armed faction
using the crisis as leverage.

Bijou's attack reveals the Whisperers' Act IV strategy: collapse the kingdom from
within before it can stand in Arc 19. Defeating her does not solve Prontera's
politics, but it gives the party what they came for: a functional Royal Guard,
royal authorization, and the first pillar of the Allied Front.

## Spaces (the symbolic geography)
- **Threshold / Separation:** the glittering halls of the Royal Banquet — a room that pretends procedure can keep apocalypse outside.
- **Dream-landscape / Initiation:** the Prontera Underground Prison — the kingdom's buried truth, now crawling upward.
- **Climax space:** the banquet hall / inner castle vaults, where noble blood and cult work become indistinguishable.

## The Beats
1. Cold open: returning to Prontera to find the city gripped by succession rumors.
2. Hook: an assassination attempt during the Royal Banquet.
3. Rising dungeon: relief work → royal audiences → Underground Prison investigation.
4. Turn: a major royal faction is unknowingly backed by the Whisperers.
5. MVP set-piece: Bijou turns the banquet into a slaughterhouse.
6. Closing thread: the Rune-Midgard army joins the final war, or arrives fractured if the party handled court badly.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Allied Front pillar; the need for god-killing tools (→ Arc 17).
- **Pays off:** Arc 1's Prontera cult seed — from sewer sigil to throne-room blood.
- **"What are you becoming?":** the party can save a nation by becoming power brokers. What lines do they cross to secure an army?
