# Quest — Invaded Prontera Relief

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Prontera's streets are still Prontera's streets, even with the Seven Families circling
> the throne like it's already vacant. Commander Elric Valdemar needs bodies on the walls
> that aren't wearing a house's colors — because the moment relief work looks partisan,
> it stops being relief.

- **Type:** repeatable city defense
- **Level band:** 180–200
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Royal_Guard_Commander]] (neutral city defense) / a noble
  house steward (wants relief work to visibly favor their family's district)
- **XP role:** the arc's field-clearing loop through Invaded Prontera, holding the line
  between succession politics and an actually undefended city
- **iRO source:** Episode 16.1 (Banquet for Heroes) daily framing; city-defense bounty
  structure

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the city gate watch-post, where relief assignments get posted faster
  than they can be filled.
- **Route:** the invaded districts themselves, defending civilians, armories, and royal
  symbols against whatever's pressing in while the court argues upstairs.
- **Hand-in:** Elric's checkpoint, where the day's relief writ gets logged against the
  Royal Guard's own dwindling roster.

## Quest object
**Smoke-Stained Relief Writ** — a defense assignment scorched at one corner, proof the
district it names was already burning when the party arrived. Elric keeps every writ; he
says a commander who doesn't track his own losses stops being able to give honest orders.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each relief run: one city-defense beat (honest field XP against the invasion pressure),
  one priority beat (civilians, armories, or royal symbols — which gets the party's
  attention first), one neutrality beat (does the run stay visibly non-partisan, or does
  it read as favoring one house).
- Relief runs that consistently protect civilians first build the **Arc 19 army quality**
  the arc hub names directly — a Prontera that remembers who defended it without asking
  whose colors they wore.
- Relief runs that favor armories or royal symbols keep the Guard combat-ready faster, but
  read, to the watching city, as choosing sides before the succession is settled.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** defend civilians first, every time, regardless of district or house —
   slowest to secure hard military assets, and the clearest single signal of the party's
   neutrality that Elric can point to.
2. **Pragmatic:** defend armories and supply lines — keeps the Guard armed and functional,
   a defensible priority that reads as competent rather than compassionate.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** defend royal symbols and house banners preferentially — fast
   favor with whichever family notices, and a relief record that looks, on paper, like
   the party already picked a side.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** city-defense XP; Smoke-Stained Relief Writs; an Arc 19 army-quality
  modifier.
- **Narrative:** the party learn that in a kingdom eating itself, "defend the city" is
  never as neutral an order as it sounds.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the army-quality modifier that [[04_Terra_Gloria]] and [[05_The_Royal_Banquet]] both draw on for Prontera's Arc 19 turnout.
- **Pays off:** Elric's founding worry — a kingdom that can't give a coherent order tomorrow — answered relief run by relief run.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's neutrality is a real principle or just the pose that plays best with the Guard.

## GM notes
- Run this as the arc's opening downtime loop, before the Royal House Audience Rounds
  raise the political stakes explicitly.
- Track the civilian/armory/symbol ratio loosely — it should surface as a specific,
  nameable detail in how Elric vouches for the party in [[04_Terra_Gloria]].
