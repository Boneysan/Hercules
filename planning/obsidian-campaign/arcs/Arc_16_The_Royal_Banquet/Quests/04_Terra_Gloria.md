# Quest — Terra Gloria

#custom #campaign #quest

> The party turn a broken succession crisis into a military compact strong enough to matter when Morroc breaks.

- **Type:** main (arc climax and army-securing resolution)
- **Level band:** 180-205
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Kronecker_G_Heine]] / [[../NPCs/Royal_Guard_Commander]]
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Bijou]], surviving royal delegates, Royal Guard captains
- **Frames:** Episode 16.2 political aftermath as the practical work of preparing for Ragnarok.

## Spaces
- **Threshold / Separation:** the war room after the banquet, stripped of ceremony.
- **Dream-landscape / Initiation:** Invaded Prontera and the prison-vault routes, where civic symbols become tactical objectives.
- **Climax space:** the inner vault holding the royal war seals, with Bijou blocking mobilization.

## Quest object
**The Unworn Crown Seal** — Kronecker's proof that command can be entrusted rather than seized. It completes the Red Command Pin and Prison Ledger as Arc 16's authority triad.

## Challenge (condition -> consequence)
- If the party preserved the Pin, Ledger, and Crown Seal, Prontera joins Arc 19 as a unified force.
- If one is missing, the army arrives divided or late.
- If Bijou dies before naming her collaborators, mobilization is fast but corrupt nobles remain embedded.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** public compact among the houses -> highest legitimacy, slower mobilization.
2. **Pragmatic:** emergency command under Elric -> fastest army, future succession crisis.
3. **Dark:** blackmail the houses into unity -> full force now, brittle trust in Arc 19.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** Prontera army ally flag; war-seal access; Bijou set-piece resolved.
- **Narrative:** the capital becomes more than a place to defend; it becomes a force that can defend others.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the need for Arc 17's god-killing tools; an army alone cannot fight Loki or Surt.
- **Pays off:** Arc 1's first guild oath at the fountain, now scaled into a kingdom's oath.
- **"What are you becoming?":** commanders who can give orders without becoming kings.
