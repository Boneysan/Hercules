# Quest — Royal House Audience Rounds

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Each of the Seven Families wants a private audience with "the adventurers who keep
> saving cities" before they'll commit a single soldier to anyone's cause. Kronecker
> calls it theater. He's not wrong. He also can't secure an army without someone sitting
> through every act of it.

- **Type:** social XP / faction loop
- **Level band:** 182–204
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Kronecker_G_Heine]] (wants every house heard honestly) / a
  rival house's steward (wants their family's audience prioritized)
- **XP role:** downtime social/faction loop through the castle's guest apartments and
  audience halls, feeding directly into the mobilization stakes of [[04_Terra_Gloria]]
- **iRO source:** Episode 16.1 (Banquet for Heroes) political framing; Terra Gloria
  daily-quest structure

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the guest-apartment antechamber, where a steward checks the party's
  names against a list that changes depending on which house is currently ahead.
- **Route:** house to house, hearing grievances, veiled threats, and genuine fear dressed
  as diplomatic language.
- **Hand-in:** Kronecker's war council chamber, where every audience gets weighed against
  the others.

## Quest object
**Wax House Token** — a seal pressed in colored wax, one per family, handed to the party
after each audience as proof of having been heard. Kronecker collects them quietly; a
full set, he says, is the only honest measure of whether Prontera can still act as one
kingdom.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each round: one social-navigation beat (honest audience XP reading a house's real
  position), one priority beat (whose audience gets scheduled first this week), one
  leverage beat (a house offers something — information, a bribe, a threat — in exchange
  for favor).
- Rounds that hear every house in turn, however tedious, build the full set of Wax
  Tokens and the **mobilization modifier** the arc hub names directly — Prontera's army
  arrives coherent rather than fractured.
- Rounds that back one house early move faster and secure a strong single ally, but the
  unheard families remember exactly who the party chose over them.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** hear every house in full, refusing to shortcut the process even under
   pressure — slowest, most exhausting, and the only branch that completes the full set
   of Wax Tokens before [[04_Terra_Gloria]].
2. **Pragmatic:** back the one house most capable of mustering real troops quickly —
   fast, functional army, and the weaker houses remember being skipped.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** expose each house's private bargains against the others as
   leverage — powerful information, immediate advantage, and a court that now knows the
   party collects secrets as readily as any noble does.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** social XP; Wax House Tokens; a mobilization modifier feeding
  [[04_Terra_Gloria]]'s army-quality outcome.
- **Narrative:** the party learn that securing an army for the end of the world runs
  through the same tedious, petty diplomacy as securing anything else.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the mobilization modifier that determines whether Prontera arrives at [[../../Act_IV_Ragnarok/Arc_19_Nightmare_of_Midgard/Quests/01_Allied_Front_Muster]] unified or fractured.
- **Pays off:** Kronecker's founding fear — a throne people murder for while the world ends outside the windows — answered one heard grievance at a time.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party's diplomacy serves the kingdom or just whichever house made the better offer this week.

## GM notes
- Run this in parallel with Invaded Prontera Relief; the two quests' modifiers should
  combine cleanly before [[04_Terra_Gloria]] resolves the mobilization outcome.
- Let at least one house's grievance turn out to be legitimate and one turn out to be a
  cover for cult influence — don't make every noble either purely sympathetic or purely
  corrupt.
