# Priest Eadric

#custom #campaign #npc

> The senior priest of Hugel's shrine to Odin — a kind, unglamorous man who has spent
> forty years tending a faith he never doubted, right up until the Valkyries he prayed
> to started killing his parishioners. He still lights the candles every morning. His
> hands shake now. He doesn't know whether he's lighting them for the gods or against them.

- **Role:** **ally / quest-giver and mentor** — the party's interpreter of a *divine* crisis;
  the human face of "the gods are failing too."
- **Location:** the Odin shrine at Hugel; the temple approach to Abyss Lake.
- **Propp function (donor):** provides the temple's old wards, the rites that *should* calm a
  Valkyrie, and a consecrated token that lets the party parley with the dying one.

## What he wants
To understand why heaven turned on him without losing the part of himself that loved it. He
is not looking for vengeance on the gods — he's looking for a way to grieve them while
they're still, technically, alive. He wants the party to bring him *truth,* even a terrible one.

## What he knows
- The Valkyries' wards have **inverted** — the temple that welcomed the faithful now repels
  them; the divine order is coming apart the same way the seals are.
- A feather was "harvested" from a Valkyrie and shipped south (the Arc 10 telegraph paid off —
  Rekenber was *mining the gods* too).
- The rite to approach a Valkyrie without being struck down — and that one of them, Sigrun, is
  *dying* and lucid, and has been trying to speak.

## Dialogue hooks (three doors)
1. *Faith kept gently:* "Help us understand them, Father." → full rites, wards, the parley
   token; he comes as far as the temple steps and gives the party their best chance to *reach*
   a Valkyrie rather than only fight her.
2. *Pragmatic:* "Just tell us how to survive them." → the combat wards only; the party can
   fight Valkyries but not *talk* to one (they miss the Loki seed Sigrun carries).
3. *Bitter at heaven:* "Your gods are murderers. Why defend them?" → he flinches, helps less,
   and quietly breaks a little — a faith dying without last rites, which the party caused.

## Theme tie-in
Eadric is the campaign's most tender "what are you becoming?" — not asked of the party's
power, but of their *capacity to grieve.* In an act about losing (the gods, the world, the
race against the cascade), he models the hard thing: loving something as it fails without
turning the love to poison. The counter to every zealot the party have met.

## GM notes / secrets
- His parley token is the gate to the dying Valkyrie Sigrun and the first *explicit* Loki
  tell — reward the *faith kept* door with it.
- Eadric is a strong Act IV presence: a priest of Odin standing beside the party when Loki is
  unmasked (Arc 19) lands hard. Keep him alive.
