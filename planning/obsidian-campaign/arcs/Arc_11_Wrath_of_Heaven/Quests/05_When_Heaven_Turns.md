# Quest — When Heaven Turns

#custom #campaign #quest

> The Valkyries of Odin's temple have started killing the people who pray to them. It
> isn't punishment and it isn't a test — it's the divine order coming apart at the same
> seams as everything else. The gods aren't angry. They're *failing.* And that's worse.

- **Type:** main (arc climax)
- **Level band:** 130–155
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Priest_Eadric]] / Wynne (contract of record)
- **Key NPCs:** [[../NPCs/Zealot_Bjorn]]; **Valkyrie Sigrun** (the dying one — see side quest)
- **MVP:** **Valkyrie Randgris** (+ Detardeurus in Abyss Lake)

## Hook (Separation)
Eadric's inverted wards and Vahl's needle (Arc 6) agree on the impossible: the *temple
itself* is now a hazard, and the harvested feather (Arc 10) proves even heaven is being
mined. The threshold is the temple steps — sacred ground that no longer wants the faithful,
choked with Bjorn's flock marching in to be judged. To cross is to stop believing the gods
will save anyone, including the party.

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Odin temple steps at Hugel — consecrated ground turned lethal.
- **Initiation:** Abyss Lake 1–3 — a drowned dragon-deep where even divine order has gone
  feral; the trial space says *the structures above are sinking into the structures below.*
- **Climax:** the Valkyrie's hall, where Randgris — heaven's own guardian — attacks the
  living without malice, the way a falling wall does.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **Clear the steps first:** Bjorn's flock blocks the temple. Talking Bjorn down (his
  *compassion* door) empties the steps peacefully; otherwise the party wade through martyrs
  who want to die — and arrive at Randgris already stained and depleted.
- **Eadric's rite vs. the fight:** with the *faith-kept* door, the party can perform the
  calming rite to *stagger* Randgris (she's not evil, she's malfunctioning — the rite
  reaches the dying order in her); without it, it's a pure DPS race against a failing god.
- **Detardeurus in the deep:** the Abyss Lake dragon guards the approach; bait it apart from
  the Valkyrie or face both as heaven and the deep close in together.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**A Valkyrie's Falling Feather** — not harvested by a knife like Rekenber's, but one that
simply *fell,* because the wing it belonged to is coming apart. Still faintly warm with
divinity, it *cools* in the hand over the arc — a god dimming in real time. Where Act I's
Sigil Ring was the cult's cold mark and Act II's Readout was a human number, this is the
first object that proves *the sacred itself is mortal.* The party carry a piece of a dying
heaven, and it asks the campaign's question upward: *if even gods unravel, what protects you?*

## Choices & branches (a price)
- **Lay Randgris to rest (with the rite)** → the party grant a failing god a clean death
  rather than a butchering; the temple quiets, Eadric can grieve, and the party learn the
  divine order is *finite* — sobering, and it costs them the last comfort that someone
  bigger is coming to help.
- **Cut her down as a monster** → faster, and it treats a god like any other MVP — but the
  party harden a notch, and Eadric (and the faithful) see them desecrate the dying. A quiet
  Catechism tick: the party are getting *used* to killing sacred things.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** Valkyrie Randgris (+ Detardeurus) clear; Abyss Lake leveling to ~155; the
  Falling Feather; Abyss Lake bounty/Gramps overlap.
- **Narrative:** "the gods are failing too" — the stakes leap from *demons escaping* to
  *the whole order of the world dissolving.* And a dying Valkyrie has been trying to speak.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the gods' failure → Arc 19 (Loki, Ragnarok); the Sigrun parley → the first explicit Voice/Loki tell.
- **Pays off:** the harvested feather (Arc 10) — Rekenber mined heaven too; the cascade as *total,* not just demonic.
- **"What are you becoming?":** the party get *comfortable* killing gods — note who flinches and who doesn't.
