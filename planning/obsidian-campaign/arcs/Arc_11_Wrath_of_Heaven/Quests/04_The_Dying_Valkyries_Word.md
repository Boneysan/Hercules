# Quest — The Dying Valkyrie's Word

#custom #campaign #quest

> One Valkyrie is not attacking. She is dying, slowly and lucidly, in a side-chapel of
> the failing temple, and she has been trying to say something for three days. Reach her
> before she goes, and she will tell you who has been laughing in the dark of heaven.

- **Type:** side — the arc's quiet revelation; the first *explicit* Loki tell
- **Level band:** 125–150
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Priest_Eadric]]
- **Key NPC:** **Valkyrie Sigrun** (dying, lucid, divine)

## Hook (Separation)
Eadric's parley token (his *faith-kept* door) lets the party approach a Valkyrie without
being struck down — and one of them, **Sigrun**, has been waiting for someone who could
hear her. The threshold is reverent, not violent: the party set down their weapons and
*kneel* to a dying god, which is its own kind of crossing for adventurers who've learned to
solve everything with a sword.

## Spaces
- **Initiation:** the temple's side-chapels — the quiet rooms behind the slaughter, where a
  goddess is failing in private.
- **Reveal space:** Sigrun's chapel: a dying immortal, her light guttering, the air *cold*
  the way the Falling Feather is cold.

## Challenge (conditions → consequences)
- **A fight you must *not* win by force:** Sigrun is dying; harming her ends the quest with
  silence. The "challenge" is *keeping her lucid and present* long enough to speak — the
  party use Eadric's rites, the parley token, and their own honesty to anchor a fading mind.
- **She answers questions, costingly:** each truth she gives dims her further. The party must
  *choose what to ask* — about the Valkyries, the seals, or the thing she keeps returning to:
  *a laughter in the order that should not be there.* A real triage of curiosity against mercy.

## Quest item / Object (symbolic)
**Sigrun's Last Word** — not an item but a *spoken* relic the party carry forward: she names
the wrongness in heaven as **a Voice that laughs** — deniably divine, undeniably *gleeful* —
and dies before she can name it [[Characters/Gods/Loki]]. (If the party kept the Arc 1
"Voice" seeds, the recognition is theirs to make.) For a prop, leave them her **broken
sword-shard,** which hums faintly *only* when that laughter is near — a divine cousin of the
Sigil Ring, tuned to the architect himself.

## Choices & branches (a price)
- **Ask her about the Voice (spend her on the truth)** → the party get the campaign's first
  hard confirmation that something is *steering* the cascade from inside the divine order —
  the Loki thread, almost named — at the cost of using a dying god's last lucidity on intel
  instead of comfort.
- **Comfort her instead (let her go in peace)** → the party gain no intel, but Eadric — and
  the campaign — mark them as people who chose mercy over advantage when no one was scoring.
  The sword-shard still comes to them; some kindnesses are repaid anyway.

## Reward (Return)
- **Mechanical:** Sigrun's sword-shard (a Loki-proximity compass for Acts III–IV); minor EXP.
- **Narrative:** the **Voice made explicit** — heaven itself confirms an intelligence behind
  the cascade. The Arc 19 reveal now has a divine witness.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Voice → confirmed as steering heaven → Arc 19 (Loki unmasked).
- **Pays off:** every "Voice" seed since Arc 1 (Holt, Sabra, the manifest's "voice that pays
  in certainty") — now a dying goddess says it too.
- **"What are you becoming?":** ask-for-intel vs. comfort-the-dying — measured when no reward rides on it.

## GM scripting notes
- This is a pure roleplay set-piece; resist combat. The tension is the party rationing a
  dying mind's last minutes between *what they need to know* and *what she needs to hear.*
- The unspoken name is the point — let the players say "Loki" themselves if they've tracked
  the Voice. Don't say it for them.
