# Quest — Hugel Airship Muster

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Hugel's airship dock was built for pilgrims and cargo. Since the Valkyries turned,
> it's the only fast way in or out of a town that heaven might strike again without
> warning. Eadric wants the party working the manifest — because who gets a seat on
> the next lift is the kind of choice the temple used to leave to prayer.

- **Type:** escort / logistics quest
- **Level band:** 112–140
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Priest_Eadric]] (the temple's evacuation effort) / Hugel's
  militia captain (wants the docks running for supply, not just people)
- **XP role:** the arc's escort-and-clear loop around the airship dock approach, bridging
  Hugel's town streets into the Manuk Field 03 fringe
- **iRO source:** Hugel quests; Abyss Lake Entrance Guide (access/logistics framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the airship dock gate, where a militia clerk checks names against a
  manifest that's shorter every day than the line waiting behind it.
- **Route:** the dock approach and the Manuk-fringe roads, escorting cargo and passengers
  past whatever's drifted closer since the last Valkyrie strike.
- **Hand-in:** the dock office, where Eadric or the militia captain signs off on the day's
  manifest.

## Quest object
**Stamped Airship Pass** — a boarding chit stamped by whichever authority filled that
seat: temple, militia, or a name the party wrote in themselves. Every stamp is a small
record of who Hugel decided mattered that day.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each muster: one escort-clearing beat (honest field XP protecting the approach), one
  manifest beat (the seats never match the need — who gets bumped), one witness beat
  (a passenger's story about what they saw the Valkyries do).
- Manifests run to protect the most vulnerable build Hugel's trust and feed directly into
  the mobilization credit the arc hub notes carries toward Act IV.
- Manifests run for cargo or coin move faster and keep the docks funded, but the vulnerable
  wait longer for a lift out of a town heaven might strike again.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** move refugees first — the frightened, the hurt, the ones with nowhere else
   to go. Slowest through the manifest, and the muster becomes the thing Hugel remembers
   the party for.
2. **Pragmatic:** move priests and clergy first — keeps the temple's rites and Eadric's
   own effort functioning, at the cost of the ordinary line waiting longer.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** move weapons and supplies first — arms the militia against another
   Valkyrie descent fastest, and leaves civilians standing on the dock watching crates
   board ahead of them.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** escort XP; Stamped Airship Passes; an Act IV mobilization credit (the
  arc hub's own term for how ready Hugel is to answer the call in Arc 19).
- **Narrative:** the party learn how a town organizes hope when the thing it prayed to
  might be the next threat.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Act IV mobilization credit that colors Hugel's turnout in [[../../Act_IV_Ragnarok/Arc_19_Nightmare_of_Midgard/Quests/01_Allied_Front_Muster]].
- **Pays off:** Eadric's quiet grief made practical — a priest who can't fix heaven's wrath can still get people onto a ship.
- **What are you becoming?:** whose names the party write into the manifest, and whose they let wait.

## GM notes
- Run this as the arc's opening downtime loop, before the party descend fully into Abyss
  Lake; it establishes Hugel's civilian stakes early.
- Track the manifest choices lightly — they should surface again, unannounced, as Hugel's
  Arc 19 turnout.
