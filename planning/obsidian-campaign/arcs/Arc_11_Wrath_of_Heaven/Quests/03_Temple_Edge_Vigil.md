# Quest — Temple Edge Vigil

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Someone has to watch the temple's outer wards through the night, in case a Valkyrie
> descends again without warning. Eadric can't ask his own thinning congregation to do it
> — not after the last vigil ended with an empty chair at breakfast. He's asking the
> party instead.

- **Type:** watch / stealth patrol
- **Level band:** 115–150
- **Quest-giver:** [[../NPCs/Priest_Eadric]] (a proper vigil) / [[../NPCs/Zealot_Bjorn]]'s
  flock (want the vigil to fail, so the wrath can find them)
- **XP role:** the arc's night-patrol loop along the temple's outer wards, at the edge of
  Outside El Dicastes (Dolomedes — Fiber Lock, fast hits)
- **iRO source:** Odin's Temple Excavation Quest; Hugel quests (watch/vigil framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the temple's outer ward-line, where the consecrated ground visibly frays
  at the edges.
- **Route:** the ward perimeter through the night, watching for both hostile field
  monsters drifting in from El Dicastes' outskirts and Bjorn's flock trying to slip past
  the watch to "meet their judgment."
- **Hand-in:** the temple steps at dawn, where Eadric reads the night's report against
  whichever wards held.

## Quest object
**Fallen Temple Feather** — a single Valkyrie feather, found burned along its spine in the
same pattern Eadric's readers found on the dragon scales. It doesn't glow. It doesn't
warm. It's the closest the party come, this arc, to touching something divine and finding
it simply broken.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each vigil: one patrol beat (honest ward-line clearing), one ward beat (a fraying point
  found and reinforced or left), one Bjorn beat (does the flock try to slip through
  tonight, and how the party handle it).
- Vigils held with prayer and patience keep the wards intact and quietly weaken Bjorn's
  narrative that heaven wants martyrs — fewer of his flock try to breach the line each
  week.
- Vigils run purely as scouting patrols gather intelligence faster but leave the wards no
  stronger, and Bjorn's flock keeps finding gaps.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** pray the full vigil rite at each fraying point, however long it takes —
   slowest patrol, strongest wards, and it visibly costs Bjorn's flock their opening.
2. **Pragmatic:** scout the ward-line efficiently, reinforcing only the worst breaks —
   standard patrol pace, partial ward strength, useful intelligence on the corruption's
   spread.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** bait a weakened section to draw a hostile Valkyrie into the open
   for study — effective intelligence on what's happening to them, and a deliberately
   thinned ward that risks exactly the kind of descent Eadric is trying to prevent.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** patrol XP; Fallen Temple Feathers; a "Loki laughter" clue — the arc hub's
  own term for evidence pointing toward [[04_The_Dying_Valkyries_Word]].
- **Narrative:** the party hear, for the first time in their own patrol reports, something
  that isn't quite divine and isn't quite mortal, laughing where the wards are thinnest.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the Loki laughter clue that primes [[04_The_Dying_Valkyries_Word]]'s explicit Voice confirmation; a direct counter-pressure against [[../NPCs/Zealot_Bjorn]]'s recruitment.
- **Pays off:** Eadric's fear that the wards aren't malfunctioning — they're being unmade on purpose.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party hold a vigil out of faith, duty, or curiosity — and whether Bjorn's flock can tell the difference from the wall.

## GM notes
- GM Note from the arc hub applies directly here: **do not say Loki's name for the
  players.** Whatever the laughter clue reveals, keep it deniable until Quest 04.
- Run this as a recurring night-loop parallel to the Dragon Scale Turn-In; let the two
  evidence threads (burn pattern, laughter clue) converge just before the main quest.
