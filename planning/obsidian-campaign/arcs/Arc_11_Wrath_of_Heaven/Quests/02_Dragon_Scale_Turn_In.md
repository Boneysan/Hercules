# Quest — Dragon Scale Turn-In

#custom #campaign #quest #xp-support

> Abyss Lake's Large Dragons have always shed scales worth a bounty. What's new is the
> burn pattern — scorched from the inside, like something divine passed through the beast
> and left without asking. Eadric wants every scale logged before the militia melts them
> down for scrap.

- **Type:** repeatable Abyss Lake bounty
- **Level band:** 113–148
- **Quest-giver:** Hugel's bounty board (standard coin) / [[../NPCs/Priest_Eadric]] (wants
  scales studied, not sold)
- **XP role:** the arc's core dungeon-clearing loop through Abyss Lake Dungeon 1–3 (Large
  Dragons — Sky Petite or Executioner Card; Dungeon Teleporter direct to F1)
- **iRO source:** Abyss Lake Entrance Guide; Bounty Board Hugel (dragon-scale framing)

## Spaces
- **Threshold:** the Abyss Lake teleporter platform, where the militia stamps every party
  in and counts every party back out.
- **Route:** floor by floor through the Large Dragon nests, the water itself running
  warmer the deeper the party go.
- **Hand-in:** either the bounty board's coin window or Eadric's study table, depending on
  where the scales end up.

## Quest object
**Cracked Dragon Scale** — a shed scale scorched through from beneath, not from any
weapon the party recognize. Eadric's readers match the burn to the same divine signature
found on the inverted temple wards — proof the corruption reaches all the way down into
Abyss Lake, not just the shrine above it.

## Challenge (condition → consequence)
- Each descent: one dragon-clearing beat (honest Large Dragon XP), one scale beat (turn it
  in for coin or hand it to Eadric for study), one pattern beat (does this floor's burn
  scarring match the last, or is it worsening).
- Scales turned over to Eadric build a floor-by-floor map of the corruption's spread —
  telegraphing Valkyrie Randgris's condition before the party ever reach her in
  [[05_When_Heaven_Turns]].
- Scales sold for bounty coin fund gear faster, but the pattern goes unstudied and the
  party meet Randgris with less warning of what they're walking into.

## Branching choice
1. **Noble:** turn in every scale to Eadric for study, refusing the bounty coin — slowest
   payout, but the party arrive at the climax already understanding what's wrong with the
   Valkyries.
2. **Pragmatic:** study the divine burns yourself between dives, splitting scales between
   Eadric and the bounty board — balanced reward, partial telegraphy.
3. **Suspicious/dark:** forge warding charms from the scales instead of turning any in —
   strong personal protection against the coming fight, and Eadric's map of the corruption
   stays incomplete.

## Reward
- **Mechanical:** Abyss Lake XP; Cracked Dragon Scales (coin, wards, or study data
  depending on branch); a telegraphy modifier for the Randgris fight the arc hub calls out
  by name.
- **Narrative:** the party confirm the corruption isn't isolated to the temple — it's
  soaked into the dungeon itself.

## Throughline
- **Plants:** the divine-burn pattern that telegraphs [[05_When_Heaven_Turns]]'s Randgris fight.
- **Pays off:** Eadric's fear that the wards' inversion isn't a local malfunction but something spreading.
- **What are you becoming?:** whether the party treat a dying god's symptoms as coin, curiosity, or a warning worth heeding.

## GM notes
- Run this as the primary leveling loop through Abyss Lake; four to six descents should
  carry the party to the main quest's level floor.
- However the branch falls, make sure at least some telegraphy reaches the table before
  Randgris — the fight should never feel like a total surprise.
