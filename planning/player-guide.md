# Player's Guide — Seal Cascade Campaign

Welcome to the Seal Cascade campaign! This guide tells you everything you need
to know to connect, create a character, and jump into a session.

---

## Getting Connected

Friends use the current private Korangar pack. The host supplies the pack and
setup instructions; [client distribution plan](../../korangar/docs/plans/friends-distribution.md)
tracks how it is built and shared.

### The Client

Ask the host for the latest Korangar Windows or macOS pack and follow its
`READ ME FIRST` instructions. The pack supplies the server address; do not
replace it with `127.0.0.1` unless you are hosting the server on your own PC.

### Creating Your Account

The GM will create an account for you before the first session. You'll receive
a username and password. Log in at the character select screen and create your
character there.

## Picking a Class

The campaign is balanced for a small group (3–6 players) working together. Any
class can work, but a party with some variety tends to have an easier time:

| Role | Good picks |
|------|-----------|
| Front-line / tank | Knight, Crusader, Swordsman |
| Healer / support | Priest, Sage, Acolyte |
| Magic damage | Wizard, High Wizard, Sage |
| Ranged / utility | Hunter, Rogue, Bard, Dancer |
| Melee burst | Assassin, Blacksmith |

No class is wrong. If you want to play something unusual, go for it — the DM
will make it work.

## What to Expect in a Session

1. **Gather in Prontera.** Before the session starts, meet up near the
   fountain. Look for the **Session Board** — it's a notice board near the
   Adventurers' Guild. It will tell you whether a session is active and which
   arc you're on.

2. **Form a party.** The GM will add everyone to a party before turning on
   campaign mode. Make sure you're in the party *before* the GM starts the
   session — this is important for quest tracking.

3. **The DM announces the start.** When the GM turns on campaign mode you'll
   see a server announcement. That's your cue to check the Session Board and
   look for the first story NPC the DM points you toward.

4. **Follow the quest markers.** Story NPCs can have quest icons above them.
   Use **Ctrl+Q** for the current quest log; next-exit minimap guidance is
   planned, so the DM may still need to name the next destination. Most of the
   adventure is driven by talking to story characters and making choices —
   combat happens when the DM decides it's time.

5. **Fight bosses as a group.** When a boss encounter begins the DM will give
   you a heads-up in the map chat. Bosses are spawned at specific spots the DM
   warps you to. Stay together, use skills, communicate.

6. **Collect rewards.** After major beats the DM distributes EXP and loot
   directly to the party. You don't need to pick anything up — it comes to you.

7. **Session end.** The DM will announce when the session is over. You can log
   out or keep chatting. Quest progress is saved automatically.

## The `@roll` Command

The campaign uses dice rolls for certain story moments (skill checks,
surprises, gambling with fate). Anyone can roll:

```
@roll 1d20        — roll one 20-sided die
@roll 2d6+3       — roll two 6-sided dice and add 3
@roll 4d10        — roll four 10-sided dice
```

The result is announced to everyone on the map. When the story calls for a
roll the DM will tell you what to roll and what the target is.

## The Seal Cascade — A Brief Overview (No Spoilers)

The world of Rune-Midgard is held together by a network of ancient seals —
invisible bindings that keep the oldest and largest things from spilling out
into the light. For three centuries those seals have been quietly decaying.

You are adventurers who stumble into the edge of that problem in Prontera and
find yourselves pulled, arc by arc, toward its center.

The campaign spans 19 arcs across four acts. You'll travel every major city in
Midgard and beyond. The choices you make — who you trust, who you spare, how
you resolve each confrontation — all carry forward. There are no throwaway
decisions. The ending is shaped by everything that came before it.

There are five ways the story can end. Only one of them is obvious.

## Quick Tips

- Free stat and skill reallocation at any level is the server's intended
  policy. The current **Reset skills** and **Reset stats** buttons are not yet
  reliable for ordinary players: their command names/permissions need a fix.
  For a one-point skill refund, `@refundskill <skill id>` is granted to
  players; refund dependent skills first. The priced Reset Girl NPC is inactive.
  Until the reset fix ships, ask the host if you need a full reset.
- Talk to every NPC the DM highlights. Story is usually in the dialogue, not
  just the quest log.
- The `@roll` command shows your result to the whole map. The DM may ask for a
  hidden roll on occasion — they'll tell you when.
- If you get disconnected during a session, just log back in. The DM can warp
  you back to the group with a single command.
- Don't stress about builds. The campaign is not a balance-tuned dungeon run.
  The DM adjusts encounters to the party.
- Ask questions in party chat. The DM is playing too and will answer out of
  character when needed.
